## Design decisions
- Structural signature: units of two or more words from `l -bracket` of the Apupiwe linearization:
  S (Cl0), REL, ADV (Adv, Manner), NP (NP/NPi/NPq/CN), AP. Same units = differ only in bare roots' roles.
- intended.tsv: exact match of units. precise=yes where the reference presents the form as the clear one or
  as fixing an attachment; subjectless verb-phrase examples are not precise (subject drop + role freedom
  give a fragment or role-shifted clause reading).
- Examples of grammar/number words only that don't parse are citations; 2.10 is outside the grammar;
  "…" and a trailing "," are filled with "wan obu".
- No separate existential: "there is" = ibe with no subject, one tree (QUESTIONS 1).
