**The outcomes lean regionally, but the clearest problems are unequal instruments and candidate selection—not unequal results by themselves.** N3 rejects population weighting; it does not require equal regional shares of vocabulary.

**Before closing the list, in priority order:**

1. **Repair concept matching and review the residual gaps.** Normalize synonym glosses with sense checks, rerun coverage, and inspect regional omissions for existing phrases, genuine missing concepts and corpus artifacts. Supplement missing child-vocabulary regions with independent evidence. Do not automatically turn every missing label into a root.
2. **Validate disputed boundary overrides against their actual ladder rung.** For modern-use overrides, document evidence from lumping languages; where evidence is inadequate, use the prescribed family-balanced fallback.
3. **Broaden loan nomination and check sampling sensitivity.** Seek candidates from regional sources and improve thin columns. The “4 of 7 regions” safeguard adds **nothing mathematically**: the three largest regions contain only ten languages, so every 12-language pass already spans four. Test alternative regional/family sampling before claiming the panel establishes cultural fairness.
4. **Equalize regional outcome totals: nothing.** No evidence warrants quotas, rejecting well-supported shared loans, or discarding the retained deep layer merely to balance counts.
