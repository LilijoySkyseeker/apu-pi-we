Text A (a folk tale)
A1. Long ago, Spider was hungry, but the rains had not come.
A2. He saw Turtle carrying a basket of yams to the river.
A3. "Friend," said Spider, "let me help you."
A4. Turtle was slow, so Spider carried the basket on his back.
A5. At the river, Spider ate half the yams while Turtle washed his hands.
A6. When Turtle came back, he saw the empty basket and laughed.
A7. "You are clever, Spider," he said, "but next year I will plant my yams where you cannot reach them."

Text B (a family's day)
B1. In the morning, my grandmother lights the fire and boils water for tea.
B2. My brother goes to the field with the goats.
B3. The sky is grey; it will probably rain.
B4. In the afternoon, the neighbours come to sit under the big tree and talk about the harvest.
B5. My grandmother says the rice is ready, but my uncle thinks we should wait three more days.
B6. At night we eat together and listen to the radio.
