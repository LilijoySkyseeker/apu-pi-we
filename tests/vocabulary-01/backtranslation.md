# Back-translation (blind reader)

Sources used: only /tmp/bt7/language.md, grammar.tsv, lexicon.tsv, compounds.tsv, numbers.tsv.

Two forms are in none of the reference files: **Ate** (A1) and **ban** (B3). I guessed both from context; see the notes on those segments.

## Text A (a folk tale)

**A1.** mon MUyi Ate, NOso MEli pi BENdo FEli sin WUpi PAsu, un GEgi mu sin Obu.
- Gloss: at time [Ate?], animal small of leg many DONE need food, but rain NOT DONE come ("not yet").
- Reading: **Once upon a time, Spider needed food, but the rains still hadn't come.**
- Notes: "Small animal with many legs" = spider (possibly an Anansi-type tale; "insect" or "centipede" are less likely). **Ate** isn't listed. As a scene-setter after "at a time" it reads as "once / long ago". It might be a garbled **afe** "yesterday", but that makes no sense in a folk tale.

**A2.** wan NAgu NOso GAke man DINyo YUfa pi PAsu se FOwo tun YOlan.
- Gloss: he/she see animal slow who carry bag of food from ground to river.
- Reading: **He saw Tortoise carrying a bag of food from the fields down to the river.**
- Notes: "Slow animal" = tortoise (or turtle; snail is possible). "From the ground" probably means from the farm or fields.

**A3.** "SOnu," NOso MEli Oyi, "so ku: lon YEma me."
- Gloss: "friend," animal small say, "REQUEST POLITE: I help you."
- Reading: **"Friend," said Spider, "please, let me help you."**

**A4.** NOso GAke gun GAke, gi tan NOso MEli DINyo YUfa mon NEbe pi nen.
- Gloss: animal slow is slow, because this, animal small carry bag at back of self.
- Reading: **Tortoise was slow, so Spider carried the bag on his own back.**

**A5.** mon YOlan, NOso MEli NAwi TImon pi PAsu, nin NOso GAke PENsi KIti pi nen.
- Gloss: at river, animal small eat half of food, while animal slow wash hand of self.
- Reading: **At the river, Spider ate half the food while Tortoise was washing his hands.**

**A6.** nin NOso GAke SIgo, wan NAgu YUfa TApun yen BUbo.
- Gloss: when animal slow return, he/she see bag empty and laugh.
- Reading: **When Tortoise came back, he saw the empty bag and laughed.**
- Notes: By the reference rule, **wan** is the most recent clause subject, Tortoise, and the dropped subject after **yen** is Tortoise again. So Tortoise is the one who laughs. Reading it as "saw the empty bag and Spider laughing" would need a subject that isn't there.

**A7.** "me gun GOle, NOso MEli," wan Oyi, "un mon FAwu wo tan, lon PINfe PAsu se FOwo pi lon mon KEmu man me mu LAnu SANne bu."
- Gloss: "you are smart, animal small," he/she say, "but at year after this, I plant food from ground of me at place which you NOT can touch it."
- Reading: **"You're clever, Spider," said Tortoise, "but next year I'll plant the food from my field somewhere you can't get your hands on it."**
- Notes: **wan** = Tortoise (the speech tag doesn't count, and Tortoise is the last subject). "Plant food from my ground" = sow my crops. One possible point: underground or underwater, where Spider can't reach.

## Text B (a family's day)

**B1.** mon LEso, MOle pi MOle pi lon MUge Onti LUmi yen MUge bi TIkun Osa li Osa pi WOnin.
- Gloss: at morning, mother of mother of me make burn fire and make very hot water for water of leaf.
- Reading: **In the morning, my grandmother (my mother's mother) lights the fire and boils water for tea.**
- Notes: "Water of leaves" = tea. "Make very hot water" = boil water.

**B2.** FAba LOwu pi lon Isi tun FOwo pi PINfe gan NOso YANsi la.
- Gloss: sibling male of me go to ground of plant with animal horn PL.
- Reading: **My brother takes the cattle out to the pasture.**
- Notes: "Horned animals" = cattle/cows (could be goats). "Ground of plants" = a field or pasture.

**B3.** YAye gun BUda ban; kon TE GEgi win Obu.
- Gloss: sky is dark [ban?]; CONF 5/6 rain about.to come.
- Reading: **The sky is dark; it will very probably rain soon.**
- Notes: **ban** isn't listed. In that position it might be a variant of **ben** "that" ("that sky"?) or a time word like "already/now". It doesn't change the sense. **kon TE** = 5 in 6, about 83%, so "very likely" / "probably".

**B4.** mon DIli wo MImi KA, Ewo la man PIyi GElu Obu li GOyu mon DEba pi GAla KUdu yen KEgu pi PAsu man sin GUge.
- Gloss: at day after .3 (= half the day), person PL who stay near come to sit at below of tree big and talk of food which DONE grow.
- Reading: **In the afternoon, the neighbours come to sit under the big tree and talk about the crops that have grown.**
- Notes: "Day after .3" = after midday = afternoon. "People who stay near" = neighbours. "Food that has grown" = the crops/harvest.

**B5.** MOle pi MOle pi lon Oyi wun YIkan PAsu gun WONwe, un FAba LOwu pi WEde pi lon PUme wun din KEya SEfu ke DIli KA.
- Gloss: mother of mother of me say that seed food is ready, but sibling male of father of me think that we(excl) must wait more day three.
- Reading: **My grandmother says the grain is ready, but my uncle (my father's brother) thinks we should wait three more days.**
- Notes: "Food seed" = grain (ready to harvest).

**B6.** mon YAmi, din NAwi gan nen nen yen DOma du DOma.
- Gloss: at night, we(excl) eat with self self (= each other) and listen tool listen.
- Reading: **At night we eat together and listen to the radio.**
- Notes: "Thing for listening" = radio, most likely. It could just mean "something to listen to", such as music or a story.
