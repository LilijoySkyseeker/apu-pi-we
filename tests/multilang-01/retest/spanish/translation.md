# Spanish → Apu Pi We (multilang-01 retest)

```
1. Estimados vecinos: les comunicamos que, por acuerdo de la Junta de Propietarios celebrada el pasado 14 de septiembre, a partir del 1 de noviembre entrarán en vigor nuevas normas de uso de las zonas comunes.
1. lanno no nume gelu la, nun din iko me la, wun gi tayu pi fanmu pi no ibe pu nume man sin muwo mon dunan pi mape, dili pi yawu da, wabun duwa la pi bone pi kemu pi we tuko mon dunan pi yawu fi, dili pi fi.
honoured.one AGENT live near PL, POLITE we.excl tell you PL, that because agreement of gathering of AGENT have PLACE live who DONE happen at month of nine, day of ten four, rule new PL of use of place of all start at month of ten one, day of one.
```
Note: "Estimados" = **lanno** before a role phrase (the doc shows it before a name or alone). Neighbour = **no nume gelu** "one who lives near"; owner = **no ibe pu nume** "one who has a dwelling". "Entrar en vigor" = **tuko** "start"; future left unmarked (the date sets it).

```
2. Queda terminantemente prohibido dejar bicicletas, carritos de bebé o cualquier otro objeto en el portal y en los rellanos.
2. go mu mu gun efon yaga bayisikalu la, siwa pi tiwu, lu bo musu eni mon pu inwa yen mon kemu la man gun mon sinye pi beya pi pu nume la.
anyone NOT NOT is allow put bicycle PL, cart of child, or UNSPEC thing other at PLACE enter and at place PL who is at front of door of PLACE live PL.
```
Note: "terminantemente" = emphatic **mu mu**; the impersonal rule = generic **go** + "is allowed". No **tin**: the Spanish states a rule rather than giving an order. **nun** is left out to keep the bluntness. Bicycle borrowed from English (**bayisikalu**, from `names.py`). Landing = "the places in front of the flats' doors"; portal = **pu inwa** "place for entering".

```
3. Los objetos que se encuentren en dichas zonas serán retirados por el conserje sin previo aviso y depositados en el trastero comunitario durante un plazo máximo de quince días.
3. no miya guyi yidu musu la man go geno mon kemu tan la, gan mu iko ya, yen yaga bu la mon pu kuwi musu pi we, nin mu ke se dili yawu te.
AGENT look house take thing PL who anyone find at place this PL, with NOT tell before, and put it PL at PLACE keep thing of all, during NOT more from day ten five.
```
Note: **passive lost.** There is no passive with an agent, so the caretaker (**no miya guyi** "one who watches the building") becomes the subject, and the objects stop being the topic. "Se encuentren" = generic **go** "anyone finds". "Plazo máximo" = **mu ke se** "not more than".

```
4. Transcurrido dicho plazo, la comunidad no se hará responsable de su custodia.
4. wo muyi tan, funti pi no ibe pu nume mu keya kuwi bu la.
after time this, group of AGENT have PLACE live NOT must keep it PL.
```
Note: "hacerse responsable" has no word, so it's rendered as obligation: "doesn't have to keep them". The idea of legal liability is lost.

```
5. El horario de uso de la azotea queda restringido de 10:00 a 20:00 horas, y para acceder a ella será imprescindible solicitar la llave en conserjería.
5. go gun efon bone kuwun na se fi po po po tun sa po po po, yen li isi tun bu, go keya mido li du benbi mon pu pi no miya guyi.
anyone is allow use roof ONLY from one zero zero zero to two zero zero zero, and for go to it, anyone must ask for TOOL open at PLACE of AGENT look house.
```
Note: "restringido" = **na** (only) on the time range. Key = **du benbi** "thing for opening". "Imprescindible" = plain **keya**; no stronger "must" exists.

```
6. Asimismo, se recuerda que no está permitido tender ropa en las ventanas que dan a la fachada principal.
6. wame, nun muge imu me la, wun go mu gun efon yaga lonne li kilan mon degun pi senmo la man gun mon sinye pi guyi.
also, POLITE make remember you PL, that anyone NOT is allow put clothes for dry at glass of wall PL who is at front of house.
```
Note: impersonal "se recuerda" = subject left out (rule 11) rather than a stated "we". Plain **mu** here vs **mu mu** in 2, as in Spanish ("no está permitido" vs "terminantemente prohibido"). Window = **degun pi senmo** "glass of the wall"; "tender ropa" = "put clothes to dry".

```
7. Para sufragar la reparación del ascensor, se aprobó una derrama extraordinaria de 180 euros por vivienda, que se cargará en el recibo de diciembre.
7. li unfu muge upe elabeyitalu, sin tayu unfu eni pi yulowu loki kefi yawu li pu nume fi, yen bu gun mon mabo pi unfu pi dunan pi yawu sa.
for pay make good elevator, DONE agree pay other of euro hundred eight ten for PLACE live one, and it is at paper of pay of month of ten two.
```
Note: "se aprobó" = subject left out. Elevator and euro borrowed (**elabeyitalu**, **yulowu**). "Por vivienda" has no distributive word, so it's "for one dwelling". "Extraordinaria" = **eni** "other, additional". The relative "que se cargará" became a separate clause, because a **man** clause would attach to **pu nume**.

```
8. Rogamos a todos los residentes que respeten estas disposiciones en beneficio de la buena convivencia.
8. so nun, ewo we man nume mon guyi tan, lefan wabun tan la, li pen we nume upe gan nen nen.
REQUEST POLITE, person all who live at house this, follow rule this PL, for we.incl all live good with self self.
```
Note: "convivencia" = "we all live well with each other". The plural speaker ("rogamos") isn't marked: **so** is just the speaker's request.

```
9. Para cualquier duda o aclaración, pueden dirigirse a la administración de lunes a viernes, de 9:00 a 14:00 horas.
9. don me la ibe bo mido lu momu lafi bo musu, nun me la lenu kegu gan no wungu funti se dili pi fi tun dili pi te, se mape po po tun fi da po po.
if you PL have UNSPEC question or want understand UNSPEC thing, POLITE you PL can talk with AGENT lead group from day of one to day of five, from nine zero zero to one four zero zero.
```
Note: the administration refers to itself in the third person (**no wungu funti** "the one who leads the community"), as in Spanish.

```
10. Atentamente, la Administración de la Comunidad de Propietarios.
10. nun se no wungu funti pi no ibe pu nume.
POLITE from AGENT lead group of AGENT have PLACE live.
```
Note: a sign-off as a fragment, "(politely) from …". There is no set closing formula.

```
11. Muy señores míos: les escribo como propietaria del 3.º B para mostrarles mi desacuerdo con la circular que nos dejaron ayer en el buzón.
11. lanno la, nun lon gun futa man ibe pu nume pi ka en bewu, yen lon tusi tun me la li wuye wun lon mu tayu gan mabo man me la sin yaga mon pu dunu mabo pi din afe.
honoured.one PL, POLITE I am woman who have PLACE live of three NAME B, and I write to you PL for show that I NOT agree with paper who you PL DONE put at PLACE get paper of we.excl yesterday.
```
Note: gender stated once (**futa man ibe** "woman who owns"). "Como propietaria" became "I am … and I write". The flat label 3.º B is **pu nume pi ka** + the letter as a name (see ambiguities). Mailbox = **pu dunu mabo** "place for getting papers". **din**: the residents, not including the administration.

```
12. Para empezar, yo no recuerdo haber votado nada de esto, entre otras cosas porque la junta se convocó con cuatro días de antelación y a una hora en la que casi todos estábamos trabajando.
12. ya, fule lon mu imu wun lon sin waka bo musu pi tan, yen ukun fi gun wun sin yebin din tun fanmu ya dili da, li muyi man fu we pi din le doku.
first, FOCUS I NOT remember that I DONE choose UNSPEC thing of this, and reason one is that DONE call we.excl to gathering before day four, for time who about all of we.excl ONGOING work.
```
Note: contrastive "yo" = **fule lon**. Vote = **waka** "choose". "Entre otras cosas" = "one reason is". "Casi todos" = **fu we** "about all": **fu** is defined for numbers, so this stretches it. "Se convocó" = subject left out.

```
13. Lo de las bicicletas me parece, sinceramente, un despropósito.
13. lon pume wun wabun pi bayisikalu gun bi mu gole, feko.
I think that rule of bicycle is very NOT smart, true.
```
Note: "sinceramente" = **feko** in the sentence-final stance slot, which the doc says ordinary words may fill. "Despropósito" = "very unwise".

```
14. En este edificio no hay garaje ni cuarto de bicis, así que ¿dónde quieren que las metamos, en la bañera?
14. guyi tan mu ibe pu kuwi tale lu pu kuwi bayisikalu, gi tan me la momu wun din yaga bu la mon kemu yu. a min mon pu pensi gawo.
house this NOT have PLACE keep car or PLACE keep bicycle, because this you PL want that we.excl put it PL at place WH. NONLITERAL Q at PLACE wash body.
```
Note: irony = **a** on the sarcastic "en la bañera?", stacked with **min** (see ambiguities). Garage = "place for keeping cars"; bathtub = "place for washing the body", which could also mean a shower or bathroom.

```
15. Y que el conserje se lleve las cosas de los vecinos sin avisar no es una norma de convivencia, es buscarse un conflicto.
15. yen don no miya guyi yidu musu pi no nume gelu la gan mu iko ya, tan mu gun wabun li nume upe gan nen nen, un gun lite li geno migi.
and if AGENT look house take thing of AGENT live near PL with NOT tell before, this NOT is rule for live good with self self, but is way for find fight.
```
Note: the subject "que …" clause became an **if** clause picked up by **tan**. "No es X, es Y" = **mu gun … un gun …**. "Buscarse un conflicto" = "a way to find a fight"; the reflexive "for oneself" is lost.

```
16. Con la azotea pasa tres cuartos de lo mismo: en julio y agosto, a las ocho de la tarde todavía hace un calor que no se aguanta, y es justo a esa hora cuando a la gente le apetece subir.
16. wabun pi kuwun gun to kige. mon dunan pi oma yen dunan pi kefi, mon sa po po po, wunta le gun bi tikun, go mu lenu [X1] tan, yen ewo la momu falan fule mon muyi ben.
rule of roof is rather same. at month of seven and month of eight, at two zero zero zero, air still is very hot, anyone NOT can [X1] this, and person PL want climb FOCUS at time that.
```
Note: the idiom "tres cuartos de lo mismo" = **to kige** "rather the same". The colon became a full stop. The cleft "es justo a esa hora cuando" = **fule** on the time phrase. "Ocho de la tarde" uses the 24-hour clock, as the doc requires.

```
17. Si encima hay que pedir la llave en conserjería y el conserje se marcha a las tres, en la práctica la mitad de los vecinos no podrá subir nunca entre semana.
17. don wame go keya mido li du benbi mon pu pi no miya guyi, yen no miya guyi kusa mon fi te po po, mon feko, timon pi no nume gelu la mu lenu falan genfo gimu fi mon dili pi doku.
if also anyone must ask for TOOL open at PLACE of AGENT look house, and AGENT look house leave at one five zero zero, at true, half of AGENT live near PL NOT can climb EVEN occasion one at day of work.
```
Note: "encima" = **wame**. "Nunca" = "not even once" (**genfo gimu fi**). "Entre semana" = **dili pi doku** "days of work"; **dili pi** + digit would be one weekday. "En la práctica" = **mon feko** "in truth", a loose fit.

```
18. En cuanto a la derrama, nadie discute que el ascensor haya que arreglarlo, pero sí tenemos derecho a que nos expliquen por qué cuesta lo que cuesta.
18. gan unfu eni, ewo fa mu tayu wun go keya muge upe elabeyitalu, un din fule gun efon lugo wun muge upe bu wupi nimo tan gi yu.
with pay other, person none NOT agree that anyone must make good elevator, but we.excl FOCUS is allow know that make good it need money this because WH.
```
Note: emphatic "sí tenemos" = **fule** before the verb. **"Derecho" (a right, an entitlement) is not carried**: **gun efon** "are allowed" means permission. "Que nos expliquen" became "know". "Cuesta lo que cuesta" = "fixing it needs this money". "En cuanto a" = a scene-setting **gan** phrase.

```
19. Les pido que nos enseñen al menos dos o tres presupuestos antes de cobrarnos un solo euro.
19. so nun, wuye din, ge sunbu pa sa lu ka pi nimo, ya me la yidu genfo yulowu fi se din.
REQUEST POLITE, show we.excl, RESULT count AT.LEAST two or three of money, before you PL take EVEN euro one from we.excl.
```
Note: quote/estimate = **ge sunbu pi nimo** "a counting of money". "Un solo euro" = **genfo** … **fi** "even one".

```
20. No creo que sea mucho pedir, teniendo en cuenta que la última reparación se hizo hace apenas dos años y ya estamos otra vez igual.
20. gi sin muge upe bu na ya fawu sa, yen eda din moti ibe batu kige, lon mu pume wun lon mido bi feli.
because DONE make good it ONLY before year two, and now we.excl again have problem same, I NOT think that I ask very many.
```
Note: the reason clause is fronted because a final **gi** clause would attach to "I ask". "Última" is only implied. "Mucho pedir" = "ask very much"; there is no "too much".

```
21. Por todo ello, solicito formalmente que estos puntos se vuelvan a votar en una junta extraordinaria, convocada con tiempo y en horario de tarde.
21. gi tan we, so nun nun, moti waka wabun tan la mon kegin, mon fanmu eni man go yebin ya muyi netu.
because this all, REQUEST POLITE POLITE, again choose rule this PL at evening, at gathering other who anyone call before time long.
```
Note: **"formalmente" (on the record, official) is not carried.** **nun nun** gives formality and deference only. "Se vuelvan a votar" = subject left out. "Mon kegin" comes before the meeting phrase so the **man** clause attaches to **fanmu**.

```
22. Quedo a la espera de su respuesta y, mientras tanto, mi bicicleta seguirá donde ha estado los últimos seis años: en mi rellano, sin molestar a nadie.
22. nun lon le sefu febo pi me la, yen nin tan, bayisikalu pi lon le piyi mon kemu man bu sin piyi nin fawu ufo, mon kemu man gun mon sinye pi beya pi lon, yen bu gun batu li ewo fa.
POLITE I ONGOING wait answer of you PL, and during this, bicycle of I still stay at place who it DONE stay during year six, at place who is at front of door of I, and it is problem for person none.
```
Note: "seguirá" = **le** "still". The relative "where it has been" leaves its place gap unmarked (**kemu man bu sin piyi**). The colon apposition became a comma.

## Rules that seemed ambiguous

- **Writing numbers:** the Writing section says numbers are written in digits, but every example in the doc spells them out (**dunan pi yawu**, **mon fi te ka po**). Clock times like `10:00` would also need the banned `:`. Numbers are spelled out here.
- **Day of the month:** "points in time are read digit by digit", but the example **dunan pi yawu** (month 10) uses quantity reading. 14 is written **yawu da** by analogy; digit reading would be **fi da**. Whether 09:00 is **mape po po** or **po mape po po** is also unclear.
- **lanno before a role or common noun** ("Estimados vecinos"): the doc only shows it before a name, or alone.
- **Two speech-act particles** (**a min**, sarcastic question): the doc doesn't say whether they stack, or in which order.
- **Letters as labels** (3.º B): no rule. Here the letter's name is treated as a name. `names.py` returns **biu**/**beu**, which breaks the vowel-glide rule, so the glide was added by hand (**bewu**, from Spanish "be").
- **Prepositional phrase after a relative clause** (3, 15): rule 8 says it attaches to "its clause's main verb", but it isn't clear whether a comma closes the relative clause. Here a comma is taken to close it.
- **Relative clause with a place gap** (22, "where it has stood"): the doc shows only subject gaps.
- **Determiner order** with both plural and demonstrative (**kemu tan la** vs **kemu la tan**): no rule.
- **Distributive "per/each"** (180 € per dwelling): no word or construction.
- Spanish impersonal *se*: leaving out the subject (rule 11) seems right, but after a speech act it could read as "you" (21).
