## Losses in sentences scored 2

- **Bureaucratic vs colloquial register.** The notice's legalese ("dichas zonas", "sufragar", "disposiciones", "transcurrido dicho plazo", "custodia") and the neighbour's colloquialisms ("tres cuartos de lo mismo", "un calor que no se aguanta", "despropósito") come out in one flat register in Apu Pi We. The back-translator rebuilt the officialese from the genre, so it was deduced. The idiom "tres cuartos de lo mismo" became "más o menos lo mismo" (L).
- **3:** The passive with the objects as topic ("los objetos… serán retirados por el conserje") became active with the caretaker as subject (L, per the notes).
- **4:** "No se hará responsable de su custodia" is a legal liability disclaimer. It became "no estará obligada a conservarlos", a duty to keep (L, per the notes).
- **5:** "Imprescindible" became "necesario", because there is no stronger "must" (L).
- **6:** "Fachada principal" became "fachada del edificio". The Apu Pi We said "front of the house", so the back-translator loosened it (B, minor). This matters because rear and patio windows are where hanging laundry is normal.
- **7:** "Derrama extraordinaria" became plain "derrama". The translator used **eni** "other/additional" and the back-translator dropped it (L/B, minor). "Por vivienda" was deduced; there is no distributive.
- **8:** The plural speaker of "rogamos" isn't marked; it was restored from context.
- **12:** "Estábamos" (past) became "estamos" (habitual), because **le** is unmarked for past.
- **13:** "Un despropósito" (absurd, outrageous) was weakened to "muy poco sensata" ("very not smart"; T, since a stronger choice was likely available).
- **14:** "Bañera" became "ducha": **pu pensi gawo** "place for washing the body" is ambiguous (L). The joke still lands.
- **17:** The exasperation in "encima" became the neutral "además".
- **18:** "Derecho" (an entitlement) was carried only as permission (L). "Que nos expliquen" (puts the burden on the administration) became "saber" (T/L).
- **22:** The defiant future "seguirá" became the present "sigue" (future unmarked; L). The defiance still comes through from context.

## Summary

Propositional meaning survived almost completely (41/44). Every focus, emphasis, "even one" and sarcasm marker that the translator placed was read back correctly. Only one root choice (**wungu** "lead", which turned the Administration into a Presidency) and two unencodable legal or procedural terms in sentence 21 cost points. What Apu Pi We flattens is register: the legalese of the notice, the cold "Muy señores míos" versus "Estimados", "formalmente", and the idioms. The back-translator rebuilt most of that from genre rather than from the text.
