## What's still missing

1. **Respect for someone with no name.** **lanno** is defined only before a
   name or alone in address. All three translators asked whether it can go
   before a role or kin word ("the respected landlord", "Uncle-ji"). Hindi
   lost "sir" said *about* the boss, and respect for Gupta's daughter and the
   boss's wife, who are never named.
2. **っけ, the half-remembered "wasn't it?",** still went to **min** and came
   back as a confident check. That's the watch-list case: **min** pressed
   into a stance job.
3. **Unseeded, as designed:** Japanese わ, よ, さ; hesitation (んー, あー);
   an attention-getter with no name (ねえ). They're left to grow.
4. **Minor English-gloss slips (E):** "administration" built on **wungu**
   "lead" came back as "the President"; 嘆く via "lament" → **bayi** "sad";
   めんどい via **doku** "work".
5. **Missing words, all already on the list:** strange, trust, midday,
   "poor fellow", formally.
