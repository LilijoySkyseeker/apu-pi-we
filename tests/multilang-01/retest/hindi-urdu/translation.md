# Hindi–Urdu → Apu Pi We (retest)

Names by `tools/names.py`: वर्मा → **en waluma**, गुप्ता → **en guputa**, रमेश → **en lamesu**, पूजा → **en puda**. Loans: truck → **tulaku**, hospital → **osupitalu** (institution). Office = **pu pi mabo** (given in the reference). A says **nun** where Hindi has जी and uses **lanno** for सर; B, the boss, uses neither (तुम/आप difference).

```
A: सर, नमस्ते। आपके पास दो मिनट होंगे? कल वाली बात बतानी थी।
A: lanno, upe. min nun me ibe minitu sa. lon wupi iko me, musu man muwo afe.
A: sir, hello. Q POLITE you have minute two. I need tell you, thing that happen yesterday.
```

```
B: हाँ हाँ, आओ, बैठो। क्या हुआ कल? सुना है कुछ गड़बड़ हो गई थी।
B: ibe ibe. tin obu yen goyu. yu muwo afe. fen batu in sin muwo.
B: have have. INSTR come and sit. what happen yesterday. TOLD problem some DONE happen.
```
हाँ हाँ: no yes-word, so the verb of the question is echoed, doubled for "yes, yes".

```
A: जी, गड़बड़ तो नहीं कहूँगा, पर दिन बड़ा अजीब रहा। सुबह जब मैं ऑफ़िस पहुँचा तो वर्मा मैडम पहले से बैठी थीं, काफ़ी परेशान लग रही थीं।
A: nun lon mu oyi wun ben gun fule batu, un dili sin gun bi [X1]. mon leso, nin lon liwon pu pi mabo, lanno en waluma le goyu ya, o wan feme to yomo.
A: POLITE I NOT say that that be FOCUS problem, but day DONE be very [X1]. at morning, while I arrive place of paper, honoured.one NAME Verma ONGOING sit beforehand, SEEN she feel rather bad.
```
"लग रही थीं" (looked) = seen source **o**. परेशान = "feel rather bad"; no word for worried. Feminine (मैडम, बैठी) not carried.

```
B: वर्मा जी? वो तो इतनी जल्दी आती ही नहीं। क्या बात थी?
B: min lanno en waluma. wan mu mu obu mon muyi tan. batu gun yu.
B: Q honoured.one NAME Verma. she NOT NOT come at time this. problem be what.
```
आती ही नहीं = emphatic NOT (**mu mu**). Honorific plural on वो lost (bare **wan**).

```
A: उन्होंने बताया कि गुप्ता साहब का फ़ोन आया था, रात को ही। उनका माल जो परसों जाना था, वो अब तक गोदाम से निकला ही नहीं।
A: wan sin iko wun lanno en guputa sin yebin fule mon yami. musu pi wan keya isi afe ya dili fi, un bu mu mu sin kusa pu kuwi musu.
A: she DONE tell that honoured.one NAME Gupta DONE call FOCUS at night. thing of him must go yesterday before day one, but it NOT NOT DONE leave place keep thing.
```
परसों = **afe ya dili fi** (anchor + offset). गोदाम = **pu kuwi musu** "place for keeping things". Relative clause avoided: **musu pi wan man …** would attach to **wan**.

```
B: अरे! तो गोदाम में किससे बात की?
B: ba. gi tan, me sin kegu gan ewo yu man gun mon pu kuwi musu.
B: EXCL. because this, you DONE talk with person what who be at place keep thing.
```
अरे = **ba** with everything else left out (rule 11). "In the godown" put in a relative clause, since a bare place phrase would attach to the verb ("talked, at the godown").

```
A: मैंने रमेश को फ़ोन लगाया, वो जो गोदाम में बैठता है, लंबा सा लड़का। दो बार तो उठाया ही नहीं, तीसरी बार में उठाया और बोला, भैया, ट्रक वाला ही नहीं आया।
A: lon yebin en lamesu. wan gun lowu loyen to netu man doku mon pu kuwi musu, iwun. gimu sa, wan mu mu febo. mon gimu pi ka, wan febo yen oyi, "faba, ewo pi tulaku mu mu obu."
A: I call NAME Ramesh. he be man young rather long who work at place keep thing, as.you.know. occasion two, he NOT NOT answer. at occasion of three, he answer and say, "sibling, person of truck NOT NOT come."
```
"वो जो …" = shared ground **iwun**. The apposition became its own sentence: an NP after a final pause would read as someone being addressed. लंबा = **netu** "long", as in Hindi. भैया = kin word in address, **faba**. बैठता (is posted there) = **doku**.

```
B: ट्रक वाला क्यों नहीं आया? उसका तो पक्का था न?
B: ewo pi tulaku mu obu gi yu. wan sin tayu, min.
B: person of truck NOT come because what. he DONE agree, right.
```

```
A: जी, पक्का था। पर पता चला उसकी बीवी की तबीयत ख़राब हो गई थी, अस्पताल ले गया था। किसी को बताया भी नहीं, बेचारा घबराया हुआ था।
A: nun sin tayu. un ofin pi wan sin lewe simi, yen wan sin isi gan yi tun osupitalu, donon. wan mu iko genfo go. [X2] gun banga.
A: POLITE DONE agree. but spouse of him DONE become sick, and he DONE go with OBV to hospital, it.turned.out. he NOT tell EVEN anyone. [X2] be panic.
```
पता चला = **donon** (it turned out). **yi** = the wife, set against the topic.

```
B: हम्म। चलो, वो तो समझ में आता है। फिर तुमने क्या किया?
B: mm. lon lafi pa ben. wo, me muge yu.
B: mm. I understand AT.LEAST that. after, you do what.
```
वो तो (concessive) = **pa** "at least that". चलो (discourse "well, okay") has no carrier and is dropped.

```
A: मैडम बोलीं दूसरा ट्रक देखो। मैंने दो-तीन जगह फ़ोन किया, सबने मना कर दिया। तभी हमारी नई इंटर्न है ना, पूजा, उसने कहा कि उसके चाचा का ट्रांसपोर्ट का काम है, वो पूछ सकती है।
A: lanno en waluma oyi, "tin geno tulaku eni." lon yebin kemu sa lu ka. kemu we mu tayu. en puda gun no yesa doku duwa pi pen, iwun. nin ben, wan oyi wun faba lowu pi wede pi sun ibe doku pi dinyo musu, yen sun lenu mido.
A: honoured.one NAME Verma say, "INSTR find truck other." I call place two or three. place all NOT agree. NAME Pooja be one.who learn work new of we.incl, as.you.know. during that, she say that sibling man of father of LOG have work of carry thing, and LOG can ask.
```
"हमारी" = inclusive **pen** (A's and B's office). "है ना" = **iwun**; Pooja's introduction is moved before तभी so that no bare NP stands between pauses. इंटर्न = **no yesa doku** "one who learns work". चाचा = father's brother; "younger" is not carried. **sun** = Pooja inside her own reported words.

```
B: अच्छा? वो छोटी सी लड़की, जो चश्मा लगाती है?
B: min feko. min futa loyen to meli man yunko degun pi funo.
B: Q true. Q woman young rather small who wear glass of eye.
```
**tiwu futa** is avoided: it is the compound for "daughter".

```
A: जी, वही। मुझे लगा कुछ होगा नहीं, पर उसने फ़ोन किया और आधे घंटे में उसके चाचा ख़ुद आ गए, बुज़ुर्ग आदमी हैं, सफ़ेद दाढ़ी वाले।
A: nun fule wan. lon sin pume wun musu fa win muwo, un wan yebin, yen nin awuwa timon, genfo faba lowu pi wede pi wan obu. yi gun lowu pani man ibe sonfa pinda pi suku.
A: POLITE FOCUS she. I DONE think that thing none SOON happen, but she call, and during hour half, EVEN sibling man of father of her come. OBV be man old who have hair white of face.
```
वही = **fule wan**. ख़ुद आ गए (came in person) = **genfo** "even he came". बुज़ुर्ग (respectful "elder") = plain **pani**, and the honorific हैं is lost. Beard = "white hair of face", with the modifier before **pi**.

```
B: ख़ुद आ गए? वाह। फिर?
B: min genfo wan obu. ba upe. yu muwo wo.
B: Q EVEN he come. EXCL good. what happen after.
```

```
A: उन्होंने कहा गाड़ी तो दे देंगे, पर किराया थोड़ा ज़्यादा लगेगा, ड्राइवर को रात भर चलाना पड़ेगा। मैंने मैडम से पूछा, मैडम बोलीं सर से पूछ लो, पर आपका फ़ोन बंद आ रहा था।
A: wan oyi wun sun fule win neke tulaku, un sanwa win gun to ke kudu, no muge kobi tulaku keya muge kobi bu nin yami minwe. lon mido lanno en waluma, yen lanno en waluma oyi, "tin mido no wungu." un liga pi me gun tapi.
A: he say that LOG FOCUS SOON give truck, but rent SOON be rather more big, one.who make move truck must make move it during night whole. I ask honoured.one NAME Verma, and honoured.one NAME Verma say, "INSTR ask one.who lead." but phone of you be closed.
```
"गाड़ी तो दे देंगे" (concessive "sure, they'll give") = **fule** on the verb. Driver = **no muge kobi tulaku** "one who makes the truck move" (causative). मैडम is repeated as in Hindi. "सर" spoken *about* B: **lanno** alone is only for address, so B is **no wungu** (the boss) and the title is lost. बंद = **gun tapi** "is closed", as in Hindi.

```
B: हाँ, मैं कल शादी में था, फ़ोन साइलेंट पर था। तो तुमने अपने से हाँ कर दी?
B: tapi. afe lon gun mon pu lewe ofin, yen liga gun mange. gi tan, min me tayu se nen.
B: closed. yesterday I be at place become spouse, and phone be quiet. because this, Q you agree from self.
```
Wedding = **pu lewe ofin** "place of becoming spouses". अपने से = **se nen** "from yourself", as in Hindi.

```
A: जी सर, माफ़ी चाहता हूँ, पर मुझे लगा गुप्ता साहब नाराज़ हो गए तो उससे ज़्यादा नुक़सान होगा। इसलिए मैंने हाँ कह दिया।
A: nun tayu, lanno. nun sepi, un lon pume wun don lanno en guputa lewe bonko, batu win gun ke kudu se ben. gi tan, lon tayu.
A: POLITE agree, honoured.one. POLITE apologize, but I think that if honoured.one NAME Gupta become angry, problem SOON be more big than that. because this, I agree.
```
नुक़सान (loss) = "a bigger problem"; there is no word for loss/damage.

```
B: कितना ज़्यादा माँगा उन्होंने?
B: wan momu nimo ke feli wifu yu.
B: he want money more many number what.
```
माँगना (ask for) = **momu**, kept apart from पूछना (ask a question) = **mido**. Honorific उन्होंने lost.

```
A: क़रीब चार हज़ार ऊपर। लेकिन सर, शाम को चाचा जी ने ख़ुद ही कहा कि बच्ची की वजह से कर रहे हैं, तो दो हज़ार कम कर देते हैं।
A: fu da munno. lanno, un mon kegin, lanno faba lowu pi wede sin oyi se nen wun sun muge tan gi tiwu, gi tan sun win momu sa munno ke uyo.
A: about 4 1000. honoured.one, but at evening, honoured.one sibling man of father DONE say from self that LOG do this because child, because this LOG SOON want 2 1000 more few.
```
The answer gives only the new part; "ऊपर" is understood from the question. चाचा जी = **lanno** + kin phrase (see unclear rules). ख़ुद ही कहा = **se nen** "of his own accord". बच्ची = **tiwu**, losing the feminine and the diminutive.

```
B: हा हा, ये तो अच्छा हुआ। और गुप्ता साहब को बताया?
B: ba, tan sin muwo upe. yen min me sin iko lanno en guputa.
B: EXCL, this DONE happen good. and Q you DONE tell honoured.one NAME Gupta.
```
The laughter (हा हा) has no word; **ba** carries the exclamation.

```
A: जी, रात को फ़ोन किया। पहले तो बहुत गुस्से में थे, बोले आप लोगों पर भरोसा करना ही ग़लत था। फिर उनकी बेटी ने फ़ोन ले लिया, आजकल सारा हिसाब-किताब वही देखती हैं। उन्होंने आराम से सुना और कहा कि आज दोपहर तक पहुँच जाए तो कोई दिक़्क़त नहीं। और सर, अभी आते वक़्त ड्राइवर का फ़ोन आया था, शहर में घुस गया है।
A: nun lon yebin wan mon yami. ya, wan gun bi bonko, yen oyi, "fule [X3] me la sin gun gagan." wo, tiwu futa pi wan yidu liga. nin dili tan la, fule yi sunbu nimo we. yi doma fanse, yen oyi wun don bu liwon ya fi sa po po mefa, gun batu fa. lanno, yen nin lon le obu, no muge kobi tulaku sin yebin lon. fen wan sin inwa oton.
A: POLITE I call him at night. beforehand, he be very angry, and say, "FOCUS [X3] you PL DONE be wrong." after, child woman of him take phone. during day this PL, FOCUS OBV count money all. OBV listen calmly, and say that if it arrive before 1 2 0 0 today, there.is problem none. honoured.one, and while I ONGOING come, one.who make move truck DONE call me. TOLD he DONE enter city.
```
आप लोगों (Gupta's polite plural, even in anger) = plain **me la**. **yi** = the daughter, set against Gupta. वही देखती हैं = **fule yi**. देखती (looks after) = "counts all the money"; **miya** would read as literal looking. दोपहर (vague midday) = clock 12:00. "घुस गया है" is the driver's report = **fen**. The daughter's honorific plural is lost; she has no name to take **lanno**.

```
B: शाबाश। फ़ैसला तुमने ठीक लिया, पर अगली बार मेरा फ़ोन न लगे तो घर पर फ़ोन कर देना, वो मुझे ढूँढ लेंगी। पूजा को मेरी तरफ़ से धन्यवाद बोलना, और उस ट्रक वाले के पैसे-वैसे मत काटना, पहले पता करो उसकी बीवी कैसी है।
B: ba, me doku upe. me waka denku, un wo tan, don me mu lenu yebin lon, tin yebin guyi, yi win geno lon. tin oyi lota tun en puda se lon, yen tin mu yidu nimo lu musu eni se ewo ben pi tulaku. ya, tin mido wun ofin pi wan gun fu yu.
B: EXCL, you work good. you choose correct, but after this, if you NOT can call me, INSTR call house, OBV SOON find me. INSTR say thanks to NAME Pooja from me, and INSTR NOT take money or thing other from person that of truck. beforehand, INSTR ask that spouse of him be how.
```
"वो … लेंगी" (an unnamed woman at home, honorific feminine) = **yi**, which keeps her unnamed but loses gender and respect. मेरी तरफ़ से = **se lon** "from me". पैसे-वैसे (echo word, "money or anything") = **nimo lu musu eni**. अगली बार = **wo tan** "after this".

## Unclear rules

1. lanno before a non-name. The reference allows **lanno** before a name, or alone in address. चाचा जी has no name, so I used **lanno faba lowu pi wede**. This isn't covered. A respected person without a name (Gupta's daughter, the wife at home) has no respect carrier at all.
2. Bare title in reference. सर is used *about* B ("सर से पूछ लो"), but **lanno** alone is for address only. I used **no wungu**.
3. Order of an address and a linker before the particle (लेकिन सर, और सर). I wrote address, then linker, then scene-setting.
4. Apposition. No rule covers it. "X, NP" at the end of a sentence reads as an address, so I split appositives into separate sentences (रमेश …, पूजा …).
5. fule with an aspect word: **sun fule win neke** or **sun win fule neke**? I put fule first.
6. Fronted count phrase (**gimu sa, …**): the scene-setting rule names time and place, not number of occasions.
7. Emphatic "himself" (ख़ुद "in person"): **nen** is only described as reflexive. I used **genfo**.
8. yi for someone never mentioned (the woman at home), and **wan/yi** when a newly introduced person becomes the topic within one turn (the uncle, the daughter).
9. A lone particle as a whole utterance (**ba.** for अरे) under rule 11.
10. ke with amounts: **ke feli** or **ke kudu** for money; stacking **to ke kudu** "rather more".
11. Vague times of day (दोपहर): there is no word, and the clock-time rule forces a precise time.
