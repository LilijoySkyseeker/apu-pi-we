**Tally (45 instances):** kept from the text: 19; carried in the text with the form changed: 7 (6 kept the respect: 3 Verma rows and 3 Gupta rows, where मैडम/साहब → जी; 1 is भैया → भाई); filled in by inference only: 15; lost: 4 (सर about B → बॉस, the daughter's honorific, the wife at home, बेचारा).

Pattern: name + **lanno** and address **lanno**/**nun** come through reliably. Every honorific plural pronoun or verb (वो/उन्होंने/हैं for Verma, Gupta, the uncle and the daughter) is bare **wan**/**yi** in the text. The back-translator restored them for every man, from the "lanno + name" earlier in the turn or from the elder description, but not for the daughter, who has no name to carry **lanno**. Gender is never in the text: Verma becomes a man every time, and बीवी and बच्ची were guessed right.

## 3. Other losses in 2-point turns

- **T4:** इतनी जल्दी (so early) → "इस वक़्त" (at this time); the translator wrote **mon muyi tan**, so "early" is gone (T, minor). Verma is masculine. The honorific plural comes only from inference.
- **T5:** गुप्ता साहब → गुप्ता जी, so the respectful-client/businessman register of साहब is flattened. अब तक (still, even now) was dropped in translation (T, minor).
- **T7:** भैया → भाई, so the softer, respectful brotherly address is gone.
- **T10:** चलो (well, okay) was dropped in translation; the back-translator restored it by guessing.
- **T11:** मैडम → वर्मा जी (the title and her gender are gone).
- **T13:** बुज़ुर्ग आदमी हैं: the honorific is not in the text (plain **pani**), but the back-translator restored it. ख़ुद आ गए → "चाचा तक ख़ुद आ पहुँचे": **genfo** (EVEN) adds an "even he" tone of surprise.
- **T17:** नुक़सान (financial or business loss) → "मामला कहीं बड़ा" (a bigger matter), because there is no word for loss/damage (L). Gupta साहब → जी.
- **T19:** बच्ची: the feminine and the diminutive are not in the text (**tiwu**) and were guessed right.
- **T20:** हा हा: B's laugh is lost and becomes "वाह" (L: no word for laughter).
- **Throughout:** B's तुम and A's आप are marked only by the presence or absence of **nun**/**lanno**; the back-translator read this asymmetry correctly.

## 4. Summary

The plot, who did what for whom, the hearsay and evidential markers, and the boss–employee register all survive (39/44). Nearly every loss is a gap in the language (L), not translator or reader error. The weak point is respect for people who are talked about rather than addressed: honorific pronouns and verbs appear only through inference, and a respected person with no name (Gupta's daughter, the boss's wife at home) has no way to receive respect. Verma's gender and the sympathy word बेचारा cannot be carried at all.
