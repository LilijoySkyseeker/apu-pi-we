# Russian dialogue → Apu Pi We

Speakers: A, B, C. C is Серёга (**en seloga**, from /sʲerʲoga/ via `names.py`; palatalization is dropped). Лёха → **en loka** (/lʲoxa/). Новая Рига → **sola en nobayaliga** "road Novaya-Riga" (role word before the name). Loans via `names.py`: navigator → **nabigeyita**, train → **tuleyin**, spreadsheet → **supulidusitu**, license → **layisansu**, petrol → **petulalu**.

Traffic jam has no word; it is rendered throughout as **tale feli** "many cars", and "standing in a jam" as **kolu** "stand". Whining (ныть) is **kegu bayi** "talk sadly" throughout.

```
A: Слушай, ты в субботу на даче будешь?
A: tin doma. mon dili pi ufo, min me gun mon guyi man gun lini se oton.
A: INSTR listen. at day of 6, Q you be at house REL be far from city.
```
Дача = "house that is far from the city"; the specifically Russian concept (seasonal country house with garden) is lost. Future «будешь» is left unmarked: the Saturday phrase sets the time.

```
B: Ну, если Серёга довезёт. Я ж без машины теперь.
B: don en seloga dinyo lon. eda lon mu ibe tale, min.
B: if NAME Seryoga carry me. now I NOT have car, Q(tag).
```
The main clause ("I'll be there") is left out (rule 11). Hesitant «ну» has no equivalent. «ж» (appeal to what the listener already knows) is rendered by the tag **min** "…, right?", which is closer to asking for confirmation than to «ж»; the same choice is used for «же/ж» in lines 4 and 11.

```
C: Довезу, куда я денусь. Только выезжаем рано, в семь.
C: de dinyo me. a min lon lenu kusa tun kemu yu. un pen kusa [рано], mon oma po po.
C: INTEND carry you. NONLIT Q I can leave to place what. but we.incl leave [early], at 7 0 0.
```
«куда я денусь» is a rhetorical question, marked with **a** (not literal). There is no word for "early", and **ya** "beforehand" means something else, so it stays in brackets. «в семь» is given as a clock label, 07:00, with the leading zero dropped (see unclear rules).

```
B: В семь? Ты серьёзно? Суббота же.
B: min mon oma po po. min me oyi feko. ben gun dili pi ufo, min.
B: Q at 7 0 0. Q you say true. that be day of 6, Q(tag).
```
«Ты серьёзно?» = "are you saying it truly?". **ben** = the situation being discussed (the trip day).

```
C: А ты хочешь в пробке на Новой Риге три часа стоять? Я — нет.
C: un min me momu kolu gan tale feli mon sola en nobayaliga nin awuwa ka. lon mu momu.
C: but Q you want stand with car many at road NAME Novaya-Riga during hour 3. I NOT want.
```
The contrast in «Я — нет» comes from the parallel sentence, and the answer echoes the verb.

```
A: Да ладно, в субботу утром нормально всё, можно в девять спокойно.
A: un, mon leso pi dili pi ufo, musu we gun upe. pen lenu kusa fanse mon mape po po.
A: but, at morning of day of 6, thing all be good. we.incl can leave calm at 9 0 0.
```
The dismissive tone of «да ладно» is reduced to **un** "but". «можно» is possibility, so it is **lenu** (ability) and not **efon** (permission).

```
C: Ага, «нормально». В прошлый раз в девять выехали, приехали к часу. Шашлык в итоге в темноте жарили.
C: a ben gun upe. mon gimu man sin muwo ya, pen kusa mon mape po po, yen liwon mon fi ka po po. gi tan, pen le siten fuyu mon lumi nin buda.
C: NONLIT that be good. at occasion REL DONE happen before, we.incl leave at 9 0 0, and arrive at 1 3 0 0. because this, we.incl ONGOING cook meat at fire during dark.
```
The sarcastic «ага, «нормально»» becomes **a** + an echo. The quotation marks around A's word can't be kept, because a quote needs a say-verb. «В прошлый раз» = "the occasion that happened before" (there is no word for "last/previous"). Шашлык = "meat at the fire". Aspect: the perfective «выехали/приехали» are plain (narrative past, set by **sin**), and the imperfective «жарили» is **le** (ongoing).

```
A: Так это потому что Лёха попросил за пивом заехать, пробки тут ни при чём.
A: un ben muwo gi en loka sin mido pen wun pen isi li bika [пиво]. tale feli mu muge ben.
A: but that happen because NAME Lyokha DONE ask we.incl that we.incl go for buy [beer]. car many NOT do that.
```
«ни при чём» = "the many cars didn't cause that".

```
C: При чём, ещё как при чём. Я по навигатору смотрел, там всё красное было.
C: muge. ba bi muge. lon sin miya nabigeyita, o sola minwe gun wiwi.
C: do. EXCL very do. I DONE look navigator, SEEN road whole be red.
```
An echo answer, then **ba bi** for «ещё как». The evidence he cites (he saw it on the navigator) is marked with **o** (seen).

```
B: Слушайте, давайте я тогда на электричке, а вы как хотите.
B: tin me la doma. gi tan, de lon isi gan tuleyin. me la isi fu me la momu.
B: INSTR you PL listen. because this, INTEND I go with train. you PL go like you PL want.
```
The plural «слушайте/вы» is **me la**. Электричка (a suburban electric train) is just "train".

```
C: Ну вот, начинается. Мы ж договаривались вместе.
C: ba tan tuko. pen sin tayu wun pen isi gan nen nen, min.
C: EXCL this start. we.incl DONE agree that we.incl go with self self, Q(tag).
```
The imperfective «договаривались» implies an agreement now being undone (an annulled result). **sin** only says "done", so that nuance is lost.

```
B: Договаривались, но не в семь утра. Я всю неделю к восьми на работу встаю, мне в выходные хоть раз выспаться надо.
B: sin tayu, un mu mon oma po po. nin nelon minwe, lon unda li liwon mon pu doku ya kefi po po. mon dili la man lon mu doku, lon wupi wopo kete pa gimu fi.
B: DONE agree, but NOT at 7 0 0. during week whole, I wake for arrive at place work before 8 0 0. at day PL REL I NOT work, I need sleep full AT.LEAST occasion 1.
```
«утра» is dropped: a 24-hour clock label already says morning, so the emphasis it adds is lost. Выходные = "days when I don't work". Выспаться = **wopo kete** "sleep full" (a manner modifier). «хоть раз» = **pa gimu fi**.

```
A: Вот, человек дело говорит. Серёж, ну давай в восемь хотя бы, компромисс.
A: ba ewo tan oyi feko. en seloga. so pen kusa pa mon kefi po po. tan gun lifin.
A: EXCL person this say true. NAME Seryoga. REQ we.incl leave AT.LEAST at 8 0 0. this be between.
```
«человек» (B referred to in the third person) is **ewo tan** "this person". The affectionate vocative «Серёж», as opposed to «Серёга», can't be shown: there is one name form. Компромисс = "this is in between" (8 lies between 7 and 9). The vocative is its own fragment, because rule 1 doesn't allow it before the particle.

```
C: В восемь — это уже не то. Ладно, смотрите: если в восемь, то потом не ноете, что долго едем.
C: mon kefi po po, ben mu gun kige. lon tayu. tin me la miya. don pen kusa mon kefi po po, tin me la mu kegu bayi wun pen isi nin muyi netu.
C: at 8 0 0, that NOT be same. I agree. INSTR you PL look. if we.incl leave at 8 0 0, INSTR you PL NOT talk sad that we.incl go during time long.
```
«уже» has no equivalent. The grudging concession in «ладно» comes out as a neutral **lon tayu**. «потом» is left to the condition.

```
A: Да кто ноет-то? Ты сам больше всех ноешь, когда стоим.
A: a min ewo yu kegu bayi. ba me ke kegu bayi se we, nin pen kolu.
A: NONLIT Q person what talk sad. EXCL you more talk sad from all, while we.incl stand.
```
The rhetorical question is marked with **a**. «сам» and the emphatic «-то» become **ba** plus the contrast with the question.

```
C: Я не ною, я за рулём. Это разные вещи. Вы там сзади спите, а я пять часов в этой каше.
C: lon mu kegu bayi, lon wungu tale. tan yen ben mu gun kige. me la wopo mon nebe, un lon gun mon tale feli tan nin awuwa te.
C: I NOT talk sad, I guide car. this and that NOT be same. you PL sleep at back, but I be at car many this during hour 5.
```
«за рулём» = "guide the car". «в этой каше» (a mess, literally porridge) = "in these many cars"; the metaphor is lost.

```
B: Ну не пять, не преувеличивай.
B: mu te. tin mu muge ke kudu tan.
B: NOT 5. INSTR NOT make more big this.
```
Преувеличивать = "make this bigger" (causative **muge** + result + object).

```
C: Четыре с половиной в прошлый раз. Я засекал, между прочим.
C: mon gimu ben, awuwa da mimi te. lon sin sunbu muyi.
C: at occasion that, hour 4 POINT 5. I DONE count time.
```
The previous occasion is now established, so it is **gimu ben** "that occasion". The pointed «между прочим» ("for your information") has no equivalent.

```
A: Ты ещё таблицу заведи, честное слово.
A: a tin me wame tuko supulidusitu.
A: NONLIT INSTR you also start spreadsheet.
```
The sarcastic imperative is **a tin**. «ещё» ("next you'll even…") = **wame**, which keeps "also" and loses the escalation. The exasperated «честное слово» has no equivalent.

```
C: Смешно вам. Давайте тогда кто-нибудь другой поведёт, раз всё так просто.
C: me la bubo. gi tan, so ewo eni wungu tale, gi musu we gun bi bunle.
C: you PL laugh. because this, REQ person other guide car, because thing all be very easy.
```
Resentment in «смешно вам» rests on context alone. «раз всё так просто» is ironic, as in Russian, and unmarked. «кто-нибудь» (indefinite "someone or other") = **ewo eni** "another person".

```
B: Я бы повёл, если б у меня права не забрали.
B: lon wungu, don kon po go mu yidu layisansu se lon.
B: I guide, if CONF 0 anyone NOT take license from me.
```
Counterfactual **don kon po**. The impersonal «забрали» (the authorities took it) = generic **go**. **sin** is avoided because **mu sin** would read as "not yet".

```
A: Ну всё, всё, в восемь — и Серёге кофе с заправки за наш счёт. Договорились?
A: so pen bamo. fon pen kusa mon kefi po po, yen din bika [кофе] li en seloga mon pu bika petulalu. min pen tayu.
A: REQ we.incl stop. ADVISE we.incl leave at 8 0 0, and we.excl buy [coffee] for NAME Seryoga at place buy petrol. Q we.incl agree.
```
«всё, всё» = "let's stop". The repetition isn't doubled. «за наш счёт» = "we buy". «наш» is A + B, excluding Seryoga, who is one of the listeners; I chose **din** (excludes you) because the offer is aimed at him. Заправка = "place for buying petrol".

## Unclear rules

1. **Vocatives:** rule 1 lets only a linker, a scene-setting phrase or an if/when clause come before the speech-act particle. Where a name used in address goes ("Серёж, давай…") isn't stated, so I made it a separate fragment.
2. **Inclusive/exclusive "we" with several listeners:** when "we" includes one listener and excludes another (line 22), it isn't said whether **pen** or **din** applies.
3. **Stacking speech-act particles:** **a min** (rhetorical question) and **a tin** (sarcastic order). It isn't said whether **a** combines with another particle.
4. **Clock times before 10:00:** is 07:00 **oma po po** or **po oma po po**? Can a casual "at seven" be just **mon oma**?
5. **Emphasis / discourse particles** (же, ж, -то, ведь, между прочим): the tag **min** is the closest match, but it asks for confirmation instead of asserting shared knowledge. There is no stated way to mark "as you know".
6. **mu + sin:** **mu sin** is "not yet", so a negated past completed event ("hadn't taken") has no clear form.
7. **Relative clauses with a time gap** ("days when I don't work" = **dili la man lon mu doku**): it isn't said what role the head noun plays inside a **man** clause.
8. **Imperfective nuances** (general-factual past, annulled result) have only **sin**, **le** or nothing to map onto.
