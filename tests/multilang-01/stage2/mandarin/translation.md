# Apu Pi We translation (from Mandarin)

```
上周四夜里十一点多，梧桐苑6号楼的住户老周下楼倒垃圾，闻到楼道里有一股塑料烧焦的味儿。
1. mon dili pi 4 ya nelon 1, wo 23 00, en lawutowu man nume mon guyi pi 6 pi en wutunwen, isi deba li gitin ge gitin, yen kanko pulasutiku onti mon pu niba pi guyi.
AT day OF 4 BEFORE week 1, AFTER 23 00, NAME Lao-Zhou WHO live AT house OF 6 OF NAME Wutong-Yuan, go below FOR throw RESULT-throw, AND smell plastic burn AT PLACE-walk OF house.
```

上周四: anchor + offset applied to the weekday label (dili pi 4 ya nelon 1). 老周, 小林, 小何 are transliterated whole (names.py on Mandarin IPA), so 老/小 survive only as part of the name, not as the familiar age-marking it is. 夜里 left out: 23 00 already says it. 烧焦 rendered as burning (onti); the charred result is not marked.

```
他顺着味道找到二楼拐角，看见一辆电动车的充电器正冒着白烟，旁边的插线板已经烫得发黑。
2. wan lefan kanko tun pu duko pi [楼层] pi 2, yen nagu wun penso pinda le obu se du tadu pi ibayiku 1, yen wun pawuwa sutulipu man gun gelu, sin tikun tun lewe tago.
3SG follow smell TO PLACE-turn OF [floor] OF 2, AND see THAT smoke white ONGOING come FROM TOOL-charge OF e-bike 1, AND THAT power strip WHO BE near, DONE hot TO become black.
```

No word for a storey of a building: [楼层]. 烫得发黑 as tikun tun lewe tago ("hot to the point of turning black").

```
老周顾不上多想，一把拔掉插头，又跑回家端了一盆水出来。
3. en lawutowu mu ibe muyi li pume feli, nin foke 1 nunya pulagu se pawuwa sutulipu, yen sigo gabe tun guyi pi nen, obu dinyo ladon kudu 1 pi osa tun aban.
NAME Lao-Zhou NOT have time FOR think much, DURING moment 1 pull plug FROM power strip, AND return running TO house OF SELF, come carrying cup big 1 OF water TO outside.
```

盆 has no word; ladon kudu "big cup" is the nearest. 端…出来: obu (towards the scene) + tun aban.

```
刚要泼，对门的小林探出头喊了一声："叔，别泼水，先拿灭火器！"
4. nin wan win gitin osa, en suyawulin man nume mon sinye, kobi lukan tun aban, yen oyi dibon "faba pi wede. tin mu gitin osa, yidu du bamo lumi ya."
WHILE 3SG SOON throw water, NAME Xiao-Lin WHO live AT front, move head TO outside, AND say loud "sibling OF father. INSTRUCT NOT throw water, take TOOL-stop fire BEFOREHAND."
```

对门 approximated as "lives in front". 叔 rendered as the kin address faba pi wede; the respect is carried by the address word, and nun is left out because the shout is urgent (the doc says urgency needs no nun). The urgency of 「！」 itself has no marker: yo is for warnings stated as facts, and this is a prohibition (tin mu).

```
两人从一楼消防箱里拎出灭火器，对着充电器喷了几下，烟很快就散了。
5. ewo 2 yidu du bamo lumi se pu kuwi bu mon [楼层] pi 1, bone bu tun du tadu gimu uyo, yen penso kusa bi yobe.
person 2 take TOOL-stop fire FROM PLACE-keep IT AT [floor] OF 1, use IT TO TOOL-charge occasion few, AND smoke leave VERY fast.
```

消防箱 here as pu kuwi bu "where it is kept"; it is spelled out in 21. 喷 has no word; bone "use".

```
车主是三楼的租户小何，他下楼一看，脸都白了，连声道歉。
6. no ibe ibayiku gun en suyawuko, no sanwa mon [楼层] pi 3. wan obu deba yen miya, suku pi wan lewe bi pinda, yen sepi gimu feli.
AGENT-have e-bike BE NAME Xiao-He, AGENT-rent AT [floor] OF 3. 3SG come below AND look, face OF 3SG become VERY white, AND apologize occasion many.
```

From 6 to 7 小何 is the person being talked about, so wan = 小何; 老周 is off stage.

```
他说自己白天送外卖，晚上图省事，就从家里拉了根线下来充电。
7. wan oyi wun nin dili, sun doku dinyo pasu, yen nin yami, gi sun momu lite bunle, sun nunya wayiya 1 se guyi pi sun tun deba li tadu.
3SG say THAT DURING day, LOG work carrying food, AND DURING night, BECAUSE LOG want way easy, LOG pull wire 1 FROM house OF LOG TO below FOR charge.
```

```
第二天一早，物业经理带人挨家查看，发现整栋楼像这样"飞线"充电的还有四五户。
8. mon tuko pi leso pi dili man lefan, no wungu guyi, gan ewo in, miya pu nume we, 1 wo 1, yen geno wun mon guyi minwe, pu nume eni 4 lu 5 tadu fu tan, gan wayiya bina.
AT start OF morning OF day WHO follow, AGENT-lead house, WITH person SOME, look PLACE-live ALL, 1 AFTER 1, AND find THAT AT house whole, PLACE-live other 4 OR 5 charge LIKE THIS, WITH wire fly.
```

The scare quotes around 飞线 can't be kept (quotation marks are only for quoted speech), so the metaphor is said plainly (wayiya bina "flying wire") and its slang status is lost. 物业经理 = no wungu guyi "one who runs the building"; 物业 = no wungu guyi la.

```
其实小区东门早就装了二十个充电桩，只是不少人嫌远，又觉得扫码麻烦。
9. un, mon beya pi anyu pi funti pi guyi, tadu powintu 20 gun yaga ya muyi netu. un ewo to feli mu tana wun bu la gun lini, yen feme wun sukan kowudu gun batu.
BUT, AT door OF east OF group OF house, charge point 20 BE put BEFORE time long. BUT person RATHER many NOT like THAT IT PL BE far, AND feel THAT scan code BE problem.
```

其实 has no counter-expectation word; un "but" carries only the contrast.

```
业主群里有人说幸亏发现得早，也有人抱怨充电桩坏了两个一直没人修。
10. mon funti kegu pi no ibe pu nume la, ewo in oyi wun upe gun wun go geno batu ya. ewo eni oyi bonko wun tadu powintu 2 gun alun, yen nin muyi netu ewo fa muge upe bu la.
AT group talk OF AGENT-have PLACE-live PL, person SOME say THAT good BE THAT ANYONE find problem BEFOREHAND. person other say angry THAT charge point 2 BE break, AND DURING time long person NONE make good IT PL.
```

幸亏: there is no word for luck; upe gun wun "the good thing is that". 抱怨 as oyi bonko "say angrily", which is a bit stronger than the source.

```
物业当天下午就把坏的桩修好了，傍晚又在每个单元门口贴出了一份通知。
11. nin dili kige, wo 12 00, no wungu guyi la sin muge upe tadu powintu 2, yen mon kegin, wame yaga mabo 1 pi ge iko mon beya we pi guyi la.
DURING day same, AFTER 12 00, AGENT-lead house PL DONE make good charge point 2, AND AT evening, also put paper 1 OF RESULT-tell AT door ALL OF house PL.
```

下午 as wo 12 00. 单元门口 is not told apart from a building door.

```
各位业主、住户：近期本小区发生一起电动自行车在楼道内充电引发冒烟的事件，所幸未造成人员伤亡。
12. nun no ibe pu nume la yen no nume la. ya muyi eten, mon funti pi guyi tan, ilekutuliku bayisikalu 1 le tadu mon bese pi pu niba, yen tan muge obu penso. upe gun wun ewo fa sin wekun lu kono.
POLITE AGENT-have PLACE-live PL AND AGENT-live PL. BEFORE time short, AT group OF house THIS, electric bicycle 1 ONGOING charge AT inside OF PLACE-walk, AND THIS make come smoke. good BE THAT person NONE DONE pain OR die.
```

Register: the notice's 电动自行车 is the full loan ilekutuliku bayisikalu, and the narrative's colloquial 电动车 is ibayiku. 事件 as a formal noun frame is not kept. 伤亡 as wekun lu kono "be hurt or die".

```
为保障大家的生命财产安全，自即日起，严禁在楼道、门厅、电梯间及室内为电动自行车或电池充电。
13. din momu kuwi pata yen musu pi ewo we. gi tan, se mefa, tin mu mu tadu ilekutuliku bayisikalu lu batali mon pu niba, mon pu inwa, mon pu pi elibeyita, yen mon bese pi pu nume.
WE.EXCL want keep life AND thing OF person ALL. BECAUSE THIS, FROM today, INSTRUCT NOT NOT charge electric bicycle OR battery AT PLACE-walk, AT PLACE-enter, AT PLACE OF elevator, AND AT inside OF PLACE-live.
```

为保障… can't go before the speech-act particle, and at the end it would attach to the forbidden charging, so it became its own statement plus gi tan "so". 安全 has no word; it is folded into kuwi "keep". 严禁 = tin mu mu (instruction + emphatic NOT), against the plain tin nun of 请.

```
严禁从住宅窗户或门口私拉电线、插线板到楼下充电。
14. tin mu mu nunya wayiya lu pawuwa sutulipu se degun pi senmo lu beya pi pu nume tun deba li tadu, gan efon fa.
INSTRUCT NOT NOT pull wire OR power strip FROM glass OF wall OR door OF PLACE-live TO below FOR charge, WITH allow NONE.
```

窗户 has no word; degun pi senmo "glass of the wall". 私 as gan efon fa "without permission".

```
电动自行车请统一停放在东门车棚，并使用车棚内的智能充电桩充电。
```

The phone number is regrouped in threes from the right, as the Writing rule says (the source groups 4 + 4). 我们将 = de din: a commitment, with we excluding the readers.

安全 again folded into kuwi. 共同 as pen we "all of us"; pen (including the readers) against din in 13 and 18.

## Gaps and distinctions not kept

Bracketed gaps: [楼层] storey (4 times), [法] law (once).

Distinctions in the source that the translation can't carry: the slang marking of 「飞线」; 老/小 as age-and-familiarity address; 安全 "safety" as a notion of its own; the institutional 将 commitment with a third-person subject; the urgency of 「！」 in a prohibition; the counter-expectation of 其实; 事件 as a formal noun frame. Approximated with existing words: 盆, 窗户, 对门, 单元, 喷, 下午.

## Unclear rules

- Numbers in writing. The Writing section says digits; rules 9 and 11 write clock times and counts as words (fu sa sa po po). Digits are used here. Clock times can't use a colon, so they are written 23 00, which looks like the grouping of a single number. The phone number was regrouped in threes, though phone digits are read as an identifier.
- Anchor + offset with weekday labels and story time. "Last Thursday" and "next Monday" (dili pi 4 ya nelon 1, dili pi 1 wo nelon 1) follow the pattern but aren't exemplified. With no anchor the anchor is "now", so "the next day" in a narrative needed a relative clause (dili man lefan).
- Topic switch for wan. When a new person becomes the subject of several sentences (小何 in 6–7), it's unclear whether he becomes wan or stays yi.
- Purpose li-phrases. They may not come before the speech-act particle, and rule 15 doesn't say whether a final li-phrase attaches to the innermost clause.
- Relation phrases after an agent/place noun with a phrasal base. In no wungu guyi gan ewo in, the phrase could belong to the base or to the main verb. Commas were used to separate.
- la after no + verb + object. In no ibe pu nume la, it's unclear whether la makes the owners or the dwellings plural.
- de with a third-person subject. It's unclear whether an institution naming itself can use de.
- Vocatives. Their position relative to the speech-act particle isn't given, nor whether nun may stand before a bare address (12).
- gun "there is" after a fronted place phrase (21). The rule says "only at the very start".
- gi + noun phrase (gi lafi yen yema). Only gi tan is exemplified.
- Loans and the checker. check.py flags every loan (tadu, powintu, pawuwa sutulipu, ibayiku, …), although the borrowing rule allows them. names.py gives different forms for rhotic and non-rhotic English input (taludu / tadu). Non-rhotic was used, to match awuwa and kilamita.
