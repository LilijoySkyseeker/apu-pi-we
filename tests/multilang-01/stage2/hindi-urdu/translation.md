# Hindi–Urdu → Apu Pi We

Names via `tools/names.py`: वर्मा → **en waluma** (the tool maps Hindi ʋ to w), गुप्ता → **en guputa**, रमेश → **en lamesu**, पूजा → **en puda**. Loans via the same rules: truck → **tulaku**, transport → **tulansupolutu**, intern → **intalun**, driver → **dulayibalu**, hospital → **asupitalu**, glasses → **gulasasu**. Office is **pu pi mabo** (the reference's own example). Godown/warehouse is **pu kuwi** "place for keeping".

Address terms: सर → **no wungu** "one who leads" (role word used alone as address); मैडम → **no wungu futa**, gender stated on first mention with her name. A's आप is carried by **nun** on A's turns to B; B's तुम is the absence of **nun**.

```
A: सर, नमस्ते। आपके पास दो मिनट होंगे? कल वाली बात बतानी थी।
A: upe, no wungu. min nun me ibe minitu sa. lon momu iko me, musu man muwo afe.
A: good, one-who-leads. Q POLITE you have minute two. I want tell you, thing that happened yesterday.
```
होंगे (presumptive, softening the request) is carried by **nun** on the question; no confidence word added, since it isn't real doubt.

```
B: हाँ हाँ, आओ, बैठो। क्या हुआ कल? सुना है कुछ गड़बड़ हो गई थी।
B: ibe ibe, tin obu, goyu. yu muwo afe. fen batu in sin muwo.
B: have have, INSTRUCT come, sit. what happened yesterday. TOLD problem some DONE happen.
```
हाँ हाँ: no yes-word, so the verb of A's question is echoed, doubled for the eager repetition. सुना है → **fen** (hearsay).

```
A: जी, गड़बड़ तो नहीं कहूँगा, पर दिन बड़ा अजीब रहा। सुबह जब मैं ऑफ़िस पहुँचा तो वर्मा मैडम पहले से बैठी थीं, काफ़ी परेशान लग रही थीं।
A: nun, lon mu oyi "batu", un dili gun bi mu fu dili eni la. mon leso, nin lon liwon tun pu pi mabo, no wungu futa en waluma le goyu ya. o wan le feme to nuta.
A: POLITE, I not say "problem", but day was very not like day other PL. at morning, while I arrive to place of paper, one-who-leads woman NAME Verma ONGOING sit beforehand. SEEN she ONGOING feel rather fear.
```
अजीब has no root; built as "not like other days". पहले से → **ya** "beforehand". लग रही थीं (looked) → **o** (seen) + **le**. परेशान → "feel fear" (worry). The honorific plural बैठी थीं / लग रही थीं (respect for Verma as third person) has no carrier: **nun** marks politeness toward the listener only.

```
B: वर्मा जी? वो तो इतनी जल्दी आती ही नहीं। क्या बात थी?
B: min en waluma. wan mu mu obu ya fu tan. batu gun yu.
B: Q NAME Verma. she NOT NOT come beforehand like this. problem is what.
```
जी (B's respect for Verma) is lost, same reason. ही नहीं → **mu mu**. जल्दी = early has no root (**yobe** is speed); "beforehand like this".

```
A: उन्होंने बताया कि गुप्ता साहब का फ़ोन आया था, रात को ही। उनका माल जो परसों जाना था, वो अब तक गोदाम से निकला ही नहीं।
A: wan iko lon wun en guputa sin yebin ya, nin yami. musu la pi yi keya isi afe ya dili fi, un mu mu sin kusa pu kuwi.
A: she tell me that NAME Gupta DONE call beforehand, during night. thing PL of OTHER must go yesterday before day one, but NOT NOT DONE leave place keep.
```
साहब is lost (honorific for a third person; no role word fits, since it is respect, not a role). उनका → **yi**: Verma stays the topic (she is telling), Gupta is the one newly brought in. परसों = "yesterday before one day". अब तक … ही नहीं → emphatic **mu mu** + **sin** ("not yet"). Split into two clauses because a relative clause after **musu la pi yi** would attach to **yi**.

```
B: अरे! तो गोदाम में किससे बात की?
B: ba. gi tan, me kegu gan ewo yu pi pu kuwi.
B: EXCLAIM. because this, you talk with person what of place keep.
```
"Who at the godown" → **pi** (a bare place phrase would attach to the verb).

```
A: मैंने रमेश को फ़ोन लगाया, वो जो गोदाम में बैठता है, लंबा सा लड़का। दो बार तो उठाया ही नहीं, तीसरी बार में उठाया और बोला, भैया, ट्रक वाला ही नहीं आया।
A: lon yebin en lamesu, ewo man doku mon pu kuwi, lowu loyen to netu. gimu sa, wan mu mu febo. mon gimu pi ka, wan febo yen oyi "faba, ewo pi tulaku mu mu obu."
A: I call NAME Ramesh, person who work at place keep, man young rather long. occasion two, he NOT NOT answer. at occasion of three, he answer and say "sibling, person of truck NOT NOT come."
```
बैठता है (is stationed) → **doku** "works"; literal **goyu** would mean physically sitting. लड़का → "young man". भैया → **faba** as address; its warmth-plus-respect is reduced to plain "sibling". ट्रक वाला → "person of truck".

```
B: ट्रक वाला क्यों नहीं आया? उसका तो पक्का था न?
B: ewo pi tulaku mu obu gi yu. wan sin tayu, min.
B: person of truck not come because what. he DONE agree, Q(tag).
```
पक्का → "had agreed"; न? → tag **min**.

```
A: जी, पक्का था। पर पता चला उसकी बीवी की तबीयत ख़राब हो गई थी, अस्पताल ले गया था। किसी को बताया भी नहीं, बेचारा घबराया हुआ था।
A: nun, sin tayu. un fen, ofin futa pi wan sin lewe simi, yen wan isi gan yi tun asupitalu. wan mu mu iko go. wan gun banga, lon feme bayi li wan.
A: POLITE, DONE agree. but TOLD, spouse woman of him DONE become sick, and he go with OTHER to hospital. he NOT NOT tell anyone. he is panic, I feel sad for him.
```
पता चला → **fen** (learned from others). बीवी → **ofin futa** (gender stated once). भी नहीं → **mu mu**. बेचारा (speaker's pity) has no word; spelled out as "I feel sad for him", which makes it an explicit statement instead of a tone.

```
B: हम्म। चलो, वो तो समझ में आता है। फिर तुमने क्या किया?
B: mm. go lenu lafi ben. wo, me muge yu.
B: heard. anyone can understand that. after, you do what.
```
चलो (resigned "okay then") is folded into **mm**; no separate carrier.

```
A: मैडम बोलीं दूसरा ट्रक देखो। मैंने दो-तीन जगह फ़ोन किया, सबने मना कर दिया। तभी हमारी नई इंटर्न है ना, पूजा, उसने कहा कि उसके चाचा का ट्रांसपोर्ट का काम है, वो पूछ सकती है।
A: no wungu futa oyi "tin walo geno tulaku eni." lon yebin kemu sa lu ka, wan la we mu tayu. nin ben, pen ibe intalun duwa, min, en puda. wan oyi wun faba lowu pi wede pi sun ibe doku pi tulansupolutu, yen sun lenu mido yi.
A: one-who-leads woman say "INSTRUCT try find truck other." I call place two or three, they PL all not agree. during that, we.incl have intern new, Q(tag), NAME Pooja. she say that sibling man of father of LOGO have work of transport, and LOGO can ask OTHER.
```
देखो (look for) → "try to find". हमारी → inclusive **pen** (B runs the office). है ना → tag **min**. चाचा → "male sibling of father"; that he is the father's *younger* brother is lost. उसके / वो inside her report → **sun**; the uncle → **yi**.

```
B: अच्छा? वो छोटी सी लड़की, जो चश्मा लगाती है?
B: min feko. min futa loyen to meli ben man yunko gulasasu.
B: Q true. Q woman young rather small that who wear glasses.
```

```
A: जी, वही। मुझे लगा कुछ होगा नहीं, पर उसने फ़ोन किया और आधे घंटे में उसके चाचा ख़ुद आ गए, बुज़ुर्ग आदमी हैं, सफ़ेद दाढ़ी वाले।
A: nun, futa ben. lon pume wun musu fa muwo, un wan yebin, yen wo awuwa timon, faba lowu pi wede pi wan obu nen. wan gun lowu pani man ibe sonfa pinda pi suku.
A: POLITE, woman that. I think that thing none happen, but she call, and after hour half, sibling man of father of her come self. he is man old who have hair white of face.
```
वही echoed as the noun phrase; the emphatic ही is lost. ख़ुद → **nen** after an intransitive verb, so it can only be "himself, in person". The honorific plural for the uncle (आ गए, हैं) is lost. दाढ़ी → "hair of face".

```
B: ख़ुद आ गए? वाह। फिर?
B: min obu nen. ba upe. wo, yu muwo.
B: Q come self. EXCLAIM good. after, what happen.
```

```
A: उन्होंने कहा गाड़ी तो दे देंगे, पर किराया थोड़ा ज़्यादा लगेगा, ड्राइवर को रात भर चलाना पड़ेगा। मैंने मैडम से पूछा, मैडम बोलीं सर से पूछ लो, पर आपका फ़ोन बंद आ रहा था।
A: wan oyi "de din neke tulaku. un sanwa gun to ke kudu, gi dulayibalu keya isi nin yami minwe." lon mido no wungu futa, yen wan oyi "tin mido no wungu." un nun liga pi me le gun tapi.
A: he say "INTEND we.excl give truck. but rent is rather more big, because driver must go during night whole." I ask one-who-leads woman, and she say "INSTRUCT ask one-who-leads." but POLITE phone of you ONGOING is closed.
```
देंगे (plural/honorific "we") → **din** + **de** (commitment). चलाना → "go"; no verb for driving. बंद आ रहा था → "was closed" (Hindi uses the same idiom).

```
B: हाँ, मैं कल शादी में था, फ़ोन साइलेंट पर था। तो तुमने अपने से हाँ कर दी?
B: mm, afe lon gun mon lewe ofin, liga gun mange. gi tan, min na me sin tayu.
B: heard, yesterday I am at become spouse, phone is quiet. because this, Q only you DONE agree.
```
शादी → the act noun "becoming spouses" after **mon**. अपने से (on your own authority) → **na me** "only you". The completive कर दी → **sin**; its "decisively, without asking" flavour is partly lost.

```
A: जी सर, माफ़ी चाहता हूँ, पर मुझे लगा गुप्ता साहब नाराज़ हो गए तो उससे ज़्यादा नुक़सान होगा। इसलिए मैंने हाँ कह दिया।
A: nun, sin tayu, no wungu. nun nun sepi. un lon pume wun don en guputa lewe bonko, pen ibe batu ke kudu se tan. gi tan, lon tayu.
A: POLITE, DONE agree, one-who-leads. POLITE POLITE apologize. but I think that if NAME Gupta become angry, we.incl have problem more big from this. because this, I agree.
```
माफ़ी चाहता हूँ → **sepi** with doubled **nun** for extra deference. नुक़सान (loss, damage) → "a bigger problem"; **agi** is only "misplace".

```
B: कितना ज़्यादा माँगा उन्होंने?
B: wan momu ke nimo wifu yu.
B: he want more money number what.
```
B's honorific उन्होंने for the uncle is lost.

```
A: क़रीब चार हज़ार ऊपर। लेकिन सर, शाम को चाचा जी ने ख़ुद ही कहा कि बच्ची की वजह से कर रहे हैं, तो दो हज़ार कम कर देते हैं।
A: ke nimo fu da munno. un no wungu, mon kegin, wan oyi se nen "din le muge tan gi tiwu ben. gi tan, de din yidu sa munno se sanwa."
A: more money about four thousand. but one-who-leads, at evening, he say from self "we.excl ONGOING do this because child that. because this, INTEND we.excl take two thousand from rent."
```
चाचा जी (kin title + honorific) → plain **wan**. ख़ुद ही (of his own accord) → **se nen** "from himself". बच्ची → **tiwu** (the girl's gender is lost; **tiwu futa** would mean "daughter"). The currency is unstated, as in the original.

```
B: हा हा, ये तो अच्छा हुआ। और गुप्ता साहब को बताया?
B: [हा हा] ba, tan gun upe. yen min me iko en guputa.
B: [laugh] EXCLAIM, this is good. and Q you tell NAME Gupta.
```
[हा हा] is laughter, a sound outside any language, not a missing concept.

```
A: जी, रात को फ़ोन किया। पहले तो बहुत गुस्से में थे, बोले आप लोगों पर भरोसा करना ही ग़लत था। फिर उनकी बेटी ने फ़ोन ले लिया, आजकल सारा हिसाब-किताब वही देखती हैं। उन्होंने आराम से सुना और कहा कि आज दोपहर तक पहुँच जाए तो कोई दिक़्क़त नहीं। और सर, अभी आते वक़्त ड्राइवर का फ़ोन आया था, शहर में घुस गया है।
A: nun, iko. lon yebin wan mon yami. ya, wan gun bi bonko, yen oyi "ba, [भरोसा करना] me la gun gagan." wo, tiwu futa pi wan yidu liga. eda na wan muge sunbu we pi nimo. wan doma fanse, yen oyi wun don bu liwon mefa ya fi sa po po, ben mu gun batu. yen no wungu, nin lon obu, dulayibalu sin yebin lon. fen wan sin inwa oton.
A: POLITE, tell. I call him at night. first, he is very angry, and say "EXCLAIM, [trust] you PL is wrong." after, child woman of him take phone. now only she do count all of money. she listen calmly, and say that if it arrive today before 12 00, that not is problem. and one-who-leads, while I come, driver DONE call me. TOLD he DONE enter city.
```
भरोसा करना (trust) has no root and no composition from existing words, so it is bracketed. Gupta's आप लोग is formal distance in anger, not deference; adding **nun** would make him sound polite, so it is left out. ही (it was wrong *at all*) → **ba**. वही देखती हैं → **na wan** "only she"; the honorific plural for the daughter is lost. दोपहर (midday) has no word → clock time 12:00. अभी (just now) is only partly kept by "while I was coming". शहर में घुस गया है is the driver's report → **fen**.

```
B: शाबाश। फ़ैसला तुमने ठीक लिया, पर अगली बार मेरा फ़ोन न लगे तो घर पर फ़ोन कर देना, वो मुझे ढूँढ लेंगी। पूजा को मेरी तरफ़ से धन्यवाद बोलना, और उस ट्रक वाले के पैसे-वैसे मत काटना, पहले पता करो उसकी बीवी कैसी है।
B: ba, me muge ben upe. me waka denku, un mon gimu eni, don me mu lenu yebin lon, tin yebin guyi pi lon. yi lenu geno lon. tin iko en puda lota se lon, yen tin mu yidu nimo lu musu eni se ewo ben pi tulaku. ya, tin mido wun ofin pi wan gun fu yu.
B: EXCLAIM, you do that well. you choose correct, but at occasion other, if you not can call me, INSTRUCT call house of me. OTHER can find me. INSTRUCT tell NAME Pooja thanks from me, and INSTRUCT not take money or thing other from person that of truck. first, INSTRUCT ask that spouse of him is how.
```
वो … ढूँढ लेंगी: the feminine honorific verb tells a Hindi listener it is a woman at home (his wife, or another senior woman) and that she is respected. **yi** carries neither; adding **futa** or **ofin** would state more than the original says. पैसे-वैसे (echo word, "money and such") → "money or anything else". कर देना (soft future imperative) → plain **tin**. पता करो → "ask".

## Rules that were unclear

1. **Third-person honorifics.** **nun** marks politeness toward the listener only. Hindi marks respect for the person *talked about* (बैठी थीं, साहब, जी, आ गए, देखती हैं). There is no rule for this, and no word. Seven or more instances were dropped.
2. **Address without a name.** The reference shows a role word before a name (**no tofo en mala**). Is a role word alone (**no wungu** for "sir") fine as a vocative, and where does it go in the sentence?
3. **Apposition and fronted topics.** "Ramesh, the one who works at the godown, a tallish boy" and "our new intern, you know, Pooja, she said…" have no stated construction. A comma after a relative clause was used; the reference says nothing may follow a relative clause in its phrase, and it is unclear whether a comma ends that phrase.
4. **"Self" as an intensifier.** **nen** is defined as reflexive. Using it after an intransitive verb (**obu nen** "came in person") is inferred, not stated. After a transitive verb it would be read as an object, so "of his own accord" became **se nen**.
5. **Emphatic NOT plus "not yet".** Does **mu mu sin** "emphatically not yet" combine like this?
6. Bare **ke**. The reference gives **ke** + adjective. Whether **ke nimo** "more money" and a bare **ke** "more, extra" are allowed is not stated.
7. **gun "there is" inside a subordinate clause.** That reading is allowed only at the start of a sentence, so "no problem" inside a **wun** clause had to become **ben mu gun batu**.
8. **"Speech-act particle, then nun" after a linker.** **un nun liga…** follows "linker before particle" plus "nun first in a statement", but the combination isn't shown.
9. **Several fronted elements.** A scene-setting phrase, then a linker, then an if-clause, all before **tin** (turn 22). Each is allowed alone; stacking them isn't addressed.
10. **Where the obviative points inside reported speech.** In turn 11 Pooja is **sun** and her uncle is **yi**. Whether **wan** in a report means the report's topic or the conversation's topic is not stated.
11. **Hindi ʋ.** `names.py` maps ʋ to **w**. The written rule only says v → b; ʋ isn't listed.
