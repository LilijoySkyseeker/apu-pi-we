# Apu Pi We translation: community notice and reply (Spanish source)

Translated directly from `source.txt`, following `docs/language.md`. Each block: Spanish, Apu Pi We, word-by-word gloss. Glosses use: AGT = **no** (one who), THG = **ge** (thing done/made), TOOL = **du**, PLC = **pu** (place for), POL = **nun**, PL = **la**, we.EXCL = **din**, we.INCL = **pen**, GEN = **go** (anyone), DONE = **sin**, STILL = **le**, UNSPEC = **bo**, PART = **kin**, NONLIT = **a**, INTEND = **de**, REQ = **so**, Q = **min**, WH = **yu**.

Loans (run through `tools/names.py`): bicycle → **bayisikalu**, bike → **bayiku**, elevator → **elabeyitalu**, euro → **yulowu**. Numbers are written in digits (Writing section); clock times are spelled out digit by digit (see note on sentence 5).

Recurring choices: residents/neighbours **no nume gelu** "ones who live near"; owners **no ibe pu nume** "ones who have a dwelling"; meeting (junta) **fanmu** "gathering"; common areas **kemu pi we** "everyone's places"; concierge **no miya guyi** "one who watches the house", concierge's lodge **pu miya guyi**; key **du udin** "thing for locking"; roof terrace **kuwun** "roof"; landing (rellano) **sinye pi beya** "front of the door"; administration **no wungu guyi** "ones who lead the house"; one-off levy (derrama) **ge unfu gimu fi** "what is paid once"; living together (convivencia) **nume gan nen nen** "live with each other".

## Notice from the administration

```
1. Estimados vecinos: les comunicamos que, por acuerdo de la Junta de Propietarios celebrada el pasado 14 de septiembre, a partir del 1 de noviembre entrarán en vigor nuevas normas de uso de las zonas comunes.
1. no nume gelu la, nun, din iko me la wun wabun duwa la pi bone pi kemu la pi we tuko mon dunan pi 11, dili pi 1, gi fanmu pi no ibe pu nume sin tayu bu mon dunan pi 9, dili pi 14.
AGT live near PL, POL, we.EXCL tell you PL that rule new PL of use of place PL of all start at month of 11, day of 1, because gathering of AGT have PLC live DONE agree it at month of 9, day of 14.
```

Note: "Estimados" is carried by **nun** (the language has no polite pronouns or salutation formula). **din** keeps the administration's "we" apart from the residents. The "agreement of the meeting held on the 14th" became "because the meeting agreed it on the 14th": the date moves to the agreeing, which happened at that meeting. The reason clause goes last (heavy last) and, at the end, attaches to the innermost clause, "the rules start".

```
2. Queda terminantemente prohibido dejar bicicletas, carritos de bebé o cualquier otro objeto en el portal y en los rellanos.
2. go mu mu gun efon yaga bayisikalu, siwa pi tiwu meli, lu musu eni bo mon pu inwa yen mon sinye pi beya la.
GEN NOT NOT is allow put bicycle, cart of child small, or thing other UNSPEC at PLC enter and at front of door PL.
```

Note: "terminantemente prohibido" = emphatic NOT (**mu mu**) on "is allowed". The impersonal "queda prohibido" is kept as a statement about **go** (anyone), not a **tin** instruction to the reader. "Cualquier" = **bo** (unspecified). Under NOT, plain **lu** gives "neither … nor". "Rellanos" = "fronts of the doors": the language has no word for stairs or landing.

```
3. Los objetos que se encuentren en dichas zonas serán retirados por el conserje sin previo aviso y depositados en el trastero comunitario durante un plazo máximo de quince días.
3. no miya guyi yidu musu la mon kemu tan nin mu iko ya, yen yaga bu la mon pu kuwi pi we nin mu ke se dili 15.
AGT watch house take thing PL at place THIS while NOT tell beforehand, and put it PL at PLC keep of all during NOT more than day 15.
```

Note: **the passive with an agent can't be kept.** There's no passive voice and nothing moves, so "the objects will be removed by the concierge" becomes "the concierge takes the things". The objects lose their place as subject. "Que se encuentren en dichas zonas" uses rule 8: a bare place phrase after a noun attaches to the verb, giving "take things at these places", which says the same thing. **tan** = "dichas" (just mentioned). "Plazo máximo" = at most (**mu ke se**). The future is unmarked: sentence 1 already set the time.

```
4. Transcurrido dicho plazo, la comunidad no se hará responsable de su custodia.
4. wo muyi tan, funti pi no ibe pu nume mu keya kuwi bu la.
after time THIS, group of AGT have PLC live NOT must keep it PL.
```

Note: there's no word for "responsible/liable", so "won't take responsibility for keeping them" became "has no obligation to keep them" (**mu keya**). That keeps the deontic content but not the legal register.

```
5. El horario de uso de la azotea queda restringido de 10:00 a 20:00 horas, y para acceder a ella será imprescindible solicitar la llave en conserjería.
5. go gun efon bone kuwun na se fi po po po tun sa po po po, yen li isi tun bu, go keya mido du udin mon pu miya guyi.
GEN is allow use roof only from 1 0 0 0 to 2 0 0 0, and for go to it, GEN must ask TOOL lock at PLC watch house.
```

Note: "queda restringido" = **na** (only) over the range **se … tun …**. Clock times are labels read digit by digit (rule 9). They're spelled out because the Writing section's digit rule gives no way to write 10:00 (`:` isn't a mark, and `10 00` would look like a grouped number). "Imprescindible" = **keya** (obligation), with no further strengthening.

```
6. Asimismo, se recuerda que no está permitido tender ropa en las ventanas que dan a la fachada principal.
6. wame muge imu me la wun go mu gun efon muge kilan lonne mon degun pi senmo man gun mon sinye pi guyi.
also make remember you PL that GEN NOT is allow make dry clothes at glass of wall REL is at front of house.
```

Note: "se recuerda" is impersonal, so the subject is left out (rule 11) rather than said as **din**. Window = "glass of the wall" (there's no root). The relative clause attaches to the nearest noun, **senmo**, giving "the wall at the front of the house", which is the façade. "Principal" is folded into "front". "Tender ropa" = "make clothes dry".

```
7. Para sufragar la reparación del ascensor, se aprobó una derrama extraordinaria de 180 euros por vivienda, que se cargará en el recibo de diciembre.
7. li unfu muge upe elabeyitalu, sin tayu wun pu nume we unfu yulowu 180 gimu fi, yen yaga bu mon mabo pi unfu pi dunan pi 12.
for pay make good elevator, DONE agree that PLC live ALL pay euro 180 occasion one, and put it at paper of pay of month of 12.
```

Note: the subjects of "se aprobó" and "se cargará" are left out, which keeps the agentless passive. "Extraordinaria" = paid once (**gimu fi**). The sense of "outside the regular fees" is only implied. "Por vivienda" = "every dwelling pays 180", with the per-unit reading left to context (there is no "each"). Recibo = "paper of paying".

```
8. Rogamos a todos los residentes que respeten estas disposiciones en beneficio de la buena convivencia.
8. so nun, me la we lefan wabun tan, li nume upe gan nen nen.
REQ POL, you PL ALL follow rule THIS, for live good with self self.
```

Note: "rogamos" = request + **nun**. "A todos los residentes" (third person) becomes "all of you", since **me** is the only way to address someone. The deferential third-person distance is lost; **nun** carries the formality.

```
9. Para cualquier duda o aclaración, pueden dirigirse a la administración de lunes a viernes, de 9:00 a 14:00 horas.
9. don me la mu lugo bo lu momu lafi bo, nun, me la lenu kegu gan no wungu guyi se dili pi 1 tun dili pi 5, se mape po po tun fi da po po.
if you PL NOT know UNSPEC or want understand UNSPEC, POL, you PL can talk with AGT lead house from day of 1 to day of 5, from 9 0 0 to 1 4 0 0.
```

Note: "duda o aclaración" = "don't know something or want to understand something". "Pueden" is availability, so it's ability (**lenu**), not permission. The administration names itself in the third person, as in the Spanish. Monday–Friday = **dili pi 1 … dili pi 5** (weekday labels, rule 9).

```
10. Atentamente, la Administración de la Comunidad de Propietarios.
10. nun, no wungu funti pi no ibe pu nume.
POL, AGT lead group of AGT have PLC live.
```

Note: there's no letter-closing formula, so **nun** stands in for "Atentamente". The institution is described, not borrowed and not given **en** (it isn't a personal name). See ambiguity 3.

## Reply from a resident

```
11. Muy señores míos: les escribo como propietaria del 3.º B para mostrarles mi desacuerdo con la circular que nos dejaron ayer en el buzón.
11. no wungu guyi la, nun, lon tusi tun me la fu no ibe pu nume [3.º B], li wuye me la wun lon mu tayu gan mabo man me la yaga mon pu dunu ge sege pi din nin afe.
AGT lead house PL, POL, I write to you PL like AGT have PLC live [3.º B], for show you PL that I NOT agree with paper REL you PL put at PLC get THG send of we.EXCL during yesterday.
```

Note: "propietaria" is feminine. Gender is a modifier "stated once when it matters", and it doesn't matter here, so it's dropped. "Como" (in the capacity of) = **fu** "like/as". The flat label "3.º B" is a Spanish floor/door identifier with no rule for letters, so it's left in brackets. "Nos" = **din** (us residents, not you). "Muy señores míos" is a stiffer formula than "Estimados" but also gets one **nun**: the difference in coldness is lost. **nin afe** at the end attaches to the relative clause (the leaving), as intended. Circular = "paper"; mailbox = "place for getting what is sent".

```
12. Para empezar, yo no recuerdo haber votado nada de esto, entre otras cosas porque la junta se convocó con cuatro días de antelación y a una hora en la que casi todos estábamos trabajando.
12. ya, lon mu imu wun lon waka bo pi tan, yen ukun kin gun wun go yebin fanmu mon bu ya dili 4, yen fanmu gun mon awuwa man din fu we le doku.
first, I NOT remember that I choose UNSPEC of THIS, and reason PART is that GEN call gathering at it before day 4, and gathering is at hour REL we.EXCL about ALL STILL work.
```

Note: **the stressed "yo" (contrast: *I*, whatever others say) is lost.** There's no focus device, and **lon** is just "I". "Para empezar" = **ya** "first". "Entre otras cosas" = "some (not all) of the reasons" (**kin**). "Con cuatro días de antelación" = anchor + offset: **bu ya dili 4** "it minus four days". "Casi todos" = **din fu we** "us, about all", on the pattern noun + **fu** + quantity. Vote = **waka** "choose".

```
13. Lo de las bicicletas me parece, sinceramente, un despropósito.
13. lon pume feko wun wabun pi bayisikalu gun musu man mu ibe ukun.
I think true that rule of bicycle is thing REL NOT have reason.
```

Note: the fronted topic "lo de las bicicletas" can't be fronted (nothing moves), so it becomes the subject of the **wun** clause. "Sinceramente" = think truly (**pume feko**), which is a manner and not a separate speech-level aside. "Despropósito" = "a thing with no reason". "Me parece" isn't uncertainty, so there's no **kon**.

```
14. En este edificio no hay garaje ni cuarto de bicis, así que ¿dónde quieren que las metamos, en la bañera?
14. guyi tan mu ibe pu kuwi tale lu pu kuwi bayiku. gi tan, a me la momu wun din yaga bu la mon kemu yu, mon pu pensi gawo min.
house THIS NOT have PLC keep car or PLC keep bike. because THIS, NONLIT you PL want that we.EXCL put it PL at place WH, at PLC wash body Q.
```

Note: **the irony is marked with a ("not literal")** over the rhetorical question. The bathtub suggestion is a clause-final **min** tag ("…, in the bathtub, right?"). The speech-act particle has to open its sentence, so this is two sentences. The colloquial "bicis" is kept as the colloquial English loan "bike" (**bayiku**) next to "bicycle" (**bayisikalu**). Garage = "place for keeping cars". Bathtub = "place for washing the body".

```
15. Y que el conserje se lleve las cosas de los vecinos sin avisar no es una norma de convivencia, es buscarse un conflicto.
15. yen, don no miya guyi yidu musu pi no nume gelu nin mu iko ya, tan mu gun wabun pi nume gan nen nen, tan gun lite pi geno migi.
and, if AGT watch house take thing of AGT live near while NOT tell beforehand, THIS NOT is rule of live with self self, THIS is way of find fight.
```

Note: the subjunctive "que … se lleve" (the act as a hypothetical) = **don**. Then **tan** picks up the act, and the two parallel clauses carry "not X, it's Y". "Buscarse un conflicto" = "a way of finding a fight": the reflexive "asking for it" nuance is weakened.

```
16. Con la azotea pasa tres cuartos de lo mismo: en julio y agosto, a las ocho de la tarde todavía hace un calor que no se aguanta, y es justo a esa hora cuando a la gente le apetece subir.
16. wabun pi kuwun gun to kige. nin dunan pi 7 yen dunan pi 8, mon sa po po po, wunta le gun bi tikun, yen mon awuwa kige, ewo la momu isi tun kuwun.
rule of roof is rather same. during month of 7 and month of 8, at 2 0 0 0, air STILL is very hot, and at hour same, person PL want go to roof.
```

Note: the idiom "tres cuartos de lo mismo" is translated by sense: "rather the same" (**to kige**). **"Un calor que no se aguanta" is weakened to "very hot".** There is no root for bear/endure. **The cleft "es justo a esa hora cuando" is lost as a construction.** "Justo" survives as **awuwa kige** "the same hour", fronted as a scene-setting phrase. "Todavía" = **le** (still). There's no weather subject, so "air" (**wunta**) supplies one: **gun** without a subject means "there is" only at the very start of a sentence.

```
17. Si encima hay que pedir la llave en conserjería y el conserje se marcha a las tres, en la práctica la mitad de los vecinos no podrá subir nunca entre semana.
17. don wame go keya mido du udin mon pu miya guyi, yen no miya guyi kusa mon fi te po po, mon pata feko, timon pi no nume gelu lenu isi tun kuwun mon muyi fa nin dili pi doku.
if also GEN must ask TOOL lock at PLC watch house, and AGT watch house leave at 1 5 0 0, at life true, half of AGT live near can go to roof at time NONE during day of work.
```

Note: "encima" = **wame** over the condition. "A las tres" is 15:00 in context. "En la práctica" = "in real life". "Nunca" = **mon muyi fa** "at no time" (the doc's example), with no separate NOT. "Entre semana" = "on working days".

```
18. En cuanto a la derrama, nadie discute que el ascensor haya que arreglarlo, pero sí tenemos derecho a que nos expliquen por qué cuesta lo que cuesta.
18. ewo fa mido wun min go keya muge upe elabeyitalu, un me la keya muge lafi din wun ge unfu gimu fi gun kudu fu tan gi yu.
person NONE ask that Q GEN must make good elevator, but you PL must make understand we.EXCL that THG pay occasion one is big like THIS because WH.
```

Note: the concession is kept with **un**. **The emphatic affirmative "sí tenemos" is lost** (no focus device; **bi** and **ba** would say "very" or exclaim). **"Tenemos derecho a" has no word for "right"**, so it's turned into the other party's obligation, "you must explain to us". The deontic content is kept, but the frame of "our entitlement" is lost. The topic opener "en cuanto a la derrama" can't be fronted (only time/place phrases can), so the levy becomes the subject of the indirect question. "Nadie discute que" = "nobody asks whether". "Cuesta lo que cuesta" = "is as big as this".

```
19. Les pido que nos enseñen al menos dos o tres presupuestos antes de cobrarnos un solo euro.
19. so me la wuye din pa wifu sa lu ka pi nimo, ya me la dunu yulowu fi se din.
REQ you PL show we.EXCL at.least number 2 or 3 of money, before you PL get euro 1 from we.EXCL.
```

Note: "pido" is a plain request, so it gets **so** without **nun**, unlike the administration's **so nun** in sentence 8. Presupuestos = "numbers of money" (price figures), and the sense "quotes from competing contractors" comes only from context. **"Un solo euro" (not even one) loses its emphasis.** Under "before", **yulowu fi** already means any money at all, but **na** "only" would mislead.

```
20. No creo que sea mucho pedir, teniendo en cuenta que la última reparación se hizo hace apenas dos años y ya estamos otra vez igual.
20. lon mu pume wun din mido bi feli, gi go sin muge upe elabeyitalu na ya fawu sa, yen eda pen moti gun kige.
I NOT think that we.EXCL ask very many, because GEN DONE make good elevator only before year 2, and now we.INCL again is same.
```

Note: "no creo" is understatement, not real doubt, so there's no **kon**. "Hace dos años" = **ya fawu sa** (no anchor, so the anchor is now). "Apenas" = **na**. "La última" is left implicit. "Estamos otra vez igual" = **pen** (the whole community, including the administration). Spanish "nosotros" doesn't mark this, so I had to choose. "Ya" (already) is carried by **eda** "now".

```
21. Por todo ello, solicito formalmente que estos puntos se vuelvan a votar en una junta extraordinaria, convocada con tiempo y en horario de tarde.
21. gi tan we, so nun, go moti waka wabun tan mon kegin, mon fanmu eni man go yebin ke ya.
because THIS ALL, REQ POL, GEN again choose rule THIS at evening, at gathering other REL GEN call more beforehand.
```

Note: **"formalmente" (officially, for the record) has no word.** **nun** gives the polite register only. The passive "se vuelvan a votar" = generic subject **go**. "Extraordinaria" = "another" meeting. "Con tiempo" = **ke ya** "more beforehand", which picks up the four-day complaint. The relative clause has to end the sentence, so "en horario de tarde" moves before it.

```
22. Quedo a la espera de su respuesta y, mientras tanto, mi bicicleta seguirá donde ha estado los últimos seis años: en mi rellano, sin molestar a nadie.
22. nun, lon sefu febo pi me la. yen nin tan, de bayisikalu pi lon le piyi mon kemu kige fu nin fawu 6, mon sinye pi beya pi lon, nin bu muge bonko ewo fa.
POL, I wait answer of you PL. and during THIS, INTEND bicycle of I STILL stay at place same like during year 6, at front of door of I, while it make angry person NONE.
```

Note: the defiant future ("seguirá") is made explicit as the speaker's intention (**de**). The Spanish implies it through a plain future. "Donde ha estado los últimos seis años" = "the same place as during six years". "Molestar" = "make angry", which is somewhat stronger. The formal closing gets **nun**, and this is two sentences because **de** must open its own.

## Rules I found ambiguous

- Vocatives. No rule says where a form of address goes, or how it orders with **nun**, which must come "first in a statement". I put the address first, then **nun** (1, 11).
- Numbers in writing vs. the examples. The Writing section says numbers are written in digits, but the newest examples (rule 11, 2026-10-10) spell out clock times (**sa sa po po**), and no written clock format exists. I used digits for counts and dates, and words for clock times. Also, rule 9 says months are "read digit by digit", but its own example is **dunan pi yawu**, a quantity reading. With digits in writing this didn't arise here, but it would when reading aloud (**dili pi 14**: "fi da" or "yawu da"?).
- Institutions: borrow or describe? The Words section says institutions are borrowed from English; Word building says government "composed fine". I described "Administración", "Junta" and "Comunidad de Propietarios".
- Topic fronting. "Nothing moves", and only time/place phrases may open a sentence. It's unclear whether a topic like "en cuanto a X" / "con la azotea" may open as a scene-setting phrase (I avoided it in 13 and 18).
- Relative clauses with an object or location gap (**mabo man me la yaga**, **fanmu man go yebin**). All the doc's examples are subject gaps.
- a + question. Whether the non-literal particle can combine with a **yu** question to make a rhetorical question, and whether **a** and a final **min** tag can coexist (14).
- "any" (bo). **bo** is "unspecified" as an operator. Using it as "any/anything" after a noun or as an object (2, 9, 12) is my extension.
- Speech-act particle mid-sentence. Only linkers, scene phrases and if/when clauses may precede it, so a Spanish sentence that turns into a question, a rhetorical aside or an intention halfway through has to be split (14, 22).
- la with tan/pi. The relative order of plural **la** and other determiners or **pi** phrases after a multi-word noun (**kemu la pi we**, **no nume gelu la**) isn't specified.
- "before/after" + offset with an explicit anchor (**bu ya dili 4** "four days before it"). Rule 9 shows anchor + offset only with time words (**wuma wo dili fi**). I assumed any noun can be the anchor.
