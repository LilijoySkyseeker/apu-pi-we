## Other losses (including in sentences scored 2)

- **Legal or administrative register flattened across the notice** (L/T mix): "dichas", "disposiciones", "sufragar", "transcurrido dicho plazo", "queda restringido", "imprescindible" all came back in plainer wording.
- **4**: "no se hará responsable de su custodia" (liability language) → "no estará obligada a conservarlos". Same deontic content, but no longer a liability disclaimer (L, no word for "responsible").
- **2, 22**: "rellanos/rellano" → "delante de las puertas / de mi puerta" (L, no word for stairs or landing). Close, but not the same space.
- **5**: "imprescindible" → "habrá que", slightly weaker.
- **6**: impersonal "se recuerda" → "Les recordamos" (subject supplied by back-translator, B, minor). "principal" dropped but carried by "fachada".
- **7**: "derrama extraordinaria" → "derrama única". The "outside the regular fees" sense becomes "one-off" (translator's choice, noted).
- **8**: deferential third person "a todos los residentes" → second person "a todos" (L, **me** is the only address form).
- **9**: "la administración" (an office) → "el administrador" (a person). Related to the 10/11 confusion.
- **11**: "Muy señores míos" (cold, stiff) → "Estimados señores" (L, one **nun** for both). The writer's gender ("propietaria") was dropped by the translator and recovered only by the back-translator's guess.
- **12**: past "estábamos" → present "estamos". The "le" (still) shading is gone.
- **13**: fronted topic "Lo de las bicicletas" flattened. "despropósito" (exasperated, colloquial) → "no tiene ninguna justificación" (cooler, argumentative).
- **14**: colloquial "bicis" → "bicicletas" (register). "bañera" → "ducha" ("place for washing the body"). The absurd image is slightly different, but the sarcasm survived.
- **15**: reflexive "buscarse un conflicto" (asking for trouble) → "buscar pelea" (translator noted).
- **16**: "un calor que no se aguanta" → "muchísimo calor" (L, no root for bear/endure). The colloquial idiom "tres cuartos de lo mismo" → "más o menos lo mismo" (tone flattened).
- **17**: "encima" (exasperated "on top of that") → "además", which is less exasperated.
- **20**: "la última reparación" left implicit. "ya estamos otra vez igual" came back vague (back-translator flagged it).
- **21**: "junta extraordinaria" (a specific type of meeting under Spanish horizontal-property law) → "otra junta" (T, minor).
- **22**: deadpan defiance (plain future with the bicycle as subject: "mi bicicleta seguirá donde ha estado") → explicit "pienso dejar mi bicicleta". The defiance survived, but the understatement and irony of letting the bike "speak" were lost. "sin molestar a nadie" is ambiguous between fact and condition (back-translator flagged it).

## Summary

The notice's facts, rules, dates, amounts and prohibitions all survived, and so did the reply's complaints, its sarcasm and its defiance. Five sentences lost a real part of their sense, mostly to structural gaps (no passive, no focus marking, no "right" or "formally"), plus one translator error that turned the Administration into the president and one back-translation misreading of **fu**. What drained away throughout was register: the notice's legal-administrative stiffness, the reply's colder salutation, and its colloquial exasperation ("bicis", "despropósito", "un calor que no se aguanta", "encima") were flattened into neutral, polite prose.
