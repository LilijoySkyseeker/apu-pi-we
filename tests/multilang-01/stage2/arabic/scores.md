## Other losses (in turns scored 2)

- **4, يا ساتر عليك → ربنا يكفينا شرّك**: the divine epithet ("the Concealer") is gone, and the replacement is a sharper jab than the original mock-exasperation. (L: no slot for religious exclamations)
- **6, طب**: the "OK then" discourse opener is lost. (L)
- **9, ماشي ماشي**: the grudging, doubled concession became plain agreement. The doubling rule forbids copying it, and the translator didn't try **mm**. (L, arguably T) The "but you pay" clause keeps most of the reluctance.
- **11, كنت عايز**: the polite past-tense softening is lost and only **nun** carries it. (L)
- **11/15/19, أستاذ → فندم**: the back-translator raised Karim's register. The translator's one-**nun**/two-**nun** grade didn't survive, because **nun** can't name which title. (L, with some B)
- **16, يا حبيبي → يا صاحبي**: the clerk's warm, paternal address became a peer's "buddy". The older-to-younger relationship is weaker. (L: no endearment convention)
- **20, إيه**: the "so, what's up" opener is lost. (L)
- **21, يا ستي**: the teasing "my lady" became the neutral يا صاحبتي. (L)
- **Method note:** **[كشري]** (7, 8) and **[النسر]** (16) were left in Arabic script inside the Apu Pi We text, so the back-translator saw source words. Those two items' survival says nothing about the language.

## Summary

Apu Pi We carried the propositional content, the speech acts (requests, thanks, blessings, rhetorical questions) and the coarse politeness gradient of this dialogue almost perfectly, reaching 44/46 with both point losses due to missing means (pessimism, the mirative "turned out"). The real erosion is in social texture: address terms flattened (أستاذ/فندم merged, حبيبي and ستي neutralised), discourse particles dropped, and religious exclamations swapped. Every case traces to the gaps already listed in gaps.md, not to translator or back-translator error.
