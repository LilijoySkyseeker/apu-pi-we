## 4. Summary

The back-translation keeps almost all the content and, surprisingly, most of the register: the rough mày/tao banter between roommates and A's dạ…ạ deference to the landlord were rebuilt from **nun nun**, kin vocatives and context, giving 42/44. The real damage is in the fine grain of address and tone: bác became chú, con became cháu, and warm or take-note particles (nha, đó, nè, hả) were lost or flipped. The only two content errors are an ambiguous "after time" (turn 6, B) and "every night" weakened to "all night" (turn 21, T).
