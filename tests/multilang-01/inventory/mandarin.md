# Mandarin inventory: register distinctions vs Apu Pi We

Sources read: `docs/language.md`, `data/grammar.tsv`, `data/lexicon.tsv`, `data/compounds.tsv` (nothing else).

**Column 3:** *must* = grammatically or socially required in that register; *habit* = the normal, expected choice; *occasional* = available and meaningful but not expected.

**Verdicts:**
- **yes**: a grammar word, rule or ordinary phrasing carries the same meaning (it may cost more words; the cost is noted).
- **partly**: an existing device carries the core meaning but loses a component (an attitude, a presupposition, a register signal, scope), or covers only some uses.
- **no**: nothing in the grammar or lexicon carries it. The speaker can only state the attitude outright as content, or the distinction collapses.

**Gloss abbreviations:** SFP sentence-final particle · CL classifier · PFV perfective 了 · CRS currently-relevant-state 了 · EXP experiential 过 · DUR durative 着 · PROG progressive 在 · BA 把 · BEI 被 · FOC focus 是 · NMLZ 的 (nominaliser) · ATTR 的 (attributive) · RDP reduplication · HON honorific · Q question particle.

## 1. Intimate / casual (friends, family)

| # | Distinction | Must/habit/occasional | Example (original + gloss) | Apu Pi We | How expressed, or what is lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | 吧 *ba*: suggestion (decision shared or left to listener) | habit | 我们走吧。 *wǒmen zǒu ba* — we go SFP "let's go" | yes | **so pen** "let's" (request: we): **so pen isi.** For "you might want to…", **fon** (advise; the decision stays with the listener). |
| 1.2 | 吧 *ba*: conjecture asking for confirmation | habit | 你饿了吧？ *nǐ è le ba* — you hungry CRS SFP "you're hungry, I take it?" | yes | Confidence plus a clause-final tag: **kon oma me momu nawi, min.** "7 in 10 you want to eat, right?" (No root for "hungry", so "want to eat".) |
| 1.3 | 呢 *ne*: follow-up question "and you?" | habit | 我喝水，你呢？ *wǒ hē shuǐ, nǐ ne* — I drink water, you SFP | yes | Rule 11 ellipsis: **lon koye osa. min me.** |
| 1.4 | 呢 *ne*: musing, "I wonder" wh-question | habit | 他去哪儿了呢？ *tā qù nǎr le ne* — he go where PFV SFP "where could he have gone?" | partly | **min** always asks the listener. Musing can only be said as ignorance: **lon mu lugo wun wan sin isi tun kemu yu.** "I don't know where he went". The "half-addressed to self, no answer expected" force is lost. |
| 1.5 | 啊/呀 *a/ya*: softening, liveliness, friendliness | habit | 你去哪儿啊？ *nǐ qù nǎr a* — you go where SFP.SOFT | partly | **nun** softens but adds deference, which is wrong between friends. There is no casual softener; the bare **me isi tun kemu yu.** sounds flatter. |
| 1.6 | 嘛 *ma*: "this is obvious / as you should know", mild reproach | habit | 我早就说了嘛。 *wǒ zǎo jiù shuō le ma* — I early already say PFV SFP "I told you so" | partly | The tag **min** appeals to shared knowledge: **lon sin oyi bu ya, min.** "I said it beforehand, right?" Obviousness and reproach are lost. |
| 1.7 | 呗 *bei*: resigned or dismissive "that's all there is to it" | habit | 不想去就别去呗。 *bù xiǎng qù jiù bié qù bei* — not want go then don't go SFP "then just don't go (no big deal)" | no | Content only: **don me mu momu isi, fon mu isi.** No device for the shrug. |
| 1.8 | 哦/喔 *o*: friendly reminder or notice | habit | 别忘了哦！ *bié wàng le o* — don't forget CRS SFP | partly | Choosing **so** (request) over **tin** (instruct) is gentler: **so mu yakin.** **yo** (warn) is too strong. The warm "just reminding you" tone is lost. |
| 1.9 | 太…了 / 啦: exclamation of delight | habit | 太好了！ *tài hǎo le* — too good CRS "great!" | yes | **ba bi upe.** (exclaim, very good) |
| 1.10 | Interjections with fixed emotions: 哎呀 dismay, 哇 wonder, 唉 sigh, 哼 contempt, 嗯 assent, 啊? surprise | habit | 哎呀，我忘了！ *āiyā, wǒ wàng le* — INTJ.DISMAY I forget PFV | partly | Only **mm** "heard", **huh?** "say again", and **ba** (exclaim, emotion unspecified): **ba lon sin yakin.** Dismay, delight, contempt and resignation all fall together under **ba**, unless the feeling is named (**bayi**, **bonko**). |
| 1.11 | Echo answers; A-not-A neutral questions | must | 你去不去？去。 *nǐ qù bu qù? qù* — you go not go? go | yes | Same strategy: **min me isi. isi.** / **mu isi.** (rule 4). |
| 1.12 | Tag questions 对吧 / 好不好 / 是不是 | habit | 明天见，好不好？ *míngtiān jiàn, hǎo bu hǎo* — tomorrow see, good not good | yes | Clause-final **min**: **nagu me wuma, min.** The pleading or irritated 好不好 ("stop it, OK?!") has no counterpart. |
| 1.13 | Pro-drop of subject and object | habit | 吃了吗？吃了。 *chī le ma? chī le* — eat PFV Q? eat PFV | yes | Rule 11: **min sin nawi. sin nawi.** |
| 1.14 | Topic–comment: object fronted as topic | habit | 这个菜我不爱吃。 *zhège cài wǒ bù ài chī* — this CL dish I not love eat | partly | Nothing moves (principle 2): **lon mu tana nawi pasu tan.** A fragment plus a resumptive pronoun approximates it: **pasu tan. lon mu tana bu.** The marking of a non-subject as topic within one clause is lost. |
| 1.15 | Sortal classifiers obligatory with numerals and demonstratives | must | 三本书，一只狗，一条鱼 *sān běn shū, yì zhī gǒu, yì tiáo yú* — three CL.volume book, one CL.animal dog, one CL.long fish | yes | Noun + number: **ingo ka**, **totu fi**, **woga fi**. Only redundant shape-class information is lost. (For honorific 位, see 2.14.) |
| 1.16 | Measure words for units of mass nouns | must | 一杯水，一张纸 *yì bēi shuǐ, yì zhāng zhǐ* — one cup water, one sheet paper | yes | **ladon pi osa** "cup of water"; **mabo fi** "one paper". |
| 1.17 | Diminishing 点儿 / 一下 ("a bit") | habit | 吃点儿东西吧。 *chī diǎnr dōngxi ba* — eat bit thing SFP | yes | **fon nawi pasu uyo.** "(suggest) eat a little food". |
| 1.18 | Verb reduplication (delimitative, casual, softening) | habit | 你尝尝。 *nǐ cháng chang* — you taste-RDP "have a little taste" | partly | **fon walo tenmi bu.** "try tasting it" or **tenmi bu nin foke** "for a moment". The casual minimising tone is lost. Doubling in Apu Pi We *strengthens* (principle 5), so copying the form would invert the meaning. |
| 1.19 | Adjective reduplication (vivid, affectionate description) | habit | 红红的脸 *hóng-hóng de liǎn* — red-RDP ATTR face "nice rosy face" | partly | **suku bi wiwi** "very red face". Intensity is kept; the affectionate vividness is lost. |
| 1.20 | Sentence-final 了: change of state / new situation | must | 下雨了！ *xià yǔ le* — fall rain CRS "it's started raining" | yes | **gegi sin tuko.** "rain has started", or **lewe** "become": **wan lewe simi** "she's got sick". |
| 1.21 | Verbal 了: completed event | habit | 我买了三个苹果。 *wǒ mǎi le sān ge píngguǒ* — I buy PFV three CL apple | yes | **lon sin bika tanun ka.** |
| 1.22 | 过: experiential ("have ever…"; implies the state is over) | habit | 你去过北京吗？ *nǐ qù guo Běijīng ma* — you go EXP Beijing Q | partly | **min me sin isi tun en beyidin mon muyi in.** "…at some time". Sense kept. The implication that the past state no longer holds (我胖过 "I used to be fat") must be said outright. |
| 1.23 | 在/着: progressive and resulting state | habit | 他在睡觉。门开着。 *tā zài shuìjiào. mén kāi zhe* — he PROG sleep. door open DUR | yes | **wan le wopo.** / **beya gun benbi.** (**gun** + verb = resulting state) |
| 1.24 | 咱们 inclusive vs 我们 exclusive "we" | habit (north) | 咱们走吧 / 我们先走了 *zánmen zǒu ba / wǒmen xiān zǒu le* — we.INCL go SFP / we.EXCL first go CRS | yes | **pen** vs **din**: **so pen isi.** / **din isi ya.** It's obligatory in Apu Pi We, more systematic than Mandarin. |
| 1.25 | Kin terms: elder/younger, paternal/maternal | must | 我哥；我舅舅 *wǒ gē; wǒ jiùjiu* — I elder.brother; I mother's.brother | yes | Compositional: **faba lowu ke pani pi lon** "my older brother"; **faba lowu pi mole** "mother's brother". It takes 4–6 words for one syllable in Mandarin. **pani** is "aged or worn". |
| 1.26 | Kin terms as address, within family and to strangers (哥, 阿姨, 大哥) | habit | 哥，等等我！ *gē, děngdeng wǒ* — elder.brother, wait-RDP me | partly | Address is a name or a role word (**en …**, **mole**). There's no short vocative for "big brother", and no fictive-kin convention for addressing strangers (阿姨 "auntie" to a shop worker). |
| 1.27 | Familiar name forms: 小X / 老X / reduplicated names (婷婷) | habit | 小王，过来！ *Xiǎo Wáng, guò lái* — little Wang, cross come | no | Only the full name with **en**: **en wanfan. so obu.** No diminutive or relative-age familiar forms, and doubling a name would mean "strengthen". |
| 1.28 | Endearments 宝贝 / 亲爱的 | occasional | 宝贝，晚安。 *bǎobèi, wǎn'ān* — treasure, evening-peace | partly | **upe yami** works as "good night" (greeting pattern **upe** + time of day). No endearment word; only a description like **ewo man lon tana**, which is not an address term. |
| 1.29 | Vulgar intensifiers and swearing | occasional | 他妈的，又堵车了！ *tā mā de, yòu dǔ chē le* — his mother ATTR, again block car CRS "damn, traffic again!" | no | No vulgar register: **ba bi yomo, tale la moti bamo.** The transgressive force, which marks intimacy or exasperation, is absent. |
| 1.30 | Teasing irony | occasional | 你可真行啊！ *nǐ kě zhēn xíng a* — you indeed really capable SFP "well, aren't you clever" | yes | **a** (not literal): **a me bi gole.** |
| 1.31 | Omitting 请/谢谢/您 among intimates (politeness formulas sound distant) | habit | 帮我拿一下。 *bāng wǒ ná yíxià* — help me take a.bit | yes | Routines are optional and **nun** is opt-in, so leaving them out reads as plain, not rude: **so dinyo bu tun lon.** |

## 2. Hierarchical (to a boss, elder or official)

| # | Distinction | Must/habit/occasional | Example (original + gloss) | Apu Pi We | How expressed, or what is lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | 您 vs 你: honorific second-person pronoun | must | 您请坐。 *nín qǐng zuò* — you.HON please sit | partly | **me** for everyone; deference goes on the speech act: **so nun goyu.** Lost: deference attached to the *person* in every mention, independent of the speech act (e.g. a plain statement mentioning 您 three times). |
| 2.2 | Title + surname address (王经理, 李老师) | must | 王经理，您找我？ *Wáng jīnglǐ, nín zhǎo wǒ* — Wang manager, you.HON seek me | yes | Role word before the name: **no wungu en wanfan. min nun me momu kegu gan lon.** It needs the full name with **en**; there's no surname-only address convention. |
| 2.3 | Title alone as address (老师！) | habit | 老师，我有个问题。 *lǎoshī, wǒ yǒu ge wèntí* — teacher, I have CL question | yes | **no tofo. lon ibe ge mido.** |
| 2.4 | Generic honorific address for unknown adults and elders (师傅, 先生, 女士, 大爷, 阿姨, 叔叔) | habit | 师傅，去机场。 *shīfu, qù jīchǎng* — master, go airport (to a taxi driver) | partly | Role words can be built (**no** + verb), but no conventional respectful term for "sir/madam/elder" exists. Age-based respect (大爷 "grandpa") is lost. |
| 2.5 | Honorific marking of the listener's or a third party's things and kin (贵, 令, 尊, 他老人家) | habit | 贵公司；令尊 *guì gōngsī; lìngzūn* — honourable company; your-honourable father | no | **funti pi me**, **wede pi me**. **nun** scopes over the speech act, not a noun phrase, so a referent can't be honoured. |
| 2.6 | Humble self-reference and humble forms (敝, 寒舍, 拙见, 晚辈, 学生 as "I") | habit | 这是我的拙见。 *zhè shì wǒ de zhuōjiàn* — this is I ATTR clumsy.view | no | **ben gun gason pi lon.** "That's my idea." No humbling device; adding **meli** "small" would actually describe the idea as small. |
| 2.7 | Honorific vs plain lexical pairs (光临/来, 贵姓/姓什么, 拜读/读) | habit | 您贵姓？ *nín guìxìng* — you.HON honourable.surname "may I ask your name?" | partly | **min nun suso pi me gun yu.** Deference is kept through **nun**; the lexical honorific is lost. |
| 2.8 | 请 + verb polite request | must | 请坐。 *qǐng zuò* — please sit | yes | **so nun goyu.** |
| 2.9 | Graded deference (请 → 敬请 → 恳请) | habit | 恳请领导批准。 *kěnqǐng lǐngdǎo pīzhǔn* — earnestly.request leader approve | yes | Repeat **nun**: **so nun nun nun no wungu efon bu.** |
| 2.10 | Requests as ability questions (能不能 / 可不可以) | habit | 您能不能看一下？ *nín néng bu néng kàn yíxià* — you.HON can not can look a.bit | yes | **min nun me lenu miya bu.** The doc expects speakers to keep doing this (watch list). |
| 2.11 | Apology or thanks for imposition (不好意思, 麻烦您了) | habit | 麻烦您了。 *máfan nín le* — trouble you.HON CRS | yes | **sepi** or **lota** with **nun**: **nun, lota.** The thanks/apology blend of 麻烦您了 forces a choice of one. |
| 2.12 | 一下 minimiser on requests to superiors | habit | 您看一下这个。 *nín kàn yíxià zhège* — you.HON look a.bit this | partly | **so nun miya tan.** Brevity can be stated (**nin foke** "for a moment"), but it's not a conventional softener. |
| 2.13 | 吧 to soften a suggestion upward | habit | 您先走吧。 *nín xiān zǒu ba* — you.HON first go SFP | yes | **fon nun me isi ya.** (**fon** leaves the decision with the listener.) |
| 2.14 | Honorific classifier 位 vs plain 个 | must | 三位老师 *sān wèi lǎoshī* — three CL.HON teacher | partly | **no tofo ka**. Only sentence-level **nun**; the referent can't be honoured (see 2.5). |
| 2.15 | Hedged disagreement upward (我个人觉得 / 恐怕 / 是不是…) | habit | 恐怕不太合适。 *kǒngpà bú tài héshì* — fear not too suitable "I'm afraid it's not quite right" | yes | **nun, lon pume wun kon oma ben mu gun upe.** Watch list: **kon** used as politeness would blur it. |
| 2.16 | Acknowledging orders (好的 / 是 / 收到 / 明白) | must | 好的，我马上去办。 *hǎo de, wǒ mǎshàng qù bàn* — good NMLZ, I at.once go handle | yes | **mm** (heard), an echo (understood), **de** + verb (will do): **de muge bu eda.** |
| 2.17 | Acknowledging a superior's effort (您辛苦了) | habit | 您辛苦了。 *nín xīnkǔ le* — you.HON toil CRS | partly | **nun, lota. me sin doku bi.** Thanks and the effort can be stated; the ritual formula is lost. |
| 2.18 | Ritual deflection of praise (哪里哪里, 过奖了) | habit | 哪里哪里。 *nǎlǐ nǎlǐ* — where where "oh, not at all" | partly | **nun, lota.** A ritual denial would read as a real one (**mu feko**). Modesty has no conventional form. |
| 2.19 | Collective honorific address (各位领导，各位同事) | habit | 各位领导… *gè wèi lǐngdǎo* — each CL.HON leader | yes | **nun nun, no wungu la yen me la …** |
| 2.20 | Ritual courtesies at parting and hosting (慢走, 您先请, 留步) | habit | 您慢走。 *nín màn zǒu* — you.HON slowly walk "take care" | yes | **upe isi.** "go well"; **so nun me isi ya.** "after you". |
| 2.21 | Indirect refusal (我再考虑考虑, 不太方便) | habit | 这个……不太方便。 *zhège… bú tài fāngbiàn* — this… not too convenient | yes | **nun, tan to gofa.** "this is rather difficult". Same strategy, and it's on the watch list. |

## 3. Written to strangers (instructions, technical, legal)

| # | Distinction | Must/habit/occasional | Example (original + gloss) | Apu Pi We | How expressed, or what is lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Written register (书面语): literary function words 之/其/于/以/所/此 and light verbs 进行/予以/加以 | must | 本产品适用于室内。 *běn chǎnpǐn shìyòng yú shìnèi* — this product suitable at indoors | no | **musu tan gun upe li bone mon bese.** The meaning comes through, but there is one register for speech and writing, so "this is an official text" can't be signalled. |
| 3.2 | Anaphoric 该 / 上述 / 本 "the said, the above" | habit | 该设备 *gāi shèbèi* — said device | yes | **yetu tan** (**tan** = just mentioned). |
| 3.3 | Institutional and formal self-reference (本公司, 我方, 本人) | habit | 本人同意。 *běnrén tóngyì* — this.person agree | partly | **lon tayu.** / **din …** (we, excluding you). Reference is kept; formality is lost. |
| 3.4 | Deontic must / may / must-not (必须 / 可以 / 不得 / 禁止) | must | 未成年人不得入内。 *wèichéngniánrén bùdé rùnèi* — minors not.permitted enter | yes | **keya** must, **gun efon** may, **mu gun efon** may not: **tiwu la mu gun efon inwa.** Kept apart from probability (stricter than Mandarin 可能/应该). |
| 3.5 | Weaker obligation 应当/应 vs 必须 | must (legal) | 用户应当定期更换密码。 *yònghù yīngdāng dìngqī gēnghuàn mìmǎ* — user should regularly change password | partly | It's **keya** (too strong) or **fon** (advice, addressed to the listener, not a duty). There's no graded "shall/should". |
| 3.6 | Prohibition signs (请勿, 禁止) | must | 请勿入内。 *qǐngwù rùnèi* — please.don't enter | yes | **tin nun mu inwa.** |
| 3.7 | Polite address to an unknown reader (敬请谅解, 您) | habit | 给您带来不便，敬请谅解。 *gěi nín dàilái búbiàn, jìngqǐng liàngjiě* — to you.HON bring inconvenience, respectfully request forgive | yes | **nun nun, din sepi gi batu tan.** |
| 3.8 | Step sequencing (首先 / 然后 / 最后 / 第一步) | habit | 首先关闭电源，然后… *shǒuxiān guānbì diànyuán, ránhòu* — first close power, then | yes | **ya** "first", **wo** "after that"; labelled steps **lite pi fi** "step 1": **lite pi fi, tin tapi …** |
| 3.9 | Agentless passive of requirement (须经批准, 已被禁止) | habit | 须经批准。 *xū jīng pīzhǔn* — must pass.through approval | yes | **gun** + verb: **keya gun efon.** "must be allowed". |
| 3.10 | Conditions (如 / 若…则 / 除非) | must | 如有疑问，请致电… *rú yǒu yíwèn, qǐng zhìdiàn* — if have doubt, please send.call | yes | **don me ibe ge mido, so nun yebin …**; unless = **don mu**. |
| 3.11 | Exceptions (除…外) | habit | 除周日外每天营业。 *chú zhōurì wài měitiān yíngyè* — except Sunday outside every.day do.business | yes | **din doku mon dili we, un mu mon dili pi oma.** "…but not on day 7". |
| 3.12 | And/or precision (和 / 或 / 及; inclusive vs exclusive or) | must (legal) | A或B *A huò B* — A or B (inclusive or exclusive left to context) | yes | **lu** inclusive, **lu X lu Y** exclusive. More explicit than Mandarin. |
| 3.13 | Numeric bounds (以上 / 以下 / 不少于 / 不超过); inclusiveness | must | 十八岁以上 *shíbā suì yǐshàng* — eighteen year above | yes | **pa** at least, **mu ke se** X at most: **ewo man nume pa fawu yawu kefi.** Unambiguously inclusive (Mandarin 以上 is a known ambiguity). |
| 3.14 | Definitions (是指 / 系指) | must (legal) | "用户"是指… *"yònghù" shì zhǐ* — "user" is point.to | yes | **"no bone" yofu ewo man …** |
| 3.15 | Party labels (甲方 / 乙方) | must (legal) | 甲方应向乙方支付… *jiǎfāng yīng xiàng yǐfāng zhīfù* — party.A should toward party.B pay | yes | Noun + **pi** + number = label: **ewo pi fi keya unfu ewo pi sa, nimo …** |
| 3.16 | Time limits (自…起…日内) | must (legal) | 自签字之日起三十日内 *zì qiānzì zhī rì qǐ sānshí rì nèi* — from sign ATTR day start thirty day within | yes | **nin dili ka yawu wo tusi suso** "during 30 days after signing". |
| 3.17 | Approximation and probability (约, 左右, 可能) | habit | 约需30分钟。 *yuē xū sānshí fēnzhōng* — about need 30 minute | yes | **fu** + number, **kon**: **wupi minitu fu ka yawu.** |
| 3.18 | Reformulation (即 / 也就是说) | habit | 即… *jí* — namely | yes | **ben yofu …** "that means …". |
| 3.19 | Open lists (等 / 包括但不限于) | habit | 包括但不限于A、B *bāokuò dàn bù xiànyú A, B* — include but not limit.to A, B | yes | **musu la fu A yen B** "things like A and B"; **… yen eni** "and others". |
| 3.20 | Technical vocabulary | must | 互联网 *hùliánwǎng* — mutual-link-net "internet" | yes | Borrowed from English: **intalunetu**. |
| 3.21 | Warnings (注意 / 警告 / 危险) | must | 危险！请勿靠近。 *wēixiǎn! qǐngwù kàojìn* — danger! please.don't approach | yes | **yo nun, mu obu gelu.**; **yo yo** for emergency. |
| 3.22 | Fixed document formulas (特此通知, 此致敬礼) | must (official letters) | 特此通知。 *tècǐ tōngzhī* — specially.hereby notify | partly | The content can be said (**tan gun ge iko** "this is a report/notice"), but there are no conventional opening or closing formulas that mark genre. |
| 3.23 | Generic and universal subject (凡…者, 任何人, 用户) | habit | 凡违反规定者… *fán wéifǎn guīdìng zhě* — all violate rule one.who | yes | **go** anyone, or **ewo we man mu lefan wabun …** "everyone who doesn't follow the rule …". |

## 4. Persuasive (arguing, opinion, complaint)

| # | Distinction | Must/habit/occasional | Example (original + gloss) | Apu Pi We | How expressed, or what is lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Opinion framing: 我认为 (reasoned) vs 我觉得 (felt) | habit | 我认为这个办法不行。 *wǒ rènwéi zhège bànfǎ bù xíng* — I consider this CL method not work | yes | **lon pume wun lite tan gun yomo.** vs **lon feme wun …** |
| 4.2 | Epistemic degree (肯定 / 一定 / 大概 / 也许 / 不一定) | habit | 他肯定知道。 *tā kěndìng zhīdào* — he certainly know | yes | **kon yawu wan lugo.**, **kon oma** … Finer-grained than Mandarin. |
| 4.3 | Evidence attribution (听说 / 据统计 / 研究表明) | habit | 听说他辞职了。 *tīngshuō tā cízhí le* — hear-say he resign CRS | yes | **fen wan sin kusa doku.** |

## Top gaps

Ranked by how often a speaker needs them.

1. **Sentence-final stance particles: 啊, 嘛, 呗, 哦, musing 呢** (1.4–1.8; register 1). Several per minute in casual talk. Apu Pi We covers the speech act (**so**, **fon**, tag **min**) but has no casual softener and nothing for obviousness, resignation or friendly reminding. **nun** is the only softener, and it signals deference.
2. **Resultative and directional complements** (5.6, 5.7; register 5, also 1 and 3). They're in almost every action clause (看完, 听懂, 找到, 拿出来). Apu Pi We must split means and result into two clauses or drop one, and it loses deixis on caused motion.
3. **Evaluative and scalar adverbs: 才/就, 连…都, 竟然, 明明, 反正, 其实, 毕竟** (4.7–4.10, 4.15–4.17; register 4). These carry the speaker's expectation and argumentative stance. **ba** and **wame** cover only fragments.
4. **Focus and clefts: 是…的, contrastive 是/就是** (4.11, 5.10; registers 4, 5). There's no focus device; only parallel sentences.
5. **Emotion-specific interjections: 哎呀, 哇, 唉, 哼** (1.10; registers 1, 5). Everything collapses into **ba**.
6. **Familiar and kin-based address: 哥, 阿姨, 师傅, 小王, 老李** (1.26, 1.27, 2.4; registers 1, 2). Only names with **en** or built role words. No fictive kin, diminutives or generic honorific titles.
7. **把/被 framing: affectedness and adversity** (5.8, 5.9; register 5, also 1). Who-did-what survives; "it happened to my cost" and the disposal framing don't.
8. **Referent honorifics and humble forms: 您 in every mention, 贵/令, 敝/拙见, 位** (2.1, 2.5, 2.6, 2.14; register 2). **nun** scopes over the speech act only, so you can't honour a person or thing, or humble your own.
