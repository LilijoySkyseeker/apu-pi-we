# Vietnamese: distinctions by style, checked against Apu Pi We

Sources read for Apu Pi We (APW): `docs/language.md`, `data/grammar.tsv`, `data/lexicon.tsv`, `data/compounds.tsv`. Nothing else in the repo was consulted.

Gloss conventions: I/you(older) etc. = the kin or status term used as a pronoun; POL = politeness particle *ạ*; PRT = sentence-final particle (meaning given in the row); PAST = *đã*; PROG = *đang*; FUT = *sẽ*; ADVERS = adversative *bị*; BENEF = beneficial *được*; CL = classifier; VOC = vocative *ơi*; NEG = negator; SV = Sino-Vietnamese word.

Frequency column: **obligatory** = leaving it out is ungrammatical or socially wrong; **habitual** = speakers mark it nearly every time it applies; **occasional** = available and used, not expected.

## 1. Casual (friends, family)

| # | Distinction | Frequency | Example (Vietnamese + gloss) | APW | How, or what's lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Kin-term pronoun pairs in the family: speaker and addressee each named by their relation (*con/mẹ*, *em/anh*, *cháu/bà*), encoding relative age, generation and gender in every clause | obligatory | *Mẹ ơi, con đói.* mother VOC, child(=I) hungry | partly | **mole, lon momu nawi.** "mother, I want to eat". The kin noun can be said as an address word, but the pronoun slots are always **lon**/**me**, so the speaker's self-label ("child") and the relation carried in every clause are lost. Older vs younger sibling needs a phrase (**faba ke pani**). |
| 1.2 | Peer-intimate pronouns *tớ/cậu*, *mình/bạn* (friendly equals, often schoolmates) | habitual | *Tớ thích cậu.* I(friendly) like you(friendly) | no | **lon tana me.** **lon**/**me** are the same for everyone; **nun** only adds deference, so there is no way to mark closeness or equal footing. |
| 1.3 | Rough/contemptuous pronouns *tao/mày* (very close same-age peers, or hostility) | habitual | *Tao không cần mày.* I(rude) not need you(rude) | no | **lon mu wupi me.** No register below neutral; both rough intimacy and open contempt are lost. |
| 1.4 | Inclusive vs exclusive "we" (*mình, chúng ta* vs *chúng tôi, bọn tớ*) | obligatory | *Bọn mình đi thôi.* we(incl, casual) go PRT(let's) | yes | **so pen isi.** "let's go" (**pen** incl., **din** excl.). |
| 1.5 | Plural markers *các, những, mấy, tụi, bọn* | habitual | *Tụi nó về rồi.* PL(casual) he return already | yes | **wan la sin sigo.** The casual flavour of *tụi* is lost (minor). |
| 1.6 | Classifiers with numerals (*con* animals, *cái* objects, *chiếc* vehicles/one of a pair, *quyển* books) | obligatory | *hai con chó* two CL(animal) dog | partly | **totu sa** "dog two". Counting works with no classifier; the noun-class information is lost. It is mostly redundant; animate vs inanimate survives only in **wan** vs **bu**. |
| 1.7 | Specific vs generic/indefinite via classifier vs bare noun | habitual | *Con chó cắn tôi.* CL dog bite me ("the dog bit me") vs *Chó cắn.* dog bite ("dogs bite") | partly | **totu sin lasu lon.** No articles: "a dog" vs "the dog" vs "dogs" is left to context; **tan**/**ben** or **bu** narrow it when needed. |
| 1.8 | Three-way demonstratives: *này* (here), *đó/ấy* (there, known), *kia* (over there, visible far) | habitual | *cái này, cái đó, cái kia* CL this, CL that, CL yonder | partly | **musu tan**, **musu ben**. Two degrees only: *đó* and *kia* both become **ben**. |
| 1.9 | *đã* / *rồi*: completed or past | habitual | *Tôi ăn rồi.* I eat already | yes | **lon sin nawi.** |
| 1.10 | *đang*: in progress | habitual | *Mẹ đang nấu cơm.* mother PROG cook rice | yes | **mole le siten yegon.** |
| 1.11 | *sẽ*: future or prediction | habitual | *Mai em sẽ gọi.* tomorrow I(younger) FUT call; *Trời sẽ mưa.* sky FUT rain | partly | **wuma, lon yebin me.** A time word or **de** (my intention) or **win** (imminent) does the work. There is no neutral future, so a bare prediction needs **kon** + number or a time word. |
| 1.12 | *rồi* as new situation / change of state | habitual | *Mưa rồi!* rain already ("it's started raining") | yes | **gegi sin tuko.** "rain has started"; **lewe** "become" for states. |
| 1.13 | *chưa*: not yet, and the "yet?" question | obligatory | *Ăn cơm chưa?* eat rice yet | yes | **min me sin nawi.** Answer **sin nawi** / **mu sin**. |
| 1.14 | *vừa / mới*: just now (recent past) | habitual | *Anh ấy vừa đi.* he just go | yes | **wan sin isi ya foke.** "went a moment ago" (offset from now). |
| 1.15 | *sắp*: about to | habitual | *Sắp mưa.* about-to rain | yes | **gegi win dosi.** |
| 1.16 | *còn*: still | habitual | *Nó còn ngủ.* he still sleep | yes | **wan le wopo.** |
| 1.17 | *bị* (adversative) / *được* (beneficial) passive; *mất* (completed with loss) | obligatory | *Nó bị chó cắn.* he ADVERS dog bite; *Em được mẹ cho tiền.* I(younger) BENEF mother give money; *Nó ăn mất cái bánh.* he eat lose CL cake | partly | **totu sin lasu wan.** / **mole sin neke lon nimo.** The event survives as an active clause, but the speaker's framing as misfortune or good luck is lost unless said separately (**ben gun yomo**). There is no passive with a named agent. |
| 1.18 | Yes/no question *có … không* | obligatory | *Anh có đi không?* you(older) EMPH go NEG | yes | **min me isi.** |
| 1.19 | Agreement-seeking *nhỉ*, *phải không*, *đúng không* | habitual | *Hôm nay nóng nhỉ?* today hot PRT(isn't it) | yes | **mefa gun tikun, min.** (tag **min**). Self-directed *nhỉ* ("what was it again?") fits **moti**: **suso pi wan gun yu, moti.** |
| 1.20 | *à / hả*: question checking a surprising assumption | habitual | *Anh về rồi à?* you(older) return already PRT(surprised check) | partly | **me sin sigo, min.** The tag gives the confirmation check; the surprise is lost (the doc doesn't say whether **ba** and **min** can combine). |
| 1.21 | *nhé / nha*: friendly softener on proposals, promises and requests ("ok?") | habitual | *Mai gặp nhé!* tomorrow meet PRT | partly | **nagu me wuma, min.** The tag gets the light consent. **nun** is deference, not warmth, so the friendly tone has no outlet. |
| 1.22 | *đi*: urging imperative | habitual | *Ăn đi!* eat PRT(go on) | yes | **tin nawi.** **tin** is a neutral instruction; the encouraging "go on" is carried by voice. |
| 1.23 | *mà / cơ / đâu*: insisting against what the listener believes | habitual | *Em nói rồi mà!* I(younger) say already PRT(but I did!) | partly | **ba lon sin oyi ben.** **ba** gives emphasis, but "contrary to what you think, and you should know" is lost. |
| 1.24 | *chứ*: obviousness ("of course", "why even ask") | habitual | *Đi chứ!* go PRT(of course) | partly | **de isi.** **kon yawu** gives certainty, but not obviousness or the mild "silly question". |
| 1.25 | *đấy / đó / kìa*: drawing the listener's attention ("look", "you know") | habitual | *Nó đến rồi đấy.* he arrive already PRT(take note); *Nó kìa!* he PRT(over there, look) | partly | **o wan sin liwon.** (seen) or **tin miya, wan sin liwon.** The "take note" stance only comes through as an explicit instruction. |
| 1.26 | *thôi*: "only / that's all"; also resignation ("oh well") | habitual | *Tôi uống một cốc thôi.* I drink one cup only; *Thôi, kệ nó.* enough, ignore it | partly | **lon koye na ladon fi.** "only" is fine; resignation and closing a topic are lost. |
| 1.27 | Vocative *ơi* and attention-getters *này* (neutral), *ê* (rude) | habitual | *Lan ơi!* Lan VOC | partly | **en lan.** A name can be said alone. There is no vocative particle and no stated slot for an address phrase in a sentence; affectionate *ơi* vs blunt *ê* is lost. |
| 1.28 | Casual yes *ừ / ờ* | habitual | *Đi không? – Ừ.* go NEG – yeah | yes | **min isi. – isi.** Answer by echo, or **mm**. |
| 1.29 | Emotional interjections *ôi*, *trời ơi*, *ối*, *chết* (delight, dismay, pain, alarm) | habitual | *Chết rồi!* die already ("oh no!"); *Ôi, đẹp quá!* oh beautiful very | partly | **ba bu gun bi sesa.** **ba** marks an exclamation, but which emotion is left to voice. APW has only **mm** and **huh?** as interjections. |
| 1.30 | Intensifiers *quá, lắm, cực, ghê* | habitual | *Đẹp quá!* beautiful very | yes | **bu gun bi sesa** / **bi bi sesa**. |
| 1.31 | Vulgar intensifiers and swearing (*vãi*, *đéo*) | habitual (among peers) | *Đẹp vãi!* beautiful (vulgar intensifier) | no | **bi bi sesa** keeps the degree; there is no taboo vocabulary, so the in-group roughness is lost. |
| 1.32 | Endearments and pet names (*cưng*, *bé*, *anh yêu*) | habitual (couples, parents) | *Cưng ăn đi.* darling eat PRT | no | **tin nawi.** No endearment words; only the name. |
| 1.33 | Dropping subject and object | habitual | *Đi đâu đấy?* go where PRT | yes | **isi tun kemu yu.** (rule 11). |
| 1.34 | Attenuating reduplication (*đo đỏ* reddish, *xinh xinh* rather pretty) | habitual | *cái áo đo đỏ* CL shirt red-red | yes | **demi to wiwi** "rather red cloth". (APW doubling *strengthens*, so it doesn't copy the Vietnamese pattern.) |

## 2. Hierarchical (boss, elders, officials)

| # | Distinction | Frequency | Example (Vietnamese + gloss) | APW | How, or what's lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Addressee term chosen by age, rank and gender (*ông, bà, bác, chú, cô, anh, chị, thầy, sếp, ngài*); bare "you" to a superior is rude | obligatory | *Bác ăn cơm chưa ạ?* uncle(=you, elder) eat rice yet POL | partly | **min nun me sin nawi.** **me** + **nun**, optionally a role word (**no tofo en …** "Teacher …"). Age, gender and the kin-like relation aren't encoded; a kin noun (**faba pi wede**) used for a non-relative reads literally. |
| 2.2 | Speaker's humble self-term (*em, con, cháu*; plural *chúng em*) instead of *tôi* | obligatory | *Em xin phép về trước ạ.* I(junior) ask-permission go-home first POL | no | **so nun me efon lon sigo ya.** **lon**/**din** are the same before anyone; the humility is spread over the utterance by **nun**, not placed on the self. |
| 2.3 | *ạ*: respect particle at the end of a sentence | obligatory | *Cháu cảm ơn bà ạ.* I(grandchild) thank grandma POL | yes | **nun lota.** (**nun nun** for more). |
| 2.4 | Polite response tokens *dạ / vâng* vs casual *ừ* (also *dạ* = "yes?" when called) | obligatory | *Dạ, vâng ạ.* yes(POL) yes POL | partly | **nun obu.** (echo + **nun**). There is no yes-word; **mm** is neutral, so there is no polite "yes?" when called. |
| 2.5 | *Thưa / Kính thưa*: honorific opening address | habitual | *Thưa thầy, em có câu hỏi.* respectfully teacher, I(junior) have CL question | yes | **nun, no tofo, lon ibe ge mido.** (address-phrase position not specified; see 1.27) |
| 2.6 | *mời* (polite offer or invitation, for the hearer's benefit) vs *xin* (humble request, for the speaker's) | habitual | *Mời bác vào nhà.* invite uncle(=you) enter house | partly | **so nun me inwa guyi.** **so** is a request; an offer for the hearer's benefit isn't distinguished (**fon** is advice, not an offer). |
| 2.7 | Honorific and euphemistic verbs (*dùng/xơi* vs *ăn*; *mất/qua đời* vs *chết*) | habitual | *Mời ông dùng nước.* invite grandpa(=you) use(HON) water; *Bà tôi mất rồi.* grandma my lose(=pass away) already | partly | **so nun me koye osa.** / **mole pi mole pi lon sin kono.** One verb per meaning; deference only through **nun**; no euphemism for death. |
| 2.8 | Respect toward a third person (*ông ấy, cụ, ngài ấy* vs *nó*) | habitual | *Ông giám đốc đã về rồi ạ.* Mr director PAST return already POL | partly | **nun no wungu sin sigo.** A title gives respect, but **nun** is aimed at the listener, and **wan** for a respected elder is the same as for a child. |
| 2.9 | Indirect, hedged request | habitual | *Anh có thể giúp em được không ạ?* you(older) can help me(junior) possible NEG POL | yes | **min nun me lenu yema lon.** (A direct **so nun me yema lon** is equally polite by design.) |
| 2.10 | Asking permission *xin phép, cho … xin* | habitual | *Cho cháu xin phép hỏi.* let me(grandchild) ask-permission ask | yes | **min nun me efon lon mido.** |
| 2.11 | Respectful plural address (*các bác, quý vị, các anh chị*), formal openers *Kính gửi* | obligatory | *Kính thưa quý vị* respectfully address honoured persons | yes | **nun nun, me la.** |
| 2.12 | Gender-neutral double address *anh chị* when gender is unknown | habitual | *Anh chị cho em hỏi.* older-brother older-sister let me ask | yes | **so nun me efon lon mido.** **me** is already neutral. |
| 2.13 | Softened disagreement with a superior | habitual | *Dạ, cháu nghĩ là chưa đúng ạ.* yes(POL) I(grandchild) think that not-yet correct POL | yes | **nun lon pume wun bu mu denku.** (plus **kon** if unsure). |
| 2.14 | Apology formula with address term | habitual | *Xin lỗi bác ạ.* ask fault uncle(=you) POL | yes | **nun sepi.** |
| 2.15 | Modest refusal or deflection (*không dám* "I wouldn't dare", also used as "you're welcome") | occasional | *Dạ, cháu không dám.* yes(POL) I(grandchild) not dare | partly | **nun, mu …** + echo. The modesty formula and the "don't thank me" use are lost. |

## 3. Written for strangers (instructions, technical, legal)

| # | Distinction | Frequency | Example (Vietnamese + gloss) | APW | How, or what's lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Sino-Vietnamese formal vocabulary vs native words (*tử vong / chết*, *phụ nữ / đàn bà*, *cư trú / ở*) | obligatory (in formal text) | *Bệnh nhân đã tử vong.* patient(SV) PAST die(SV) | no | **ewo simi sin kono.** One root per meaning; register can't be shifted by word choice. |
| 3.2 | Impersonal or generic subject (*người ta, người dùng*) | habitual | *Người dùng phải đăng nhập.* person use must register-enter | yes | **no bone keya inwa.** / **go** "anyone". |
| 3.3 | Agentless passive with *được* (neutral formal) | habitual | *Hồ sơ được nộp tại phòng 3.* dossier PASS submit at room 3 | yes | **go sege mabo la tun kemu pi ka.** Or drop the subject (rule 11), or **gun** + verb for the resulting state. |
| 3.4 | Obligation *phải, có nghĩa vụ* | obligatory | *Bên thuê phải trả tiền.* party rent must pay money | yes | **no sanwa keya unfu.** |
| 3.5 | Right / permission *được phép, có quyền*, and the *có thể* ability/permission ambiguity | habitual | *Người thuê có thể chấm dứt hợp đồng.* person rent can end contract | yes | **no sanwa gun efon fugi ge tayu.** APW forces a choice between **lenu** (able) and **gun efon** (allowed), where *có thể* is ambiguous. |
| 3.6 | Prohibition *không được, nghiêm cấm* | obligatory | *Không được chạm vào.* NEG permitted touch into | yes | **tin mu sanne.** / **go mu gun efon sanne bu.** ("strictly": **mu mu**) |
| 3.7 | Recommendation *nên* | habitual | *Nên rửa tay trước khi ăn.* should wash hand before when eat | yes | **fon pensi kiti ya nawi.** |
| 3.8 | Polite impersonal instruction *vui lòng, xin, hãy* | habitual | *Vui lòng không chạm vào.* please NEG touch into | yes | **tin nun mu sanne.** |
| 3.9 | Warning *chú ý, cảnh báo, lưu ý* | habitual | *Chú ý: sàn ướt.* attention: floor wet | yes | **yo nun, fowo gun wulon.** |
| 3.10 | Condition *nếu … thì, trong trường hợp* | obligatory | *Nếu máy quá nóng thì dừng lại.* if machine too hot then stop | yes | **don yetu gun bi tikun, tin bamo bu.** (**kon** adds likelihood). |
| 3.11 | Exception *trừ khi, ngoại trừ* | habitual | *Trừ khi có thỏa thuận khác, …* unless exist agreement other | yes | **don mu gun ge tayu eni, …** ("if there is no other agreement"); a relative clause for "except X" (**ewo man mu gun tiwu**). No dedicated word, but nothing is lost. |
| 3.12 | Distributive *mỗi* (each) | habitual | *Mỗi người được một phiếu.* each person get one ticket | partly | **ewo we dunu mise fi.** "all people get one card" is ambiguous between one each and one shared; there is no "each" (**fi** + relative clause is clumsy). |
| 3.13 | Step order *bước 1, trước tiên, sau đó, cuối cùng* | obligatory | *Bước 1: mở túi.* step 1: open bag | yes | **lite pi fi, tin benbi yufa.** Also **ya** "first", **wo** "after that", **mon fugi** "at the end". |
| 3.14 | Nominalisers *việc, sự, cuộc* | habitual | *Việc sử dụng điện thoại bị cấm.* NOM use phone ADVERS forbid | yes | **go mu gun efon bone liga.** Position makes the action noun. |
| 3.15 | Anaphoric reference *nói trên, này, đó* (the aforementioned) | habitual | *các điều khoản nói trên* PL clause said above | yes | **wabun la man sin oyi** / **wabun la tan**. |
| 3.16 | Bounds *không quá, ít nhất, từ … đến* | obligatory | *không quá 30 phút* not exceed 30 minute | yes | **mu ke se minitu ka yawu**; **pa**; **se X tun Y**. |
| 3.17 | Connectors *do đó, tuy nhiên, nhằm, vì* | habitual | *Do đó, …* therefore | yes | **gi tan**, **un**, **li**, **gi**. |
| 3.18 | Definitions *là, được hiểu là* | habitual | *"Bên thuê" được hiểu là người thuê nhà.* "party rent" PASS understand be person rent house | yes | **"no sanwa" yofu ewo man sanwa guyi.** |
| 3.19 | Numbered labels *Điều 5, Khoản 2* | obligatory | *Điều 5* article 5 | yes | **wabun pi te**. |
| 3.20 | Respectful address to an unknown reader (*Quý khách, bạn*) | habitual | *Quý khách vui lòng xuất trình vé.* honoured-guest please show ticket | yes | **tin nun, me wuye mise.** |
| 3.21 | Technical terms (Sino-Vietnamese coinages or loans) | habitual | *mạng internet* net internet | yes | **intalunetu**. Borrowed from English, not drawn from a formal native layer. |
| 3.22 | *và/hoặc* precision | occasional | *A và/hoặc B* | yes | **A lu B** (both allowed); **lu A lu B** (not both). |

## 4. Persuasive (argument, opinion, complaint)

| # | Distinction | Frequency | Example (Vietnamese + gloss) | APW | How, or what's lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Opinion framing *theo tôi, tôi nghĩ, tôi thấy* | habitual | *Theo tôi, kế hoạch này không ổn.* according me, plan this not stable | yes | **lon pume wun lite tan mu upe.** |
| 4.2 | Certainty and hedges *chắc chắn, chắc là, có lẽ, hình như* | habitual | *Chắc là anh ấy quên rồi.* probably he forget already | yes | **kon oma wan sin yakin.**; *hình như* ≈ **san**. |
| 4.3 | Intensifiers *rất, quá, cực kỳ, vô cùng* | habitual | *Chậm quá!* slow very | yes | **ba bu gun bi gake.** / doubling. |
| 4.4 | Mitigators *hơi, một chút* | habitual | *Hơi chậm.* rather slow | yes | **to gake**. |
| 4.5 | Emphatic negation *không hề, chẳng, chả* | habitual | *Tôi không hề biết.* I NEG at-all know | yes | **lon mu mu lugo.** |
| 4.6 | Denying the listener's assumption (*có … đâu*, *mà*, *cơ mà*) | habitual | *Tôi có biết đâu!* I EMPH know where(=NEG, contrary to what you assume) | partly | **lon mu mu lugo.** The emphasis survives; "you're wrong to assume it" is lost. |
| 4.7 | Counter-expectation and reproach *lại* | habitual | *Sao anh lại làm thế?* why you(older) COUNTER do so | no | **me sin muge ben gi yu.** It asks why, but "which you shouldn't have / which surprises me" has no marker. |
| 4.8 | Concession *mặc dù … nhưng*, *vậy mà* | habitual | *Tuy nhỏ nhưng tốt.* though small but good | yes | **bu gun meli, un bu gun upe.** |
| 4.9 | Scalar focus "even" (*ngay cả … cũng, đến … cũng*) | habitual | *Ngay cả trẻ con cũng biết.* even child also know | no | Closest is **tiwu wame lugo** "children also know"; the "least likely case" scale is lost. There is no "even". |
| 4.10 | Contrastive/exclusive focus *chính*, *mới* | habitual | *Chính anh nói thế.* precisely you(older) say so | partly | **me sin oyi ben.** No focus device; contrast only through parallel sentences (**lon mu oyi ben. me oyi ben.**), as the doc notes. |
| 4.11 | Rhetorical question (*Ai mà chẳng biết?*, *Chẳng lẽ …?*) | habitual | *Ai mà chẳng biết?* who PRT not know (= everyone knows) | partly | **a** (not literal) exists, but the doc doesn't say whether it combines with **min**; usually the claim is just stated (**ewo we lugo.**). |
| 4.12 | Irony / sarcasm | occasional | *Giỏi nhỉ!* clever PRT (ironic) | yes | **a me gun gole.** |
| 4.13 | Exasperated or indignant particles in complaints (*hả, chứ, trời ơi*) | habitual | *Sao chậm thế hả?* why slow so PRT(exasperated) | partly | **me gun bi gake gi yu.** **ba** can't be added to a question (as far as the doc says); the exasperation is left to voice. |
| 4.14 | Insult epithets *đồ* + adjective/noun | occasional | *Đồ ngu!* thing stupid | partly | **me mu gole.** States the judgement; the contemptuous *đồ* frame and its force are lost. |
| 4.15 | Comparison and superlative *hơn, nhất* | obligatory | *Cái này tốt hơn cái kia.* CL this good more CL that | yes | **musu tan gun ke upe se musu ben.**; "most" = **se we**. |
| 4.16 | Solidarity via inclusive *chúng ta* | habitual | *Chúng ta phải hành động.* we(incl) must act | yes | **pen keya muge.** |
| 4.17 | Proposals and appeals *hãy, nên, … đi* | habitual | *Anh nên về đi.* you(older) should go-home PRT | yes | **fon me sigo tun guyi.** / **so pen …** |
| 4.18 | Apologetic preface *xin lỗi, nhưng* | habitual | *Xin lỗi, nhưng tôi không đồng ý.* sorry, but I not agree | yes | **sepi, un lon mu tayu.** |
| 4.19 | Warning or threat *coi chừng* | occasional | *Coi chừng đấy!* watch-out PRT | yes | **yo …** |
| 4.20 | Cause and consequence *vì … nên* | obligatory | *Vì mưa nên tôi ở nhà.* because rain so I stay home | yes | **gi gegi, lon piyi mon guyi.** |
| 4.21 | Generalisations *ai cũng, lúc nào cũng, chẳng bao giờ* | habitual | *Lúc nào cũng thế!* time which also so | yes | **mon muyi we, ben muwo.**; "never" = **mon muyi fa**. |

## 5. Narrative (story, news)

| # | Distinction | Frequency | Example (Vietnamese + gloss) | APW | How, or what's lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Top gaps

Ranked by how often a Vietnamese speaker would reach for the distinction.

1. **Relational person reference** (1.1, 2.1, 2.2; styles 1, 2): Vietnamese can't say "I" or "you" without choosing a kin or status term that encodes relative age, generation and gender. APW has neutral **lon**/**me** plus **nun**; the speaker's humble self-term (*em, con, cháu*) can't be expressed at all.
2. **Stance particles about the listener's knowledge** (1.20–1.25, 4.6, 4.13; styles 1, 4): *mà, chứ, đấy, à, nhé, hả* mark "contrary to what you think", "obviously", "take note", "surprised check", "friendly ok?". APW has only tag **min**, **ba** and **nun**, so most of these come out as emphasis or politeness, or not at all.
3. **Counter-expectation and mirativity** (4.7, 5.7, 5.8; styles 4, 5): *lại* (unexpected or reproachable), *hóa ra / ai ngờ* (surprising discovery), *bỗng* (sudden). There is no marker, and no word for "suddenly".
4. **Adversative / beneficial framing** (1.17, 5.14; styles 1, 5): *bị / được / mất* tell whether an event hurt or helped the person affected. APW keeps the event but drops the framing, and has no passive with an agent.
5. **Register below neutral and evaluative reference** (1.2, 1.3, 1.31, 1.32, 4.14, 5.11; styles 1, 4, 5): intimate and rough pronouns, contemptuous third persons and classifiers (*hắn, thằng, mụ, bọn*), endearments and swearing. APW has an outlet upward (**nun**) but none for closeness, affection or contempt.
6. **Scalar and contrastive focus** (4.9, 4.10; style 4): no "even", no focus particle; *ngay cả … cũng* and *chính* are basic argument tools.
7. **Honorific and formal vocabulary layers** (2.7, 2.8, 3.1; styles 2, 3): Sino-Vietnamese formal words, honorific verbs (*dùng, mất*), and respect toward a third person. APW has one word per meaning, and **nun** is aimed only at the listener.
8. **Near-miss and distributive quantity** (5.6, 3.12; styles 5, 3): no "almost" (*suýt, gần*) and no "each" (*mỗi*). Both need clumsy paraphrase, and "each" can be ambiguous in legal text.

Note (not a distinction): the doc already lists **lon** "I" as matching a Vietnamese vulgarity (*lồn*). For a Vietnamese speaker this lands hardest in style 2, where the first-person word is said to elders and superiors.
