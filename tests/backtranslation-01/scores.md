## Recurring kinds of loss

1. **Profanity softened.** "fuck you" became "damn you", "bitch ass" became "damned", and "what the fuck" became "what the hell". The comic, profane register of Text A is toned down throughout.
2. **The commander's gender was lost.** She became "he" in B8, B11 and B22, because the ungendered pronoun **lu** gave the reader nothing to go on.
3. **Specific terms were generalised.** "panic" and "panic response" became "great fear", "commander" became "leader", "offend" became "make angry", "my system" became "my body", and "daydreaming" became "dreaming". Each is close, but the specific sense was lost: a conditioned reflex, a military rank, an affront versus anger.
4. **Abstract and figurative phrases were garbled.** "distant remnants of themselves", "warped awkwardly" and the "retreat from retreating" wordplay all came back with a different or broken meaning.
5. **Emotion labels shifted.** Disgust became hurt (B20), and helpless questioning became incredulous echoing (B4).
6. **Comic register was flattened.** The mock-expert "pretty basic level of stick expertise" lost both its deadpan framing and its logical structure (A2).

The core events, who did what, and the speech acts (orders, questions, insults, sarcasm marked with **san**) survived well. No segment lost its core meaning.
