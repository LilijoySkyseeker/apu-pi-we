# Back-translation

## 1. Translation

```
A: Hi. Are you all still coming on Saturday?
B: Probably, yeah. What time was it again?
A: Around seven. Maybe seven-thirty. I have to clean up first.
C: Wait, is this at your place or at Marcus's?
A: My place. Marcus can't. His landlord is doing some work on the water pipes.
C: Oh no. Again? That happened a month ago too, right?
A: Yeah, same thing. The water flooded his kitchen. This one's bad.
B: At this point he needs to move out.
A: I know. I told him that. He says he can't find a place with cheaper rent.
C: True. Rent is really high right now.
B: So do I need to bring anything?
A: Maybe chips or something. Or drinks. Anything's fine. It's not a big deal.
C: I can bring the dip I made for Dez's birthday.
B: The one with spinach? Oh please bring it. It's so good.
C: Will do. But I have to remember to buy the stuff to make it.
A: Is Dez coming? I texted them, but they haven't answered yet.
B: They're probably working Saturday. They said maybe after, around ten.
A: That's fine. We'll probably still be up.
C: Wait. Do I need to park on the street, or can I park in your driveway?
A: The street's fine. Just don't block the neighbour's driveway. They get really mad.
B: They yelled at me once before.
C: I won't block it. See you all Saturday.
```

## 3. Unsure turns

- **Turn 5**: "muge musu bo gan du dinyo osa" is literally "is doing some unspecified thing with the water-carrying things". I took it as the landlord doing work on the pipes (and that's why Marcus can't host), but it could also mean the landlord did something to the pipes that caused damage, or is having them replaced. The general sense (plumbing trouble at Marcus's) is clear; the landlord's exact role isn't.
- **Turn 20**: "tin mu tapi musu pi no nume gelu" is literally "don't close the thing of the one who lives near". I read "close" as "block" and the unnamed "thing" as the neighbour's driveway, which fits the parking context and turn 22, but the actual object (driveway, garage, gate, mailbox) is guessed.
