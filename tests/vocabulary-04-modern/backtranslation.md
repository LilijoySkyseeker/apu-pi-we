# Back-translation, test bt10

Format per segment: **gloss** (word by word), then **reading** (natural English).

## Text F: two friends talking

**F1.** "min me Obu tun FANmu li LUse mon YAmi tan?"
- Gloss: Q you come to gather(ing) for play at night this?
- Reading: "Are you coming to the party tonight?" (FANmu "a gathering" + li LUse "to have fun" = a party / get-together.)

**F2.** "mu Obu. lon KEya YEsa li MIdo pi LUgo."
- Gloss: not come. I must learn for asking of knowledge.
- Reading: "Can't. I've got to study for an exam." ("asking of knowledge" = test / exam. The echoed "not coming" stands in for "no".)

**F3.** "so so Obu! na nin MUyi MEli."
- Gloss: REQUEST REQUEST come! only during time small.
- Reading: "Oh come on, come! Just for a little while." (The doubled request makes it insistent and pleading.)

**F4.** "Upe, un lon win KUsa ya FUgi."
- Gloss: good, but I about-to leave before end.
- Reading: "Okay, but I'm leaving early, before it ends."

## Text S: a notice from a school

**S1.** MOle yen WEde la gun MIdo li Obu tun KEgin pi ge YUyun pi pu YEsa.
- Gloss: mother and father PL is asked for come to evening of thing-sung of place-learn.
- Reading: Parents are invited to the school's evening concert. ("thing sung" = song; "place for learning" = school; "evening of songs" = concert night.)

**S2.** TIwu la KEya YUNko LONne KIge INan, yen DINyo ge KOye.
- Gloss: child PL must wear clothes same clean, and carry thing-drunk.
- Reading: Children must wear a clean uniform and bring a drink (e.g. a water bottle). ("same clothes" = uniform.)

**S3.** so ku Iko no TOfo, don GAwo pi TIwu pi me FEbo YOmo tun PAsu in.
- Gloss: REQUEST POLITE tell one-who-teaches, if body of child of you answer bad to food some.
- Reading: Please let the teacher know if your child has any food allergies. ("the body reacts badly to some food" = food allergy / intolerance.)

## Text I: a radio interview

**I1.** "no YEma Ewo SImi, Ewo bi FEli LEwe SImi nin WUnta KUbo gi yu?"
- Gloss: one-who-helps person sick, person very many become sick while air change because what?
- Reading: "Doctor, why do so many people get sick when the weather changes?" ("one who helps sick people", used as a form of address = doctor. "the air changes" = the weather / season changes.)

**I2.** "WUnta LEwe WEki yen KIlan, yen MUsu bi MEli man MUge SImi Isi se Ewo tun Ewo ke BUnle mon KEmu la man KEte gan Ewo."
- Gloss: air become cold and dry, and thing very small which make sick go from person to person more easy at place PL which full with person.
- Reading: "The air gets cold and dry, and germs spread from person to person more easily in crowded places." ("very small things that make you sick" = germs / viruses; "places full of people" = crowded places.)

**I3.** "PENsi KIti MUyi FEli gun du KUwi ke BUnle se we."
- Gloss: wash hand time many is thing-for-keep more easy than all.
- Reading: "Washing your hands often is the easiest way to protect yourself." ("thing for keeping" = protection / prevention: the simplest way to stay healthy.)

## Text J: a job advertisement

**J1.** din MIya li no KObi TAle man sin DOku nin MUyi NEtu, yen man KEgu LIsun SA.
- Gloss: we(excl.) look for one-who-moves car who DONE work during time long, and who talk tongue two.
- Reading: We're looking for an experienced driver who speaks two languages (bilingual). ("one who moves a car" = driver; "has worked for a long time" = experienced; "tongue" = language.)

**J2.** tin SEge MAbo pi me li DOku gan LIga.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT send paper of you for work with phone.
- Reading: Send your CV by phone (e.g. by text or messaging app). ("your paper for work" = CV / résumé / job application. By the attachment rule, "with phone" goes with the verb, so it's the means of sending. A less likely reading is "send your CV along with your phone number".)
