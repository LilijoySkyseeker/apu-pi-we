# Vocabulary test 04

Friends talking (Buenos Aires), a school notice (Jakarta), a radio health interview (Delhi), a job advert (Johannesburg).

**All 13 segments correct.** Built and composed words carried:
- party = "gathering for fun"; exam = "asking of knowledge"; uniform = "same clothes"
- food allergy = "body answers badly to some food"; germs/viruses = "very small things that make sickness"; crowded = "full of people"
- protection = "thing for keeping"; CV = "your paper for work"
- bilingual = "two tongues"; experienced = "has worked a long time"

"so so Obu!" (doubled request) was read as insistent pleading, which is the doubling principle at work.

**Lost to missing words: under 1 point.** "Email" came back as "by phone (text or messaging)".
