Four short modern texts. Quotation marks mark speech.

Text F (two friends talking)
F1. "min me Obu tun FANmu li LUse mon YAmi tan?"
F2. "mu Obu. lon KEya YEsa li MIdo pi LUgo."
F3. "so so Obu! na nin MUyi MEli."
F4. "Upe, un lon win KUsa ya FUgi."

Text S (a notice from a school)
S1. MOle yen WEde la gun MIdo li Obu tun KEgin pi ge YUyun pi pu YEsa.
S2. TIwu la KEya YUNko LONne KIge INan, yen DINyo ge KOye.
S3. so ku Iko no TOfo, don GAwo pi TIwu pi me FEbo YOmo tun PAsu in.

Text I (a radio interview)
I1. "no YEma Ewo SImi, Ewo bi FEli LEwe SImi nin WUnta KUbo gi yu?"
I2. "WUnta LEwe WEki yen KIlan, yen MUsu bi MEli man MUge SImi Isi se Ewo tun Ewo ke BUnle mon KEmu la man KEte gan Ewo."
I3. "PENsi KIti MUyi FEli gun du KUwi ke BUnle se we."

Text J (a job advertisement)
J1. din MIya li no KObi TAle man sin DOku nin MUyi NEtu, yen man KEgu LIsun SA.
J2. tin SEge MAbo pi me li DOku gan LIga.
