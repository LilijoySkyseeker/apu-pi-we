G1. If he had come, I would have eaten.  (counterfactual: he did not come)
G2. If he came (which is unlikely), I would eat.
G3. If he comes, I will eat.  (open condition)
G4. Mara must be at home.  (= she is very probably at home: about 5 in 6)
G5. Mara must stay at home.  (= she is obliged to)
G6. You may go.  (permission)
G7. You can go.  (ability)
G8. This house is bigger than that house, but smaller than ours (ours includes the listener).
G9. Mara is the smartest of all.
G10. Which do you want, fish or meat?  (choose one)
G11. Do you want fish or meat?  (yes/no: any of them at all)
G12. You get either fish or meat, not both.
G13. About 36 people came.
G14. I need from four to five bags.
G15. Pay at most twelve.  (instruction; 12 = 2×6)
G16. A third of the money is mine.
G17. Don't open the door.
G18. I also saw him.  (I, in addition to others... or: I also SAW him — "also" covers "saw him")
G19. They saw each other.
G20. I don't know whether he came.
G21. Let's go home.
G22. He hasn't eaten yet, but he is still sleeping.
G23. She went to the market to buy fish.
G24. I watched twice.
