# Back-translation G1–G24

Notes: **wan** has no antecedent in these single sentences, so it is "he/she" (I write "she"). Tense is unmarked unless **sin** appears. Numbers are base 6.

| # | Gloss | English (reading) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | IF CONF-0 he/she DONE come, I DONE eat | "If she had come, I would have eaten." Counterfactual (**kon PO** = 0 in 6): she did not come and I did not eat. Past. |
| G2 | IF CONF-1 he/she come, I eat | "If she were to come (which is unlikely, about 1 in 6), I would eat." The condition is unlikely but still possible. Present/future. |
| G3 | IF he/she come, I eat | "If she comes, I'll eat." Open, real condition; the speaker does not say how likely it is. |
| G4 | CONF-5 NAME Mara BE AT house | "Mara is probably at home" (5 in 6, about 83%). This is "must be" in the sense of probability, not obligation. |
| G5 | NAME Mara must stay AT house | "Mara has to stay at home." Obligation. |
| G6 | you BE allow go | "You may go." Permission: you are allowed to go. |
| G7 | you can go | "You can go." Ability: you are able to go. This is not permission. |
| G8 | house THIS BE MORE big FROM house THAT, BUT MORE small FROM house OF WE.incl | "This house is bigger than that house, but smaller than our house." "Our" is inclusive: yours and mine. |
| G9 | NAME Mara BE MORE smart FROM ALL | "Mara is the smartest of all." Literally "smarter than everyone." |
| G10 | you want fish OR meat WH? | "Which do you want, fish or meat?" This asks for a choice, and you answer by echoing "fish" or "meat". |
| G11 | Q you want fish OR meat | "Do you want fish or meat?" A yes/no question about the whole "or": do you want either one? |
| G12 | you take OR fish OR meat | "You take either the fish or the meat, but not both." This is exclusive "or" (**lu X lu Y**). There is no speech-act particle, so it is a statement, not an order. |
| G13 | person LIKE 36 DONE come | "About 36 people came." |
| G14 | I need bag FROM 4 TO 5 | "I need four to five bags." Necessity: I need them. This is a range of count. |
| G15 | INSTRUCT pay NOT MORE FROM 2·6 | "Pay no more than 12." An instruction setting a maximum of 12 (base-6 "two sixes"). The unit is unstated. |
| G16 | 1 OF 3 OF money BE OF I | "A third of the money is mine." |
| G17 | INSTRUCT NOT open door | "Don't open the door." A negative instruction. |
| G18 | I also see he/she | "I also saw her." "Also" covers the act of seeing her, in addition to other things I did. It does not mean "her too, as well as others I saw." The tense is unmarked. |
| G19 | he/she PL see SELF SELF | "They saw each other." Reciprocal. The tense is unmarked. |
| G20 | I NOT know THAT Q he/she DONE come | "I don't know whether she came." An indirect yes/no question. |
| G21 | REQUEST WE.incl go TO house | "Let's go home." A suggestion that includes the listener. |
| G22 | he/she NOT DONE eat, BUT he/she ONGOING sleep | "She hasn't eaten yet, but she's still sleeping." Both pronouns are the same person. The "but" sounds slightly odd. The likely sense is "she hasn't eaten yet; she's still asleep." |
| G23 | he/she DONE go TO PLACE buy FOR buy fish | "She went to the shop (literally 'place for buying') to buy fish." The second **li** marks purpose. |
| G24 | I DONE look time 2 | "I looked twice." Two occasions; this could also be "I have looked twice." |
