# Grammar coverage test 01 (roadmap B2)

**Method:** 24 single sentences, one per new construction. Built in contrasting pairs where possible:
- three kinds of condition (open, unlikely, contrary to fact);
- probable vs obligatory "must";
- permission vs ability;
- which-one vs yes/no questions;
- inclusive vs exclusive "or";
- about, ranges, at most, fractions;
- don't, also, each other, whether, let's, not yet / still, purpose, "twice".

A fresh blind reader got only the reference and word lists. Scored by Claude: every reading is clear-cut against the reference.

**Result: 24/24 correct**, including every contrast pair. Counterfactuals via the confidence scale (**don kon PO** "if — and it isn't so") were read as contrary-to-fact without hesitation.

**Notes:**
- The reader translated **wan** as "she" where English had "he". The pronoun has no gender, so that's not an error.
- "Also" before the verb was read as covering "saw her", which is what the scope rule says.
- The one oddity was the English "but" in G22, which Claude wrote.

**No new grammar words were needed.** Two roots were added: **Efon** "allow", **WAme** "also".

**Caveat:** single sentences with the rules at hand. This tests that the constructions are learnable from the reference, not how they hold up in fast speech.
