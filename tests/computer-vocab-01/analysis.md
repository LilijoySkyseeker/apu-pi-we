# Computer vocabulary 01: analysis

Sources: 71 standard interface strings (`ui-strings.txt`, written by Claude
from common phone and app menus) and a blind email thread about a computer
problem (`email.txt`). A fresh translator marked 36 gaps (`translation.md`).

**The translator's default was English loans** (*kamupuyutalu*, *fayilu*,
*kopi*, *insutolu*, *supamu*, *bulawusa*). The sources were English, and the
author had just flagged English focus. Checked against Mandarin, Spanish,
Russian and Arabic, most interface words are **everyday words reused**
(搜索/*buscar*/искать "seek", 删除/*borrar*/حذف "remove", 下载/*descargar*
"down-load", 设置/*ajustes* "set/adjust"), and "computer" is usually built as
"counting machine" (计算机, حاسوب, *Rechner*).

**Decision:**
- 15 everyday roots the lexicon lacked: seek, remove, share, connect, add,
  check, shake, sound, page, key/button, private, garbage, copy, adjust,
  document/file.
- 14 built words (computer, screen, password, account, settings, link,
  folder, keyboard, undo, update, scroll, upload, screenshot, spam).
- 2 loans where the form really is shared (camera, contact).
- Brands keep their own spelling.
- **doku** "work" extended to machines.

**Not done:** browser, cache and formula are left as phrases or later loans
(low frequency).
