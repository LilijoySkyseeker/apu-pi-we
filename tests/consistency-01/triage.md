## Resolved (48 raw items, 18 topics)

- Where a vocative or address goes (SA.1, SR.1, SS.1, SH.2): Direct address and titles.
- **lanno** before role, kin or nameless person words, alone in reference, respect for the person talked about, "Mr Henderson" (RH.1, RH.2, RJ.1, RS.3, SH.1, SE.14): Direct address and titles (**lanno**, "lanno before any person word").
- Clock times before 10:00, casual "at seven" (RJ.4, SA.6, SR.4, SE.13, JR.7): rule 9 (**po mape po po**; 12-hour style).
- Numbers written as digits, clock notation passing through, how labels like "day 14" are read (RS.1, RS.2, SM.1, SS.2): Writing (Numbers, Punctuation) and rule 10 (labels under 100 are counted).
- Stacking speech-act particles, rhetorical and sarcastic questions and orders (the old **a**) (RS.4, SA.3, SR.3, SS.6): rule 1 (**genso**/**ba** before another particle).
- Existential **gun** after a linker, in a subordinate clause, after a fronted phrase (SE.1, SH.7, SM.9, JR.3): rule 7 ("at the start of any clause with no subject").
- The old mechanical **wan** rule: first/second-person subjects, mentioned but never a subject (E1.1, E1.2): rule 6 (topic-based reference).
- The old limits on dropping subjects and objects; a fronted **gi** clause with no main clause (E1.3, E1.4, E1.5, GR.7): rule 11.
- "As you know", overlapping sentence-end words, "turned out" (SR.5, RJ.8, SA.8): Stance at the end of the sentence (**a**, **buni**, **donon**, low **kon** + **min**).
- **de** with "we" or an institution as subject (SE.7, SM.7): rule 1 ("**de din** … a notice from an office").
- The `names.py` interface; Hindi ʋ (E1.9, SH.11): Words, "The adapted form is the fallback" (IPA input; ʋ → w).
- Vague times of day (RH.11): rule 9 (**leso**, **minfi**, **duwun**, **kegin**, **yami**).
- Topic fronting "as for X" (SS.4): rule 8 (**pi** X,).
- **bu** for an event (GR.5): rule 6 (events take **tan**/**ben**).
- **na**/"also" before a single word or a subject (SA.7, SE.11): principle 3; Small words (**fe**).
- **kon** on a one-word echo answer (SE.15): principle 3; rule 11 example (**kon ka wo, …**).
- Which verbs take a verb object (**mido nunya**) (SE.6): Words, Verb + verb ("if the first verb can take an object").
- Indirect "how" with a **wun** clause (SE.10): rule 5 (**yu** inside a **wun** clause).
