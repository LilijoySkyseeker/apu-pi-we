# Vocabulary test 03: modern texts, new genres

**Method:** five original texts: a recipe; ticket-machine instructions (Mexico City); a race report (Nairobi); a neighbour complaint (Istanbul); a storm warning (Manila). Checked with `tools/check.py`, then read blind.

**Result:** 13 segments, essentially all correct.
- **Composition carried:** screen = "glass", language = **LIsun** "tongue" (a colexification found in many languages), coins = "round money", ticket = "paper of travel", machine = "tool", fry = "cook in fat", sugar = "small sweet thing", pepper = "hot seeds", neighbour = "person who stays near", building manager = "leader of the building", police = "keepers of the city", window = "glass of the wall", charge a phone = "make it full with power".
- **Lost to a missing word: about 1 point.** Onions came back as potatoes, from "round tubers". **onion** added.
- **Pronoun finding:** in "the runner won, and her friend came second. Thousands of fans cheered as she crossed the line", Claude wrote **wan** for the winner. By the mechanical rule, **wan** is the most recent subject, the friend; the winner, one subject further back, is **yi**. So the reader read it as the friend being cheered. That's a translator error, but a telling one: the natural pull is towards the *topic*, while the rule tracks *recency*. Second time the rule has needed care (see test 02). Open: watch in later tests; revisit if it causes errors in natural use.

**Stop condition:** not met. Losses to missing words by test: 2–3, then 1–1.5, then about 1. Two consecutive tests under 1 are needed.
