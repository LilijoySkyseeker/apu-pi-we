# Back-translation (blind reader, bt9)

## Text R (a recipe)

**R1.** tin PENsi YEgon, yen BINla bu mon Osa LAge, tun bu gun TUtin.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT wash rice, and boil it at water salty, to it is soft.
- Reading: **Wash the rice, then boil it in salted water until it's soft.**

**R2.** tin FONsu TENgi KOwan la, yen SIten bu la mon GImen.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT cut tuber round PL, and cook it PL in fat.
- Reading: **Cut up the potatoes and fry them in oil.**
- Notes: "round tubers" = potatoes; "cook in fat" = fry. (Possibly chips/fries are meant.)

**R3.** tin YAga FUyu, MUsu SInta MEli, yen YIkan TIkun in; wo tan, NEke bu TIkun.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT put meat, thing sweet small, and seed hot some; after this, give it hot.
- Reading: **Add the meat, a little sugar and some pepper; then serve it hot.**
- Notes: "small sweet thing" read as "a little sugar" (could also be "a little something sweet"); "hot seeds" = pepper / chili (peppercorns or chili flakes).

## Text I (instructions on a ticket machine)

**I1.** tin SANne DEgun li WAka LIsun pi me.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT touch glass for choose tongue of you.
- Reading: **Touch the screen to choose your language.**
- Notes: "glass" = touchscreen; "tongue" = language.

**I2.** tin YAga NImo KOwan lu MIse mon BEse, wo tan YIdu MAbo pi KObi pi me.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT put money round or card at inside, after this take paper of moving of you.
- Reading: **Insert coins or a card, then take your ticket.**
- Notes: "round money" = coins; "paper of moving/travel" = ticket.

**I3.** don YEtu mu DOku, tin MIdo no KUwi li YEma.
- Gloss: if tool not work, INSTRUCT ask one-who-keeps for help.
- Reading: **If the machine isn't working, ask a member of staff (attendant) for help.**
- Notes: "tool" = the machine; "keeper" = attendant / station staff / guard.

## Text P (a sports report)

**P1.** no GAbe se en KEnya sin YIyin GAbe, yen SOnu pi wan Obu pi SA.
- Gloss: one-who-runs from Kenya DONE win run, and friend of him come of-number 2.
- Reading: **The Kenyan runner won the race, and his teammate came second.**
- Notes: "friend" read as teammate / compatriot; "come number 2" = finished second.

**P2.** Ewo fu SA YIyo man TAna wan Oyi DIbon, nin wan LIwon mon FUgi.
- Gloss: person about 2x6^4 who like him say loud, while he arrive at end.
- Reading: **About 2,600 fans cheered as he crossed the finish line.**
- Notes: SA YIyo = 2 x 1296 = 2,592, so "about 2,600"; "people who like him" = fans; "say loudly" = cheer; "arrive at the end" = cross the finish line.
- Reference problem: by the stated rule, *wan* = the most recent clause subject, which is the teammate from P1's last clause, so strictly the fans were cheering the second-placed teammate. In a race report I'd expect the winner. The second *wan* is worse: the most recent subject before it is the crowd (Ewo), which makes no sense, so I read it as the same "him". I'm not sure who the fans were cheering.

## Text D (a complaint between neighbours)

**D1.** Ewo man PIyi GElu pi din MUge ge YUyun DIbon mon YAmi we.
- Gloss: person who stays near of us(excl) do what-is-sung loud at night all.
- Reading: **Our neighbour plays loud music every night.**
- Notes: "person who stays near us" = neighbour (the person next door); "make loud song(s)" = play loud music.

**D2.** din sin KEgu BONko tun no WUNgu pi ge SElo, un MUsu fa KUbo.
- Gloss: we(excl) DONE talk angrily to one-who-leads of what-is-built, but thing none change.
- Reading: **We complained to the building manager (landlord), but nothing changed.**
- Notes: "talk angrily to" = complain to; "leader of the building" = building manager / landlord.

**D3.** MUyi wo tan, din win YEbin no KUwi pi OTon.
- Gloss: time after this, we SOON call keeper of city.
- Reading: **Next time, we'll call the police.**
- Notes: "keeper of the city" = police (possibly city council / noise enforcement).

## Text W (a storm warning)

**W1.** LApo KONke le Obu se PUya; kon TE bu LIwon mon YAmi tan.
- Gloss: storm strong ONGOING come from sea; CONF 5/6 it arrive at night this.
- Reading: **A severe storm (maybe a hurricane) is coming in from the sea; it will very likely (about 83%) arrive tonight.**

**W2.** tin PIyi BEse, PIyi LIni se DEgun pi SENmo, yen MUge KEte LIga la pi me gan LUla.
- Gloss: INSTRUCT stay inside, stay far from glass of wall, and make full phone PL of you with power.
- Reading: **Stay indoors, keep away from windows, and charge your phones.**
- Notes: "glass of the wall" = windows; "make your phones full with power" = charge your phones.
