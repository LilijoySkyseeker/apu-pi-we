Five short modern texts. KEnya is a name.

Text R (a recipe)
R1. tin PENsi YEgon, yen BINla bu mon Osa LAge, tun bu gun TUtin.
R2. tin FONsu TENgi KOwan la, yen SIten bu la mon GImen.
R3. tin YAga FUyu, MUsu SInta MEli, yen YIkan TIkun in; wo tan, NEke bu TIkun.

Text I (instructions on a ticket machine)
I1. tin SANne DEgun li WAka LIsun pi me.
I2. tin YAga NImo KOwan lu MIse mon BEse, wo tan YIdu MAbo pi KObi pi me.
I3. don YEtu mu DOku, tin MIdo no KUwi li YEma.

Text P (a sports report)
P1. no GAbe se en KEnya sin YIyin GAbe, yen SOnu pi wan Obu pi SA.
P2. Ewo fu SA YIyo man TAna wan Oyi DIbon, nin wan LIwon mon FUgi.

Text D (a complaint between neighbours)
D1. Ewo man PIyi GElu pi din MUge ge YUyun DIbon mon YAmi we.
D2. din sin KEgu BONko tun no WUNgu pi ge SElo, un MUsu fa KUbo.
D3. MUyi wo tan, din win YEbin no KUwi pi OTon.

Text W (a storm warning)
W1. LApo KONke le Obu se PUya; kon TE bu LIwon mon YAmi tan.
W2. tin PIyi BEse, PIyi LIni se DEgun pi SENmo, yen MUge KEte LIga la pi me gan LUla.
