A short story with dialogue. MAla, TOmasa and BEne are names. Quotation marks mark speech.

S1. "pin me YIdu YUfa NImo pi lon?" MAla MIdo.
S2. "lon mu YIdu yi. TOmasa YIdu yi."
S3. "TOmasa gun mon GUyi YEsa nin DIli MINwe."
S4. "wan Obu tun GUyi mon KOse, yen Oyi wun sun WUpi NImo li SIwa KUdu."
S5. "gun NImo WIfu yu mon yi?"
S6. "lon PUme: DOla FI LUbu TI, yen ga NO MAbo SIwa KUdu pi lon."
S7. "pin mu me Udin BEya pi me?"
S8. "mu Udin. lon YAkin."
S9. "so ku YEbin wan. WIfu pi wan gun: Ome PUmu FIdi TIwe, DAsa NObi SAge, KOse Ome YAlu."
S10. BEne YEbin. TOmasa FEbo mon YEbin pi TIwe.
S11. TOmasa Iko BEne wun sun SIgo NImo mon WUma wo PUmu.
S12. "wan mu YIdu NImo," BEne Oyi. "yi YIdu MAbo."
S13. "don wan Agi MAbo ben, de lon Iko WEde."
S14. "WEde sin LUgo. TOmasa YEbin wan do."
S15. MAla BUbo. "ba yi sin MUge. yi gun ke GOle se pen."
