# Round-trip 01: Japanese → Apu Pi We

Source: `source.ja.txt`. Three speakers (A, B, C), casual friends; Kenta (健太) is absent and is the topic, so **wan** is Kenta throughout once he is named.

```
A: ねえ、今週の土曜って空いてる？
A: mon dili pi ufo pi nelon tan, min me ibe muyi.
   at day of 6 of week this, Q you have time.
```
- ねえ (attention-getter) has no word; dropped. 空いてる → "have time" (**enpa** is "free, not held or controlled", which fits less well). The って topic is carried by fronting the time phrase as scene-setting. "Saturday of this week" stacks two **pi** phrases; see ambiguities.

```
B: 土曜？んー、午後からならたぶん大丈夫。なんで？
B: min dili pi ufo. don wo leso, kon oma ibe. gi yu.
   Q day of 6. if after morning, CONF 7 have. because what.
```
- 午後 (afternoon) has no root; built as **wo leso** "after the morning". 大丈夫 answered by echoing A's verb **ibe** (rule 4). たぶん → **kon oma**. んー (hesitation) has no word; dropped.

```
A: 健太が引っ越すじゃん。手伝ってくれる人探してるらしくてさ。
A: en kenta kubo pu nume, min. fen wan le walo geno ewo la man yema wan.
   NAME kenta change place-of dwelling, TAG. TOLD he ONGOING try find person PL who help him.
```
- 引っ越す = **kubo pu nume** "change one's dwelling place". じゃん (reminding the listener of shared knowledge) → tag **min**; this is closer to "right?" than to an assertion of shared knowledge. らしい read as hearsay → **fen**. くれる (help directed to Kenta's side) is carried only by the object **wan**; the in-group "favour received" stance isn't marked.

```
B: え、もう引っ越すの？来月って言ってなかったっけ。
B: min wan win kubo pu nume. wan sin oyi wun wo dunan fi, min.
   Q he SOON change place-of dwelling. he DONE say that after month 1, TAG.
```
- もう ("already", with surprise that it's so soon) has no word; **win** "about to" carries the imminence but not the surprise. え dropped. っけ (checking a half-remembered fact) → tag **min**; the "I seem to recall" memory nuance isn't carried (no **kon** added). 言ってなかった (negative question) became a positive statement + tag, the usual way to carry that bias.

```
A: なんか大家さんの都合で早まったんだって。
A: fen tan sin lewe ke ya, gi ukun pi no ibe guyi.
   TOLD this DONE become more before, because reason of one-who have house.
```
- 大家 = **no ibe guyi** "one who owns the house". The honorific さん on a third party has no equivalent (**nun** is speaker-to-listener politeness only); not carried. 早まった → **lewe ke ya** "became more beforehand"; see ambiguities. なんか (vague hedge, "something like") dropped; **fen** carries だって. 都合 → **ukun** "reason".

```
B: マジか、大変だね。で、何時から？
B: min feko. tan gun gofa, min. yen se muyi yu.
   Q true. this is difficult, TAG. and from time what.
```
- 大変だね: ね (inviting shared feeling) → tag **min**. **muyi yu** "what time" follows the rule 11 example.

```
A: 朝九時集合って言ってた。
A: wan sin oyi wun fanmu mon mape po po.
   he DONE say that gather at 9 0 0.
```
- 朝 is implied by the 24-hour clock, so not said separately. Who gathers is left out, as in the Japanese. The time is read digit by digit as `9:00` (**mape po po**), not `09:00`.

```
B: 九時はきついわ…午前中バイト入ってるし。
B: mape po po gun gofa. gi lon doku nin leso.
   9 0 0 is difficult. because I work during morning.
```
- バイト (specifically a part-time job) → **doku** "work"; the part-time sense is lost. し (trailing reason) → a sentence opening with **gi**.

```
C: 俺行けるよ。車も出せるし。
C: lon lenu isi. yen lon lenu bone wame tale.
   I can go. and I can use also car.
```
- 俺 (casual masculine "I") → **lon**; speaker gender and register aren't carried. 車を出す ("bring one's car for the group's use") → **bone tale** "use the car". も → **wame** before the word it covers.

```
A: え、ほんと？助かる！健太絶対喜ぶわ。
A: min feko. ba tan yema. kon mape en kenta lewe sogi.
   Q true. EXCL this help. CONF 9 NAME kenta become happy.
```
- 助かる → "this helps", with no object, as in the Japanese. 絶対 → **kon mape** (9 in 10) rather than **kon yawu**: it's colloquial, so it's read as strong confidence, not certainty. Kenta is named again because the Japanese names him after C's turn.

```
C: でもさ、あいつの部屋って物多くない？前行ったとき本だらけだったじゃん。
C: un, gun musu feli mon pu nume pi wan, min. nin pen sin isi ya eda, bu gun kete gan ingo, min.
   but, there-is thing many at place-of dwelling of him, TAG. while we.incl DONE go before now, it is full with book, TAG.
```
- 部屋 has no root; **pu nume pi wan** "his dwelling place" (his flat). あいつ (slightly rough "that guy") → **wan**; the tone is lost. 多くない？ (negative question expecting yes) → statement + tag. **bu** = his place, the thing being talked about. Who went is unstated in the Japanese; B's reply shows B was there, so **pen** is used.

```
B: あー、漫画やばかったよね。壁一面。
B: manga gun bi feli, min. senmo minwe.
   manga is very many, TAG. wall whole.
```
- 漫画 is borrowed as **manga** (via `names.py`), treating manga as an international word. やばい (slang for "insane") → **bi feli** "very many": the amazed tone is only partly carried. あー (recognition) dropped. 壁一面 → the fragment "a whole wall" (rule 11).

```
A: そうそう、だから段ボール足りないって嘆いてた。
A: feko. gi tan, wan sin oyi bayi wun sun wupi [段ボール] ke feli.
   true. because this, he DONE say sadly that LOGO need [cardboard-box] more many.
```
- 段ボール bracketed: there's no "box" root, and "paper basket" (**kanga pi mabo**) would read as a basket containing paper. 足りない → "needs more". 嘆く → **oyi bayi** "say sadly". The progressive past of 嘆いてた is carried only as **sin**. そうそう → **feko**; the doubled emphasis isn't carried.

```
C: スーパーでもらってくればいいじゃん。タダだし。
C: fon wan dunu bu la se pu bika pasu, min. yen go mu unfu.
   ADVISE he get it PL from place-for buy food, TAG. and anyone NOT pay.
```
- スーパー → **pu bika pasu** "place for buying food", built rather than borrowed. もらう (receive as a favour) → **dunu** "get"; the "given to him" sense is lost. 〜てくる (go and come back) isn't carried separately. タダ → "nobody pays": **enpa** is the "not held" sense of free, so it can't be used.

```
A: それ言ったんだけど、「めんどい」って。
A: lon sin oyi tan, un wan oyi "wupi doku feli".
   I DONE say this, but he say "need work much".
```
- Rule 12 requires a say-verb, so **wan oyi** is said where Japanese drops it. めんどい → "needs a lot of work". The lazy, "can't be bothered" flavour is lost. それ → **tan**: it's what C just said, and Japanese's listener-side distance has no counterpart.

```
B: いや、引っ越しのほうがめんどいでしょ。
B: un kubo pu nume, wupi doku ke feli, min.
   but change place-of dwelling, need work more much, TAG.
```
- いや → **un** "but". でしょ → tag **min**. The comma ends the subject so that **pu nume wupi …** can't be read as one derived phrase.

```
C: ほんとそれ。じゃあ俺、金曜の帰りにもらってくるわ。
C: tan gun bi feko. gi tan, mon dili pi te, de lon dunu bu la nin sigo.
   this is very true. because this, at day of 5, INTEND I get it PL during return.
```
- 帰り → **nin sigo** "during the return" (home is left to context). わ (stating a decision) → **de**.

```
A: さすが。じゃあ私、お昼なんか買ってくね。
A: ba me gun upe. gi tan, nin isi, de lon bika pasu in li timon pi dili.
   EXCL you are good. because this, while go, INTEND I buy food some for half of daytime.
```
- さすが ("just what I'd expect of you") → "you're good!"; the "as expected" part is lost. お昼 (lunch) has no root; built as "for midday" with **timon pi dili** "half of the daytime" (judgement call; it could be read as "half a day"). 〜ていく (buy on the way) → **nin isi** "while going". 私 vs. 俺: not carried.

```
B: バイト終わったらそっち直行するから、場所送っといて。
B: wo lon fugi doku, de lon isi fongo tun kemu ben. gi tan, so sege lon kemu ya.
   after I end work, INTEND I go straight to place that. because this, REQUEST send me place beforehand.
```
- 〜ておいて (do in advance) → **ya** "beforehand". そっち → **kemu ben** "that place".

```
A: 了解、あとでLINEする。終わったらみんなでご飯行こ、健太のおごりで。
A: wo, de sege gan layin. wo fugi, so pen we isi nawi, yen en kenta unfu.
   after, INTEND send with LINE. after end, REQUEST we.incl all go eat, and NAME kenta pay.
```
- 了解 is folded into **de** + verb, the documented "will do" acknowledgment. LINE (an app brand) is borrowed via `names.py` as **layin**, without **en**, as the rule for brands says. ご飯行こ → **so pen** "let's". おごり (a treat) → "Kenta pays".

```
C: それ本人に言ってある？
C: min me sin iko tan tun wan.
   Q you DONE tell this to him.
```
- 本人 ("the person himself", as opposed to us) → **wan**; the contrast isn't marked. てある (resulting state) → **sin**.

```
A: 今から言う。
A: de iko eda.
   INTEND tell now.
```
- Answered by echoing the verb, with **de** (rule 4). The implied "not yet" isn't said, as in the Japanese.

## Rules I found ambiguous

1. Stacked pi phrases. "Saturday of this week" (**dili pi ufo pi nelon tan**): it's unclear whether the second **pi** attaches to **dili** or to the label **ufo**. There's no documented way to tie a weekday to a particular week.
2. Degree words on relation words. Does **ke ya** "more beforehand" = "earlier" work? Rule 13 shows **ke** only before adjectives, and **ya** alone is an adverb (rule 8). No documented way to say "earlier / later than planned".
3. Existential gun after a linker. Rule 7 says **gun** means "there is" only at the very start of a sentence with no subject. Does **un, gun …** count as the start?
4. Derived nouns with a verb-phrase base, followed by the clause's verb. **kubo pu nume wupi …**: since **pu** can take a whole verb phrase, the verb could be swallowed into the derived noun. I used a comma, but the pause rule lists only three places, and none covers this exactly.
5. Adjectives vs. verbs as predicates. Rule 7 requires **gun** before an adjective, but not every root is clearly an adjective or a verb (**kete** full, **sogi** happy, **feko** true). I used **gun** for the ones glossed as adjectives.
6. Tag min with fon. Is a clause-final tag **min** allowed after a non-statement speech act (**fon …, min**)?
7. Clock reading of times before 10:00. 09:00 as **mape po po** or **po mape po po**? The examples only show times with two-digit hours.
8. Combining aspect words. There's no documented way to say "was doing" in the past (**sin** + **le**), so 嘆いてた lost its progressive.
9. Interjections. Only **mm** and **huh?** exist. ねえ, え, あー, んー and いや had nowhere to go except dropping them or turning them into a linker.
