## Doubtful lines

- Line 4 (dimas, "yebin lanno en joko [X1]"): [X1] left as is. Could be a department/place ("di gudang"), "langsung" (directly), or a particle like "deh"; context doesn't settle it.

- Line 2 ("nun to gun wonwe", also line 12): literally "I am ready"; rendered as the office reply "Siap" (acknowledging the task), not as being literally ready.
- Line 11: "so to sin nagu bu ya" is a request with "I" as subject ("let me have already seen it beforehand"); "pi kemin man mu sin wonwe" read as "the file, even if it isn't finished yet". The "so" + "to" combination and the topic phrase without "wi" (even) make this the least certain line.
- Line 9: "funti pi no wungu" (group of leaders) rendered as "jajaran pimpinan"; could be directors/management.
- Line 15: "doku tason yodun" (work rather late) rendered "kerja agak malam"; could also be "lembur" (overtime).
- Line 16: "sonu" (friend) as end-of-sentence address rendered "bro"; assumes Sar and Dimas are casual peers and Dimas is male.
- Line 17: "de imu ge oyi pi me" (I will remember what you said) rendered idiomatically as "Aku pegang omonganmu"; the trailing "buni" folded into "ya".
