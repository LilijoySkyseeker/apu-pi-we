## Five most serious losses

1. **Art. 14.2, asylum exclusion trigger.** "Prosecutions genuinely arising from non-political crimes" became "if someone truthfully says that he/she has broken the law". The test moves from whether a prosecution is genuine to whether the accusation is true, or even to self-confession. The legal mechanism changes. (T)
2. **Art. 22, resource qualifier turned into a duty.** "In accordance with the organization and resources of each State" limits what each state owes. "Each country uses ... its own money for this" turns that limit into an obligation. "Social security" also became "help from all people", with a flagged give/receive reversal. (T)
3. **Art. 16.1, nationality as a ground.** "The country also cannot stop this" most naturally says the state may not restrict marriage at all. The original only bars restrictions based on nationality. (L)
4. **Art. 15.2, right to change nationality.** "Change his/her own country" reads as altering the country, not changing citizenship. The same missing word causes this. (L)
5. **Art. 29.1, duties to "his/her own group".** The community within which personality develops becomes a possibly partisan in-group, inside the article that sets out a person's duties. (T) Close runner-up: worship dropped from Art. 18. (T)

## Patterns not scored down

These were scored 2 where they appear alone, but they recur throughout.

- "Fundamental" comes back as "important".
- "Dignity" comes back as "importance (of a person)".
- "Society" comes back as "all people".
- The adjective-after-verb ambiguity ("know good and bad" or "know well and badly", Preamble ¶2 and Art. 1).

Each is a small weakening on its own. Together they are the main L/G pressure points.
