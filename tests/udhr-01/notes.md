# UDHR translation: notes

Translation: `translation.md`, 2011 words, 126 sentences. It follows `terms.md` with **dundi** (respect, honour, dignity) and **gigin** (country, nation, state). Each line below is article, what, why.

## Choices across the whole text

- All articles: "everyone has the right to X" is **go ibe sogan li X** (G1) for verbs and nouns alike. Later clauses pick up **go** with **wan** (G7), and "his own" in the same clause is **pi nen**.
- All articles: dignity is **dundi pi ewo**. When the owner is already in the clause, it fills the **pi** slot: **ibe dundi kige** (Art 1), **dundi pi wan** (Art 12, "his honour"). So honour and dignity are one word, as instructed.
- All articles: "the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration" is **sogan we pi kemin tan** "all the rights of this document". "Freedoms" is dropped (G6), and **pi** replaces a relative clause, which would attach to the wrong noun or nest. It is weaker: freedoms are only implied as rights. "These rights and freedoms" is **sogan tan la**.
- Preamble, Art 26.2: "human rights and fundamental freedoms" is **enpa bawe yen sogan pi ewo**. The order is swapped so the **pi** phrase comes last and can't take in the rest of the list.
- All articles: "the law" as an actor is **wabun la**, and "court/tribunal" is **pu pi wabun la**. The **la** is optional, but it keeps the subject noun from running into the verb (bare noun + word can read as a description, 4.2). "By law" is **fu wabun**, "before the law" is **kige li wabun**.
- Art 4, 5, 9, 15.2, 17.2: "No one shall be V-ed" is the active generic **go mu gun efon V go/ewo** "no one may V anyone" (12, and G2's form for a long V). Art 12 uses **ewo eni** "others" as the actor. G2's main form **go keya mu gun V** is not used anywhere: it can't take objects or a second coordinated verb without losing **keya mu**. This departs from G2.
- Norms without an actor stay plain statements (G3), as in Art 1, 21.3 **go waka kunsu**, and **waka tan manni muwo**. With **keya**, "everyone must vote secretly" would read as compulsory voting.
- Art 10, 11, 18: "public" for a hearing or trial is **nin ewo we lenu nagu** "while everyone can see". For a place (Art 18) it is **kemu mu kunsu**. These are two forms for an event and a place.
- Throughout: "the United Nations" is **funti pi gigin la** (7 syllables, about 10 times). "Member States" is **gigin la pi funti pi gigin la** (heavy). "International" is **pi gigin la**.
- Preamble, Art 16.3: "world" is **fowo minwe** (terms 91). **fowo** is glossed only "ground, earth or soil", so this leans on **minwe**.

## Preamble

- 1: "inherent" is **gi wan gun ewo** "because they are human", and "inalienable" is its own sentence **go mu lenu yidu bu la se wan**. "Recognition … is the foundation" is **yinki pi … gun wun ewo we tayu tan**: there's no word for foundation or basis, so it uses **yinki** "plant root" as a metaphor.
- 2: "resulted in" is an opener: **gi ewo la mu dundi …, wan la sin muge musu bi yomo**. "Contempt" is **bebu** "disgust", which names a feeling, not an attitude. "Barbarous acts" is **musu bi yomo** "very bad things", which is weak.
- 2: "Freedom of speech and belief" is **go oyi yen pume enpa**, so belief shrinks to thinking. "Freedom from want" is **ibe tonya**. "Proclaimed as the highest aspiration of the common people" is **ewo we sin oyi wun ge ganin man gun ke tisan se we, gun …**. **pi wan la** is dropped because a relative clause after it would attach to **wan la** (5.3).
- 3: the long "if man is not to be compelled …" becomes a condition: **don wabun la mu kuwi bu la, ewo la keya migi lula yomo pi no wungu, gi gun lite eni fa**. "As a last resort" is the **gi** clause.
- 4: "promote the development of friendly relations" is **walo lewe sonu pi nen nen**. "Promote" and "development" fold into **walo** "try".
- 5: "reaffirmed their faith" is **sin oyi moti … wun sun gewan**, with the logophoric **sun** (10.3). "Worth" is **bawe**.
- 6: the pledge is a quote with **de din** (G4).
- 7: "this pledge" is **ge oyi ben**, and "full realization" is **muge minwe**.
- Proclamation: G8 **gi tan,**. "Common standard of achievement" is **ge momu kige** "common goal". "Organ of society" is **funti mesun pi ewo we**. "Progressive measures, national and international" is **doku man guge mon gigin pi nen yen gigin eni**. "Territories under their jurisdiction" is **kemu man bu la wungu**. The original single sentence is split into 6.

## Articles

- 1: "born free" is **tuko nume gan enpa** (there is no root for "born"). "Endowed with reason" is **lenu pume**. "Act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood" is **muge nen nen fu faba la**, using **muge** + object + manner (4.2).
- 2.1: there is no word for discrimination, so "without distinction of any kind, such as …" becomes the list as subject + **mu kubo tan** "doesn't change this". It is split into two lists so that the relative clause (origin) and the derived word (opinion) each end their list, since both run to the end of the phrase (5.3, 5.4). "Colour" is **niki pi unne**. "Other status" is **musu eni**, which is vague. "Political or other opinion" is **ge pume pi wungu lu ge pume eni**.
- 2.2: the status clause becomes four concrete cases under **wi don**. "Territory" is **kemu**, "trust" is "another country leads it", and "limitation of sovereignty" is **ibe lula ke uyo**. The technical terms are paraphrased.
- 3: "security of person" is **sonli**.
- 4: "in all their forms" is **fu niki bo** "in any form". With **niki we**, **mu** would read "not all forms are forbidden", a scope trap. "Servitude" is **yonnu ewo li doku mu enpa**.
- 5: torture is **muge bi wekun go gan momu**. terms 51 had **wekun bi**, but degree words come first (6.2). Word order alone tells **muge go bi yomo** "treat cruelly" (object + manner) apart from **muge meli go** "degrade" (result + object). "Or punishment" is its own sentence, **tan gun feko wi li wekun pi wabun**.
- 7: "in violation of this Declaration" is **fu kemin tan mu efon**, which is loose. "Incitement" is **iko ewo eni li muge ben** "tell others to do it", which is weaker. The condition comes first so the **lu** clause can't attach to the main clause.
- 8: "effective" is focus **o** before the verb ("really fix it"). "Competent" is dropped. "Constitution" is **wabun bawe pi gigin**.
- 9: **gan ukun fa** comes last and grammatically goes with the last verb only. It covers arrest and detention only by sense.
- 10: "a criminal charge" is **waka wun min wan sin alun wabun**. "Independent and impartial" is a separate sentence with **keya gun**, because a relative clause after **pu pi wabun** would describe **wabun** (5.3). "In full equality" is **tan gun kige li ewo we**.
- 11.1: "presumed innocent" is **wabun la pume wun wan mu alun bu**. It relies on the past frame from **sin alun**, because **mu sin** means "not yet". "Until proved guilty" is its own sentence with **ya** (G10). "Public trial" and "guarantees" are separate sentences.
- 11.2: "omission" is **mu muge musu bo**. "Did not constitute an offence" becomes "the law allowed it then", and "the applicable penalty" becomes "the penalty the law allowed then".
- 12: a topic opener **pi …,** lists the protected things. "Attacks upon honour and reputation" is **oyi yomo fu dundi yen suso pi wan**, which is narrower (speech only). "Interference" is **inwa**.
- 14.2: "prosecutions genuinely arising from non-political crimes" is **ewo bo oyi feko wun wan sin alun wabun mu fu wungu**. "Purposes and principles" is **wabun yen ge momu** (the derived word goes last so it can't take in the list).
- 15: "nationality" is **ibe gigin** "have a country". terms 65's **li gun ewo pi gigin** begins a subjectless **gun** clause, which 4.5 reads as "there is". "Nor denied the right to change" is said positively: **wan fe ibe sogan li kubo gigin pi nen**.
- 16.1: "of full age" is the condition **don lowu lu futa sin guge**. "As to, during, and at the dissolution of marriage" is the range **se tuko pi bu tun fugi pi bu** (9.3).
- 16.2: "only with the consent of the intending spouses" is **na don wan la nen tayu …**.
- 16.3: "natural and fundamental group unit" is **funti meli man gun yinki pi ewo we**. "Natural" is carried only by the **yinki** metaphor.
- 18: "freedom of thought, conscience, religion" is **enpa pi pume yen lite pi nosen yen lugo pi upe yen yomo**. Here the **pi** phrase taking in the whole list gives the right meaning. Religion as **lite pi nosen** "way of god(s)" is weak for non-theistic religions (terms 25). The article is split into 5 sentences.
- 19: "hold opinions without interference" is **ewo eni mu inwa ge pume pi wan**, since **li ibe ge pume gan inwa fa** reads poorly. "Regardless of frontiers" is its own sentence, **lifin pi gigin la mu bamo ben**.
- 20.2: **go mu keya inwa funti** (G2) says no one has to join. That states that there is no duty, not that coercion is forbidden, so it is slightly weaker.
- 21.1: "directly or through representatives" is **wan nen lenu muge tan, lu no wungu man go waka enpa, lenu muge tan li wan**.
- 21.3: "basis of authority" is **obu se**. "Periodic and genuine elections" is **waka feko** plus **waka tan manni muwo**. **keya manni muwo** puts **manni** inside a verb chain, so it isn't used.
- 22: "economic, social and cultural rights" is **sogan man gun fu nimo yen ewo we yen lite pi nume pi funti**. A relative clause is needed because **fu** can't attach inside a noun phrase (4.4). **sogan pi ewo we** is unusable because it means "rights of all people" (the title). "Entitled to realization" is **keya ibe**. "Personality development" is **guge enpa**.
- 23.3: "remuneration ensuring an existence worthy of human dignity" becomes two sentences: "fair pay" and then "that money must be enough to live with dignity".
- 24: "reasonable limitation of working hours" is **mu keya doku awuwa bi feli** "need not work very many hours".
- 25.1: "medical care and necessary social services" are merged into **ewo we yema wan la nin wan la simi lu wupi bu**. The list of risks moves to an opener so its **lu** clauses can't attach to the main clause. "Beyond his control" is **yen wan mu lenu kubo ben**.
- 25.2: "motherhood and childhood" is **mole yen tiwu**. "Special" is **ke feli**. "Born out of wedlock" is **mole yen wede … mu gun ofin**.
- 26.1: "right to education" is **sogan li yesa** "right to learn". **li gun tofo** hits the 4.5 "there is" reading, and **li tofo** is "to teach". "Compulsory" is **tiwu we keya yesa mon pu yesa**. "Technical and professional" is **tofo pi doku** (terms 41 had **li**, but **li** can't attach inside a noun phrase, 4.4).
- 26.2: "racial or religious groups" gets its own sentence. "Tolerance" is **efon wun eni pume eni** (terms 83).
- 26.3: "their children" is **tiwu pi nen**. **pi wan la** would mean someone else's children (10.3).
- 27.1: "share in scientific advancement and its benefits" is **dunu upe pi guge pi lugo**, which loses "take part in".
- 27.2: "moral and material interests" is **upe pi wan yen nimo …**. "Material" narrows to money.
- 28: "an order in which …" is **yile pi ewo we yen gigin la** plus a new sentence **mon yile tan, …**, because a relative clause would attach to **gigin la** (5.3).
- 29.1: reordered: "only in a group can anyone grow freely and fully. **gi tan**, everyone has duties to his group."
- 29.2: "democratic society" is **ewo we man waka no wungu pi nen**. "Just requirements of morality, public order and general welfare" is **kuwi lite upe yen bonwi yen upe pi ewo we fu denku**.
- 29.3: "in no case" is **mu mu**, and "contrary to" is **don ben alun**.
- 30: "nothing in this Declaration may be interpreted as implying" is **fa pi kemin tan yofu wun**.

### Several readings (56)

Causes:
- **L**: **li** + a single content word is counted as both purpose (**li** + verb) and relation (**li** + noun). The meaning is the same.
- **R**: a trailing relation phrase or clause attaches either inside the **li/wun/don** clause or to the main verb. 4.4 settles clauses (innermost), but not bare relation phrases. The meaning is the same in context.
- **Y**: the scope of **yen/lu** after a relation phrase or **pi** phrase isn't defined. Usually harmless, sometimes a real reading (for example Art 16.1 "…right to marry, and (he) starts a family").
- **C**: the parser reads the predicate after a heavy subject + pause as a call (Art 2.1, both sentences).

## Worst problems

1. **There is no working form for "No one shall be V-ed."** G2's **go keya mu gun V** can't take objects, coordination or "as a slave". The active **go mu gun efon V go** works, but it recasts every protection as a ban on doers, and the parser rejects it. Six articles depend on it (4, 5, 9, 12, 15.2, 17.2).
2. **The scope of yen/lu after a relation phrase or pi phrase is undefined.** "A pi B yen C" and "sogan li X yen Y" have two structures, and the reference only says what **pi** attaches to on the left. Every list had to be ordered by hand: **pi** item last, derived word last, relative clause last. This causes most of the multi-readings.
3. **"Right to be X" is blocked.** **li gun X** starts a subjectless **gun** clause, which 4.5 makes "there is". Nationality, education and security had to become **ibe gigin**, **yesa** and **sonli**, which is different wording from terms.md.
4. **A relative clause after a pi phrase can't describe the head (5.3), and relative clauses and derived words run to the end of their phrase.** These forced sentence splits in the Preamble, Art 10, 21.3 and 28, and list reordering in Art 2, 14, 19 and 25.
5. **Key abstractions are paraphrased weakly:**
   - religion as "way of god(s)";
   - barbarous as "very bad";
   - contempt as "disgust";
   - incitement as "tell to do";
   - attack on honour as "speak badly about";
   - material interests as money;
   - standard of achievement as "goal";
   - sovereignty and trust territory spelled out case by case;
   - "natural" (family) only through the **yinki** metaphor.

## Revision 2

This revision works from two reviews: `scores.md` (round trip; items are its table rows, cause T/L/G) and `relecture-fr.md` (French review; items are its # column). Where it conflicts with the notes above, this section wins. The reference doesn't decide two questions: (a) whether a **pi** phrase before or after a **yen/lu** list covers the whole list, and (b) whether a phrase at the end of a clause covers several joined verbs or only the last. So no sentence below depends on either. The fixes repeat the verb on each item, put conditions first as **don** openers, split sentences, or make a list the subject.
