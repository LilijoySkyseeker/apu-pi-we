# Document of the rights of all people

## Preamble

Each person of the family of all people has the same rights and the respect [honour] of a person, because he/she is a person. No one can take them from him/her. The root of freedom, of what is right [correctness], and of peace of the whole earth [the world] is that all people agree on this.

Because people did not respect the rights of persons and were disgusted by them, they did very bad things. [Alternative: "did not respect the rights of persons and [did not] despise them", with "not" covering both verbs.] These things made the knowledge of good and bad of all people angry [i.e. outraged everyone's sense of good and bad]. All people have said that the thing hoped for that is higher than everything is a whole earth where people speak and think freely, are not afraid, and have enough.

The power of law must keep [protect] the rights of persons. If the laws do not keep them, people must fight the bad power of the leaders, because there is no other way.

Countries must try to become friends of each other.

The people of the group of countries have said again in its document [the document of "it"; presumably the group's own document] that they trust in the important rights of a person, in the respect and the importance of a person, and in the same rights of men and women. They have also chosen to help so that all people grow and live better, with more freedom.

The countries of the group of countries have said: "We will work with the group of countries so that all people respect and keep the important freedoms and the rights of persons in all places."

A shared [same] understanding of these rights is the most important thing [more important than all], so that the countries can make wholly real what they said [alternative: can wholly do what they said].

So, the gathering of the group of countries tells the document of the rights of all people to all people. It is the same goal [thing wanted] of all people and all countries. Each person and each group of all people must remember this document at all times. They must try, with teaching and learning, to make people respect these rights more. They must also try, with work that grows in their own country and in other countries [what they try is left out; from context, to make people respect these rights]. The goal of this is that all people agree on these rights and truly keep them, in the countries of the group of countries and in the places that they [lit. "it" plural, i.e. the countries] lead.

## Article 1

All people begin to live with freedom, and have the same respect and the same rights. They can think and have knowledge of good and bad, and should treat each other like siblings [lit. "do each other like siblings"].

## Article 2

Everyone has all the rights of this document. The kind of person, or the kind of skin, or being a man or a woman, or language, or religion [lit. "way of God"], or the country or group that he/she comes from, does not change this. His/her things, or his/her start of life [birth], or other things, or thoughts about leading [political opinions], or other thoughts, also do not change this.

Even if his/her country or place is free, or another country leads it, or it does not lead itself, or it has less power in some other way, this too does not change [alternative: that too does not change it].

## Article 3

Everyone has the right to life, freedom and safety.

## Article 4

No one may own a person or hold a person for unfree work [alternative: hold a person in order to work unfreely]. Owning people, and buying and selling people, is not allowed in any form.

## Article 5

No one may make anyone feel very great pain on purpose. Nor may anyone treat people very badly, or not as a person, or make people small [belittle them]. This is true even for the pain of the law [punishment].

## Article 6

Everyone has the right that the laws regard [lit. "think"] him/her as a person in all places.

## Article 7

All people are the same before [lit. "for"] the law, and have the right that the laws keep them equally. If someone does not treat them equally, in a way this document does not allow, or tells other people to do that, all people also have the right that the laws keep them equally.

## Article 8

If someone breaks an important right that the important law of the country or another law gives people, he/she [most likely the one whose right was broken; grammatically could be the one who broke it] has the right that the place of law [court] of the country really makes that good [puts it right].

## Article 9

No one may take a person, or hold a person, or make a person leave his/her country, without any reason.

## Article 10

If the courts [lit. "places of law"] decide about a person's rights and the things he/she must do, or decide whether he/she broke the law, he/she has the right that the courts listen to him/her correctly [fairly] while all people can see. These courts must be free and the same for all [alternative: "the places of these laws"]. This is the same for all people.

## Article 11

1. If someone says that a person broke the law, that person has the right that the laws think he/she did not break it. This is true until [lit. "before"] the courts show by law that he/she broke it. The courts must listen to him/her while all people can see. They also must give him/her all the things that he/she needs to keep [defend] himself/herself.

2. If a person did something or did not do something, and the law of the country or the law of the countries allowed that at that time, the courts must not say that he/she broke the law. The courts also must not give him/her a pain heavier than the pain that the law allowed at that time.

## Article 12

As for a person's private things, or his/her family, or his/her house, or the things he/she sends, other people may not enter them without any reason. Other people also may not speak badly about his/her honour [respect] and name. Everyone has the right that the laws keep him/her from that.

## Article 13

1. Everyone has the right to move and live freely inside each country.

2. Everyone has the right to leave any [lit. "each"] country, even his/her own country. He/she also has the right to return to his/her own country.

## Article 14

1. Everyone has the right to seek and have a safe place in another country if someone hunts him/her.

2. No one can use this right if someone truly says that he/she broke a law, not about leading [i.e. for a non-political matter; unclear what "not about leading" attaches to]. No one can use this right either if someone truly says that he/she did things that break the law and the goals of the group of countries [alternative: "the laws and goals of the group of countries"].

## Article 15

1. Everyone has the right to have a country.

2. No one may take a person's country from him/her without any reason. He/she also has the right to change his/her own country.

## Article 16

1. When [if] a man or a woman has grown up, he/she has the right to marry and start a family. Kind of person, country or religion cannot stop this. Men and women have the same rights in marriage, from its start to its end.

2. Two people can marry only if they themselves agree freely and wholly.

3. The family is a small group that is the root of all people, and it has the right that all people and the country keep it.

## Article 17

1. Everyone has the right to have things with no other person [alone] or with other people.

2. No one may take a person's things from him/her without any reason.

## Article 18

Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, of religion, and of knowledge of good and bad [alternative: "to freedom of thought, and to religion, and to knowledge of good and bad"]. This right is also the right to change religion or belief [lit. "what is thought true"; alternative: "true thought"]. He/she also has the right to show his/her religion or belief freely, alone or with other people. He/she can do that in a private place or a non-private place. This is true when he/she teaches them, or does them, or keeps them, or speaks to God.

## Article 19

Everyone has the right to freedom of thoughts [opinions] and of speech [alternative: "to freedom of opinion, and to speech"]. This right is also the right that other people do not enter his/her thoughts. He/she also has the right to seek, get and share ideas and news with any means of telling. The between of countries [the spaces between countries, presumably borders] does not stop that.

## Article 20

1. Everyone has the right to gather freely, with peace. He/she also has the right to enter groups freely.

2. No one has to enter a group [alternative reading in English terms: "no one must enter a group"; the grammar gives "not have to", not a prohibition].

## Article 21

1. Everyone has the right to lead his/her own country with other people. He/she can do this himself/herself, or leaders whom people choose freely can do this for him/her.

2. Everyone has the same right to enter the work of the leaders in his/her own country.

3. The power of the leaders must come from what the people of the country want. People must show this will by truly choosing the leaders, and this choosing usually [regularly] happens [unclear whether "must" carries over: "and this choosing must happen regularly"]. During this choosing, all people have the same right to choose, and people choose privately, or choose in another way that is as free as that.

## Article 22

Everyone is one of all the people, and has the right to help from all people. He/she also must have the rights that are about money, about all people, and about the way of living of the group. He/she needs them in order to grow freely and live with the respect of a person. His/her country and the countries must work with each other for this, according to the way, the things and the money of each country.

## Article 23

1. Everyone has the right to work and to choose freely his/her own work. He/she also has the right to work in a correct [fair] and good way, and all people must keep him/her from not having work [unclear whether this last clause is part of the right or a separate statement].

2. Everyone has the right to the same money for the same work. This is the same for all.

3. A person who works has the right to correct [fair] and good money. This money must be enough for him/her and his/her family to live with the respect of a person. If needed, all people also must help him/her in other ways.

4. Everyone has the right to build and enter groups of workers to keep his/her own good.

## Article 24

Everyone has the right to stop work [rest] and have free time. This also means that he/she does not have to work very many hours, and usually has free days with money.

## Article 25

1. Everyone and his/her family have the right to have enough for a good body and a good life. This also means that they have food, clothes and a house, and that all people help them when they are sick or need it. If he/she has no work, or is sick, or cannot work, or is old, or his/her spouse has died, or does not have enough in some other way, and he/she cannot change that, he/she also has the right to be safe.

2. Mothers and children have the right to more keeping and help [alternative: "to keeping, and to more help"]. All children have the right that all people keep them equally, even if their mother and father are not spouses.

## Article 26

1. Everyone has the right to learn. No one has to pay for at least the teaching of children. All children must learn at a place for learning. All people must be able to get teaching for work. All people also must be able to enter high teaching equally, according to their own ability.

2. Teaching must help a person to grow wholly. It also must help people to respect more the important freedoms and the rights of persons. It must help all countries to understand each other, be friends, and allow others to think other things. This is also true for all groups of kind of person or of religion. It also must help the work of the group of countries to keep peace.

3. Mothers and fathers have the right to choose the kind of teaching of their own children before other people [i.e. ahead of others; alternative: earlier in time than others].

## Article 27

1. Everyone has the right to make the way of living of his/her own group free, with other people [this is the strict reading of "muge" + quality + object; alternative and more natural: "to freely practise the way of living of his/her own group with other people"]. He/she also has the right to enjoy things made for beauty. He/she also has the right to get the good of the growth of knowledge.

2. If a person has made a thing of knowledge, or a written thing, or a thing made for beauty, he/she has the right that the laws keep his/her good and the money he/she gets from it.

## Article 28

Everyone has the right to a system of all people and of the countries. In this system, all the rights of this document can be wholly true.

## Article 29

1. A person can grow freely and wholly only in a group. So, a person has things he/she must do for his/her own group.

2. When a person uses his/her own rights, only the laws can stop him/her. The laws can do that only so that people agree with and respect the rights of other people, and keep good ways, peace and the good of all people, correctly, among all people who choose their own leaders [unclear who "keeps": the people, or the laws].

3. No one can ever [emphatic "not"] use these rights if that breaks the law and the goals of the group of countries [alternative: "the laws and goals of the group of countries"].

## Article 30

Nothing [lit. "none"] of this document means that any country, any group or any person has the right to do anything in order to break one of the rights of this document.
