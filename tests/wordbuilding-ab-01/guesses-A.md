## Notes on the uncertain ones
- W5 has no marker: "person from sky" fits angel (traditional) and alien (modern) about equally.
- W13, W20, W22, W38: **ge** covers both "the one it's done to" and "what it makes", so person and thing readings compete (lesson/student, gift/recipient).
- W33/W38: YONnu also means "hold back" in the reference (**lon YONnu nen**), which points to guard/prisoner; the literal "holder / thing held" (e.g. handle) is just as possible.
