Role column: Y/N says whether the best guess has the right role.

## 4. Where the schemes differ

Only 5 of the 38 items scored differently. Two of those differences come from the schemes themselves, and they cancel out.

1. **W13 student (B +1). This is a scheme effect.** A's `ge` covers both "the one it's done to" and "what it makes", so `ge TOfo` read as "what is taught", which is "lesson". B's `Ewo gun TOfo` says "person" outright, so the student reading was forced. This was A's only role error. Reader A flagged the same ambiguity on W20 (recipient), W22 and W38, and got those right only by picking the more common reading. Generic heads carry animacy; the single patient marker doesn't.
2. **W28 product (A +2). This is a scheme effect, partly from how each marker was explained.** A's marker was explained as "what it makes", which points to "product". B's `gun` was explained as "resulting state / thing that is done", which points to "deed". MUge means both "do" and "make", and each scheme's wording picked one sense.
3. **W5 alien (A +1). The forms differ in a second way, so this isn't a fair comparison.** Neither scheme has a marker here: both use `Ewo`. But A's form has `se` ("from") and B's doesn't. "Person from sky" led to angel, with alien as an alternative. "Person sky" led to pilot. The difference comes from the missing preposition, not from the markers.
4. **W33 guard (B +1). This looks like reader variance.** Both forms are "agent + hold". Reader A took the literal "holder" and listed guard as an alternative. Reader B went for "guard". Neither marker steers toward the right sense.
5. **W3 heater (A +1). This is reader variance.** "Tool hot" and "thing hot" are equally informative, and B simply chose stove over heater.

Both readers missed the same two items: W9 (construction site rather than factory) and W10 (statement rather than message). These misses come from what the verbs mean, not from either scheme.

**Bottom line.** A scored 71 and B 69, with role accuracy tied at 37/38. With one reader per scheme, a 2-point gap is noise. The generic-head scheme decodes about as well as dedicated markers and resolves person versus thing better for patients. The A forms are shorter: `ge` against `MUsu gun` / `Ewo gun`, and `du` against `MUsu`.
