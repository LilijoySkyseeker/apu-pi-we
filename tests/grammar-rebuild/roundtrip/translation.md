# Roundtrip: Japanese → Apu Pi We

Source: `source.txt`. Each block: Japanese, Apu Pi We, English word-by-word gloss.

Gloss abbreviations: Q = **min** (question), REQ = **so**, INTEND = **de**, ADVISE = **fon**, EXCL = **ba**, TOP = topic **pi** (as for), NAME = **en**, CONF.n = **kon** + n sixths, INFERRED = **wemen**, HEARSAY = **inwun**, DONE = **sin**, ONGOING = **le**, ABOUT.TO = **buntu**, FOCUS = **o**, PL = **la**, LOGO = **sun**, "you.know" = **a**, "okay?" = **buni**, ", right?" = tag **min**.

## 1

```
A: ねえ、今週の土曜って空いてる？
A: [X1], pi dili pi ufo pi nelon tan, min me ibe muyi.
   [X1], TOP day of 6 of week this, Q you have time.
```

- 土曜って (topic って) → topic phrase **pi …,**. Saturday = **dili pi ufo** (Monday = 1).
- 空いてる → "have time"; **enpa** ("free, not held or controlled") reads as not being confined.

## 2

```
B: 土曜？んー、午後からならたぶん大丈夫。なんで？
B: min dili pi ufo. [X2], don se duwun, kon da gun batu fa. gi yu.
   Q day of 6. [X2], if from afternoon, CONF.4 there.is problem none. because what.
```

- たぶん → **kon da** (4 in 6). 大丈夫 → "there is no problem" (**gun** with no subject = there is).
- なんで → **gi yu** with no **min**, as in the reference's **yu** examples.

## 3

```
A: 健太が引っ越すじゃん。手伝ってくれる人探してるらしくてさ。
A: en kenta kubo pu nume, a. wemen wan le ditun ewo man yema wan, [X3].
   NAME Kenta change place live, you.know. INFERRED he ONGOING seek person who help him, [X3].
```

- じゃん → **a** (shared ground). らしい → **wemen**, kept apart from だって (turn 5, **inwun**).
- くれる: only the direction (help *him*) is carried, by **yema wan**.

## 4

```
B: え、もう引っ越すの？来月って言ってなかったっけ。
B: [X4], min wan kubo pu nume ganna. kon ka wan sin oyi wun sun kubo pu nume wo dunan fi, min.
   [X4], Q he change place live early. CONF.3 he DONE say that LOGO change place live after month 1, right?
```

- もう → **ganna** "before the expected time".
- っけ → low confidence plus the tag, as the reference prescribes. The speaker is unstated in Japanese. I read it as Kenta (**wan**) and wrote the subject because dropping it would be unclear. **sun** marks Kenta as the reported speaker.

## 5

```
A: なんか大家さんの都合で早まったんだって。
A: inwun sin lewe ganna gi ukun in pi lanno no ibe guyi.
   HEARSAY DONE become early because reason some of HON one.who have house.
```

- だって → **inwun**. 大家さん → **lanno no ibe guyi** (さん → **lanno**).
- なんか (vagueness) → **in** "some" on 都合. The subject (the move) is dropped, as in Japanese.

## 6

```
B: マジか、大変だね。で、何時から？
B: ba feko. tan gun gofa, buni. yen, se muyi yu.
   EXCL true. this is difficult, okay?. and, from time what.
```

- マジか is self-directed, not a question, so it's **ba** and not **min**. ね → **buni**. で → linker **yen**.

## 7

```
A: 朝九時集合って言ってた。
A: wan sin oyi wun fanmu mon mape pi leso.
   he DONE say that gather at 9 of morning.
```

- 朝九時 → 12-hour style, **mape pi leso**.

## 8

```
B: 九時はきついわ…午前中バイト入ってるし。
B: pi mape, bu gun gofa, [X5]. gi to ibe doku pi timon nin leso.
   TOP 9, it is difficult, [X5]. because I have work of half during morning.
```

- 九時は (contrastive topic) → **pi mape,**. バイト → compound **doku pi timon**. Reason し → linker **gi**, with the main clause supplied by context.

## 9

```
C: 俺行けるよ。車も出せるし。
C: to lenu isi, [X6]. yen, lenu peton fe tale.
   I can go, [X6]. and, can share also car.
```

- 車も → **fe tale** ("the car too"). 出す → **peton** "let others have too". Additive し → **yen**.

## 10

```
A: え、ほんと？助かる！健太絶対喜ぶわ。
A: [X4], min feko. ba tan yema. kon ufo en kenta lewe sogi, [X5].
   [X4], Q true. EXCL this help. CONF.6 NAME Kenta become happy, [X5].
```

- 絶対 → **kon ufo** (it's a certainty claim, not emphasis).

## 11

```
C: でもさ、あいつの部屋って物多くない？前行ったとき本だらけだったじゃん。
C: un [X3], pi fudon pi wan, gun musu feli, min. nin sin isi ya, bu gun kete gan ingo, a.
   but [X3], TOP room of him, there.is thing many, right?. while DONE go before, it is full with book, you.know.
```

- 物多くない？ (negative question asking for agreement) → tag **min**.

## 12

```
B: あー、漫画やばかったよね。壁一面。
B: [X7], gun [X8] bi feli, min. mon senmo minwe.
   [X7], there.is [X8] very many, right?. at wall whole.
```

- やばい here means an extreme amount → **bi feli**. よね → tag **min** (ね alone → **buni**).

## 13

```
A: そうそう、だから段ボール足りないって嘆いてた。
A: tan gun feko. gi tan, wan sin tayin wun sun wupi ke nansa pi mabo.
   this is true. because this, he DONE complain that LOGO need more box of paper.
```

- 足りない → "needs more". 段ボール → **nansa pi mabo**. 嘆く → **tayin** "complain".

## 14

```
C: スーパーでもらってくればいいじゃん。タダだし。
C: fon wan isi li dunu bu la mon pu bika pasu, a. gi go mu unfu.
   ADVISE he go for get it PL at place buy food, you.know. because anyone not pay.
```

- スーパー → **pu bika pasu** "place for buying food". **pu bika kudu** would be ambiguous (big place, or buying big things).
- ればいい → **fon**. タダ → compound **go mu unfu**.

## 15

```
A: それ言ったんだけど、「めんどい」って。
A: to sin oyi tan, un wan oyi "[X9]".
   I DONE say this, but he say "[X9]".
```

- The quote needs a say-verb (rule 12), so Japanese's bare って gets **wan oyi**.

## 16

```
B: いや、引っ越しのほうがめんどいでしょ。
B: un, pi kubo pu nume, bu gun ke [X9], min.
   but, TOP change place live, it is more [X9], right?
```

- いや (rejecting Kenta's reason, not answering a question) → **un**. ほうが → **ke**, with the standard left out. でしょ → tag **min**.
- The topic phrase keeps **gun** from following the verb **nume** directly.

## 17

```
C: ほんとそれ。じゃあ俺、金曜の帰りにもらってくるわ。
C: tan gun bi feko. gi tan, pi to, nin sigo mon dili pi te, de isi li dunu bu la, [X5].
   this is very true. because this, TOP I, while return at day of 5, INTEND go for get it PL, [X5].
```

- 俺、 (fronted, with a pause) → topic **pi to,**. Friday = **dili pi te**.

## 18

```
A: さすが。じゃあ私、お昼なんか買ってくね。
A: [X10]. gi tan, pi to, de bika pasu in pi minfi yen dinyo bu, buni.
   [X10]. because this, TOP I, INTEND buy food some of noon and carry it, okay?
```

- なんか ("or something") → **in**. ～てく → **yen dinyo bu** "and carry it".

## 19

```
B: バイト終わったらそっち直行するから、場所送っといて。
B: wo doku pi timon, to isi fongo tun kemu ben. gi tan, so sege kemu ya.
   after work of half, I go straight to place that. because this, REQ send place beforehand.
```

- ～ておく → **ya** "beforehand". The recipient is left out, as in Japanese.

## 20

```
A: 了解、あとでLINEする。終わったらみんなでご飯行こ、健太のおごりで。
A: lafi. de sege bu gan en line wo. wo fugi, so pen we isi li nawi gan en kenta unfu.
   understand. INTEND send it with NAME LINE after. after end, REQ we.incl all go for eat with NAME Kenta pay.
```

- LINE is a brand, so it's a name: **en line**. 行こ → **so pen**. おごりで → **gan** + clause "with Kenta paying".

## 21

```
C: それ本人に言ってある？
C: min me sin iko tan tun o wan.
   Q you DONE tell this to FOCUS him.
```

- 本人 ("him himself", as opposed to others) → **o wan**.

## 22

```
A: 今から言う。
A: de buntu iko wan.
   INTEND ABOUT.TO tell him.
```

- 今から → **buntu**. **iko wan eda** would read "tell him 'now'" (recipient + thing).

## [X] meanings

- **[X1]** ねえ: interjection that calls for the listener's attention ("hey"), with no name or title.
- **[X2]** んー: hesitation filler while thinking ("hmm").
- **[X3]** さ: casual particle, sentence-final or after a linker, that lightly asserts and keeps the floor ("like", "y'know"), with no claim of shared knowledge.
- **[X4]** え: interjection of surprise or disbelief at what was just heard.
- **[X5]** わ: sentence-final particle of mild self-directed assertion (voicing one's own reaction or decision).
- **[X6]** よ: sentence-final particle that tells the listener something new to them ("I'm telling you").
- **[X7]** あー: interjection of recognition ("oh yeah, I remember").
- **[X8]** 漫画: comics / manga. No word for picture or drawing, so it can't be composed.
- **[X9]** めんどい: tedious, a hassle, not worth the effort (not "difficult").
- **[X10]** さすが: praise that something is just what one expected of the person ("that's so you", "as expected").

## Ambiguous rules

1. Fillers and interjections (ねえ, んー, え, あー, interjectory さ): the reference names a place only for stance words (sentence end) and **mm**/**huh?**. Where a new filler goes, at the start or after a linker, is unspecified.
2. A modifier after a compound with a verb in it (**kubo pu nume ganna**): does it modify the whole compound or only **nume**?
3. **gun** after a compound ending in a verb (**kubo pu nume gun …**) could be read as "to be" after another verb (rule 7).
4. Chained **pi** (**dili pi ufo pi nelon tan**): it's unclear whether the second **pi** attaches to **dili** or to **ufo**.
5. Whether **bu** may refer to an event (the move), or only **tan**/**ben** can.
6. Where **bo** "unspecified" goes on a noun: before it, like an operator, or after it, as in **kon bo**. I avoided it and used **in**.
7. A sentence opening with **gi** + clause and the main clause left out (rule 11 vs rule 15).
8. Past progressive (～てた): whether **sin** and **le** can combine is undefined. I used **sin** alone.
9. **fanmu** "gather" has no second gloss, so it could mean collect or assemble.
10. Whether **min** is needed or allowed on **yu** questions. The examples omit it.
11. Whether a doubled content word (そうそう → **feko feko**?) follows principle 5. All the examples are grammar words.
12. **yen** after an object noun (**pasu in pi minfi yen dinyo bu**) could read as a noun list ("noon and …") rather than as joining two clauses.
13. The scope of an afterthought (健太のおごりで): a pause is allowed only in the three listed places, so the afterthought comma was dropped.
