Four short texts. KOko is a name.

Text M (a sign in a science museum)
M1. WIno pi Uwan ben sin KUsa bu MUyi NEtu ya gun Ewo la.
M2. FOwo pi pen DUko MUyi FI mon DIli we, yen Isi KOwan mon GElu pi GIwe MUyi FI mon FAwu we.

Text P (a post online)
P1. ba TOtu sin Agi! wan gun MEli yen PInda, yen FEbo tun en KOko.
P2. Ewo in sin NAgu wan mon GElu pi pu BIka PAni, mon LEso tan.
P3. so ku SEge tan tun Ewo Eni la. LOta, Ewo we!

Text H (from a history book)
H1. mon MUyi LIni, NOso KUdu pi DIgan sin DINyo BUNwu yen LUtu BIlun se KEmu pi DIgan tun OTon KUdu la pi MONna.
H2. no KOda la IBe NImo FEli, yen no LUgo la Obu se KEmu LIni li YEsa mon KEmu ben.

Text R (a hotel review)
R1. KEmu BEse sin gun INan, yen SOti gun TUtin, un SOla gun DIbon mon YAmi.
R2. PAsu pi LEso sin gun bi Upe. de lon SIgo.
