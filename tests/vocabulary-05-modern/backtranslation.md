# Back-translation, test bt11

All content words were found in lexicon.tsv. KOko is a name (article **en**).

## Text M (a sign in a science museum)

**M1.** WIno pi Uwan ben sin KUsa bu MUyi NEtu ya gun Ewo la.

- Gloss: light OF star THAT DONE leave IT time long BEFORE BE person PL
- Reading: **The light from that star left it long before there were any people (humans).**
- Note: "ya gun Ewo la" = "before [there] were people", with subjectless **gun** read as "there is".

**M2.** FOwo pi pen DUko MUyi FI mon DIli we, yen Isi KOwan mon GElu pi GIwe MUyi FI mon FAwu we.

- Gloss: ground OF WE.incl turn time one AT day ALL, AND go round AT near OF sun time one AT year ALL
- Reading: **Our Earth spins once a day and goes around (orbits) the sun once a year.**
- Note: "FOwo pi pen" ("our ground") = the Earth, our planet. "Isi KOwan mon GElu pi GIwe" ("go round near the sun") = orbit the sun.

## Text P (a post online)

**P1.** ba TOtu sin Agi! wan gun MEli yen PInda, yen FEbo tun en KOko.

- Gloss: EXCLAIM dog DONE lose! HE/SHE BE small AND white, AND answer TO NAME Koko
- Reading: **Lost dog! He's (she's) small and white, and answers to the name Koko.**

**P2.** Ewo in sin NAgu wan mon GElu pi pu BIka PAni, mon LEso tan.

- Gloss: person SOME DONE see HIM/HER AT near OF PLACE buy old, AT morning THIS
- Reading: **Someone saw him near the old market this morning.**
- Note: "pu BIka" ("place for buying") = a shop or market. PAni "old" most likely modifies the place ("the old market"); a less likely reading is "place for buying old things" = an antique or second-hand shop.

**P3.** so ku SEge tan tun Ewo Eni la. LOta, Ewo we!

- Gloss: REQUEST POLITE send THIS TO person other PL. thank, person ALL
- Reading: **Please share this with other people. Thanks, everyone!**

## Text H (from a history book)

**H1.** mon MUyi LIni, NOso KUdu pi DIgan sin DINyo BUNwu yen LUtu BIlun se KEmu pi DIgan tun OTon KUdu la pi MONna.

- Gloss: AT time far, animal big OF sand DONE carry salt AND stone yellow FROM place OF sand TO city big PL OF south
- Reading: **Long ago, camels carried salt and gold across the desert to the great cities of the south.**
- Notes: "MUyi LIni" ("far time") = long ago. "NOso KUdu pi DIgan" ("big animal of the sand") = camels. "LUtu BIlun" ("yellow stone") = gold. "KEmu pi DIgan" ("place of sand") = the desert (probably the Sahara; literally "from the desert to the cities").

**H2.** no KOda la IBe NImo FEli, yen no LUgo la Obu se KEmu LIni li YEsa mon KEmu ben.

- Gloss: AGENT sell PL have money many, AND AGENT know PL come FROM place far FOR learn AT place THAT
- Reading: **The merchants grew rich, and scholars came from far-off lands to study there.**
- Notes: "no KOda" ("one who sells") = merchants or traders. "no LUgo" ("one who knows") = scholars or learned men. "IBe NImo FEli" = had a lot of money, i.e. were rich.

## Text R (a hotel review)

**R1.** KEmu BEse sin gun INan, yen SOti gun TUtin, un SOla gun DIbon mon YAmi.

- Gloss: place inside DONE BE clean, AND bed BE soft, BUT road BE loud AT night
- Reading: **The room was clean and the bed was comfortable (soft), but the street was noisy at night.**
- Note: "KEmu BEse" ("inside place") = the room.

**R2.** PAsu pi LEso sin gun bi Upe. de lon SIgo.

- Gloss: food OF morning DONE BE VERY good. INTEND I return
- Reading: **Breakfast was very good. I'll be back (I'll stay here again).**
- Note: "PAsu pi LEso" ("morning food") = breakfast.
