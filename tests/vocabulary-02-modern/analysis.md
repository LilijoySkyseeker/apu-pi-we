# Vocabulary test 02: modern urban texts

**Method:** five original short texts in modern settings: a Lagos market call, a Seoul subway announcement, a Mumbai office email, a São Paulo clinic, a Cairo news item. Calendar words were avoided while senary's calendar is reworked. Translated with the 439-root lexicon, using built words and descriptions for missing world-layer concepts. A fresh blind reader got only the reference and word lists. `tools/check.py` caught one stale form before the test.

**Result:** 13 segments, essentially all correct in content and act. Modern abstractions composed well:
- expensive = "needs much money"; price = "money of";
- government = "the leaders";
- meeting = "gathering of the group";
- sales report = "what is told about what is sold";
- laptop = "small counting tool";
- nurse = "one who helps sick people"; prescription = "paper of the thing for sickness";
- pill = "small thing for sickness";
- blood pressure = "push of blood";
- protest = "talk angrily";
- a mobile-money transfer, and room 4.

**Lost to missing words, about 1–1.5 points:**
- City Hall came back as Parliament, from "house of the leaders".
- Bread came back as grain, from "food of seed".

Added on demand: **city** (*OTon*), **bread** (*SOwe*). Both are frequent across cultures in modern text.

**Grammar finding:** "road above the river" (a bridge) was understood by sense, but the attachment rule says a bare place phrase after a noun belongs to the verb. Rule added: give a noun a location with a relative clause (**man gun mon …**).

**Stop condition:** not yet met. Test 01 lost 2–3 points to missing words, and this one about 1–1.5. Two consecutive tests under 1 point are needed.
