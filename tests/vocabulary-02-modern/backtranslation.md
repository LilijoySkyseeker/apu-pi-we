# Back-translation (blind reader, bt8)

## Text L (a market)

**L1.** lon sin YEbin TIwu pi FAba pi MOle pi lon se pu BIka.
- Gloss: I DONE call child OF sibling OF mother OF I FROM PLACE-buy.
- Reading: **I called my cousin from the market.** (child of my mother's sibling = cousin; "place for buying" = market/shop; "call" probably by phone)

**L2.** TAnun WIwi la WUpi NImo FEli MEfa, gi TAle KUdu la Obu wo MUyi.
- Gloss: fruit red PL need money many today, because car big PL come after time.
- Reading: **Tomatoes are expensive today because the trucks came late.** ("red fruits" = tomatoes, possibly strawberries; "need much money" = cost a lot; "big cars" = trucks; "come after time" = arrive late)

**L3.** wan Oyi wun sun win SEge NImo gan LIga pi nen.
- Gloss: she say THAT LOGO SOON send money WITH phone OF self.
- Reading: **She said she'd send me the money by phone** (a mobile money transfer / mobile payment). ("wan" = the cousin; "sun" = she, the reported speaker)

## Text S (an announcement on a train)

**S1.** pu BAmo wo tan gun GUyi pi no WUNgu la.
- Gloss: PLACE-stop after this BE house OF AGENT-lead PL.
- Reading: **The next stop is Parliament** ("house of the leaders"; could also be City Hall / the government building).

**S2.** BEya la BENbi mon FUNbe.
- Gloss: door PL open AT left.
- Reading: **Doors open on the left.**

**S3.** so ku NEke Ewo PAni la man KObi, du GOyu pi me.
- Gloss: REQUEST POLITE give person old PL WHO move, TOOL-sit OF you.
- Reading: **Please give your seat to elderly passengers.** ("old people who are moving/travelling" = elderly passengers, possibly those getting on; "thing for sitting" = seat)

## Text M (a message at work)

**M1.** ku, FUNti pi din: FANmu pi FUNti gun WUma LEso.
- Gloss: POLITE, group OF we.excl: gather OF group BE tomorrow morning.
- Reading: **Dear team: the team meeting is tomorrow morning.** ("gathering of the group" = meeting)

**M2.** so ku DINyo ge Iko pi ge KOda, yen du SUNbu MEli pi me la.
- Gloss: REQUEST POLITE carry RESULT-tell OF RESULT-sell, and TOOL-count small OF you PL.
- Reading: **Please bring the sales report and your laptops.** ("what is told about what is sold" = sales report; "small counting devices" = laptops, i.e. small computers; possibly calculators)

**M3.** INtalunetu mon KEmu BEse pi DA mu DOku.
- Gloss: internet AT place inside OF 4 NOT work.
- Reading: **The internet in Room 4 isn't working.** ("inside place" = room; "pi DA" = number label 4)

## Text C (a clinic)

**C1.** no YEma Ewo SImi SUNbu DANdi pi MAkon pi lon, yen NEke lon MAbo pi MUsu li SImi.
- Gloss: AGENT-help person sick count push OF blood OF I, and give I paper OF thing FOR sick.
- Reading: **The nurse took my blood pressure and gave me a prescription.** ("one who helps sick people" = nurse; "counted the push of my blood" = measured blood pressure; "paper for medicine" = prescription)

**C2.** lon KEya NAwi MUsu MEli FI li SImi MUyi SA mon DIli we, wo NAwi PAsu.
- Gloss: I must eat thing small 1 FOR sick time 2 AT day ALL, after eat food.
- Reading: **I have to take one pill twice a day, after meals.** ("one small thing for sickness" = one pill/tablet; "every day")

## Text N (news)

**N1.** no WUNgu la Oyi wun NImo pi PAsu pi YIkan mu win LEwe ke nin FAwu tan.
- Gloss: AGENT-lead PL say THAT money OF food OF seed NOT SOON become more WHILE year this.
- Reading: **The government said grain prices will not rise this year.** ("the leaders" = the government/authorities; "money of seed-food" = price of grain/cereals)

**N2.** no DOku fu SA YIyo KEgu BONko mon GElu pi SOla mon BIge pi YOlan.
- Gloss: AGENT-work about 2 x6^4 talk angry AT near OF road AT above OF river.
- Reading: **About 2,600 workers protested near the bridge.** (2 x 1296 = 2592, a round number in base 6; "talk angrily" = protest/demonstrate; "road above the river" = bridge. Strictly by the attachment rule "above the river" would attach to the verb, i.e. "protested near the road, above the river", but "bridge" is the natural reading.)
