Five short modern texts from different places. INtalunetu is a borrowed word (the language's adaptation of a foreign word).

Text L (a market)
L1. lon sin YEbin TIwu pi FAba pi MOle pi lon se pu BIka.
L2. TAnun WIwi la WUpi NImo FEli MEfa, gi TAle KUdu la Obu wo MUyi.
L3. wan Oyi wun sun win SEge NImo gan LIga pi nen.

Text S (an announcement on a train)
S1. pu BAmo wo tan gun GUyi pi no WUNgu la.
S2. BEya la BENbi mon FUNbe.
S3. so ku NEke Ewo PAni la man KObi, du GOyu pi me.

Text M (a message at work)
M1. ku, FUNti pi din: FANmu pi FUNti gun WUma LEso.
M2. so ku DINyo ge Iko pi ge KOda, yen du SUNbu MEli pi me la.
M3. INtalunetu mon KEmu BEse pi DA mu DOku.

Text C (a clinic)
C1. no YEma Ewo SImi SUNbu DANdi pi MAkon pi lon, yen NEke lon MAbo pi MUsu li SImi.
C2. lon KEya NAwi MUsu MEli FI li SImi MUyi SA mon DIli we, wo NAwi PAsu.

Text N (news)
N1. no WUNgu la Oyi wun NImo pi PAsu pi YIkan mu win LEwe ke nin FAwu tan.
N2. no DOku fu SA YIyo KEgu BONko mon GElu pi SOla mon BIge pi YOlan.
