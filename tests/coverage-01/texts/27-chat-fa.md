## original
A: اگر مخالفتی نیست خواهان انتقال مقاله به شوربا هستم. نام فعلی فقط در ایران مرسوم است. نام اصلی این غذا شوربا است که بسیاری از اهالی ایران از جمله ترک ها هم با همین نام آنرا می شناسند.

B: به گمانم شوربا نام عربی غذا است. ضمنا ترکی و فارسی دو زبان متفاوت هستند. اگر ترکها به غذا چیز دیگری می گویند ربطی به زبان فارسی ندارند.

## english
A: If there's no objection, I'd like the article moved to "shorba". The current name is only usual in Iran. The original name of this dish is shorba, and many people in Iran, including the Turks, know it by that name too.

B: I think shorba is the Arabic name of the dish. Besides, Turkish and Persian are two different languages. If the Turks call the dish something else, that has nothing to do with the Persian language.

[omitted]

C: Oppose. The custom is that everywhere in Iran, both in the media and among the people, it's known as abgoosht. I don't know much about shorba, but if it means the same thing, it would be better to move shorba to abgoosht.

D: In answer to Behzad Modares I have to say that in Afghanistan abgoosht is called exactly shorba (though the colloquial pronunciation is shorwa), and the word abgoosht is obsolete. [omitted]
