## english
[cut]
A: The picture below of making jiaozi isn't right, is it? That's making guotie [pan-fried dumplings].

[omitted]

B: Is the picture in the article really making jiaozi? That's clearly the way you wrap wontons. It isn't representative. Nowhere in the north does anyone use water to stick the wrapper together when making jiaozi.

C: In the south this is how wontons and jiaozi are told apart: wonton wrappers are square, jiaozi wrappers are round, and the round ones can also be used to wrap shaomai.

D: In Dalian there's a saying, "soft dough for jiaozi, hard dough for noodle soup". It means the dough for jiaozi should be a bit soft and the dough for noodles needs to be a bit hard. I wonder whether the article is right or our saying is?!
