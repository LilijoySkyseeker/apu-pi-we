- ru.wikinews Moscow/Kyiv metro fare articles: dense with figures; chose the Helsinki SMS-ticket article instead.

- pt.wikinews "Sarampo pode voltar..." (Agência Brasil text): lead is full of case counts; Agência Brasil licence terms have changed over time.
- zh.wikinews 華南氣溫顯著下降: a list of temperatures and warnings.
