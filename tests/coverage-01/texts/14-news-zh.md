## original
菜鳥驛站取件改須裝淘寶App

菜鳥驛站改用淘寶掃碼取件，較原先憑短訊取件碼快數倍，但強制須下載淘寶App取件，不論快件來自拼多多、天貓等任何所有電商平台。維聞記者親測後察覺強推淘寶App令人不快，有消費者認為淘寶屬壟斷資本搶佔市場。

## english
Picking up parcels at Cainiao Stations now requires installing the Taobao app

Cainiao Stations have switched to pickup by scanning a code with Taobao, which is several times faster than the previous method of collecting with a pickup code sent by text message, but people are forced to download the Taobao app to collect their parcels, no matter whether the parcel comes from Pinduoduo, Tmall or any other e-commerce platform. A Wikinews reporter who tried it in person found the forced push of the Taobao app unpleasant, and some consumers think Taobao is monopoly capital seizing the market.
