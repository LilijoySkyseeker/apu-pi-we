## english
Thais in America celebrate "Songkran - Thai New Year" in lively style

After having had no chance to go back to Thailand for several years, Thanyarat Milanda and Phawadee Rattanawet, Thais in the city of Los Angeles, reflected on their feelings as Thais abroad while they came to join the Thai New Year celebration and the annual Songkran festival at Wat Thai Los Angeles, California.

[omitted]

Getting to meet and chat, making merit together, taking part in Buddhist activities, pouring water on elders' hands to ask their blessing [rot nam dam hua], and pouring water over the monks and the Buddha images, in the Thai-style atmosphere of a Thai community abroad, helps many people ease their longing for their birthplace quite a bit.
