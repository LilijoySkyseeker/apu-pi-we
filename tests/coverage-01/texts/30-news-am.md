## english
In Addis Ababa, consumers complain that the soaring price of teff makes it hard to get by [literally "to eat and pass the night"]

In the city of Addis Ababa, consumers who said the price of teff keeps soaring complained that getting by [literally "eating and passing the night"] is becoming hard for them.

They said that a kilo of white teff, which cost 80 birr two months ago, is now selling for as much as 150 birr.

Residents of the city told the Voice of America that, following the conflict and instability that have spread in the teff-growing areas of the Amhara region, food grains including teff are not found on the market in the amount needed.

Ato Mesfan Assefa, deputy head of the Trade Bureau of the Addis Ababa City Administration, for his part said that grain products are coming into Addis Ababa from all parts of the country, including the Amhara region. He also announced that the city administration has set aside 1.4 billion birr and is working to increase the supply of crops.

Dr. Gete Andualem, associate professor in the Department of Marketing Management at Addis Ababa University, for his part points out that although the lack of peace contributes to the soaring price of food grains including teff, there are other, economic reasons as well.
