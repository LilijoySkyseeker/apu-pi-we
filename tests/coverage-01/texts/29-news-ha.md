## original
An Ga Watan Azumin Ramadan A Najeriya

Washington DC — Sarkin musulmi ya ce sun sami labarin tabbacin ganin watan Ramadan daga shugabanni da kungiyoyi na addinin musulunci, kuma sun amince.

Ya yi kira ga dukkan Musulman Najeriya da su tashi da azumin watan Ramadan gobe Litinin.

Haka kuma, ya yi kira ga al’ummar musulmi a fadin Najeriya da su dage da addu’o’i don fitar da kasar daga cikin halin kunci da aka samu kai ciki.

Tun farko dai Jami’an Saudiyya sun sanar da ganin jaririn watan Ramadan. Ba da jimawa ba, kasashen Larabawa da dama na yankin Gulf, da Iraki da Siriya, sun bi sanarwar don tabbatar da cewa su ma za su fara azumi ranar Litinin.

Shugabannin sun kuma yi ta musayar sakonnin taya murna cewa an shiga watan Ramadan mai alfarma.

## english
The Ramadan fasting moon has been sighted in Nigeria

Washington DC — The Sultan of the Muslims said they had received confirmation that the Ramadan moon had been sighted from Islamic religious leaders and organisations, and they had accepted it.

He called on all Nigerian Muslims to wake up fasting for the month of Ramadan tomorrow, Monday.

He also called on the Muslim community across Nigeria to keep on with prayers to bring the country out of the hardship it has found itself in.

Earlier, Saudi officials had announced the sighting of the newborn moon of Ramadan. Soon afterwards, many Arab countries of the Gulf region, and Iraq and Syria, followed the announcement to confirm that they too would begin fasting on Monday.

The leaders also kept exchanging messages of congratulation that the holy month of Ramadan had begun.
