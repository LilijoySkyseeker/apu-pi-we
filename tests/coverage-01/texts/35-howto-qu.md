## original
Jankakipa lawa

Imawantaq ruwasunchik
- 3 cebollas
- 500 gr trigo
- Apio
- 3 achojchas
- 3 zanahorias
- habas
- 3 dientes de ajo
- 1 Pimentón
- 3 tomates
- perejil
- sal a gusto

Wakichiynin
1. Yakuwan q'umirkunawan mayllanayki, laranayki tiyan, chanta papakunata t'ikpanayki tiyan
2. Juk mankapi, yakuta k'achiwan churanayki tiyan t'impuchiqkama.
3. Tukuy q'umirkunata mankaman churanayki tiyan, 30 chininiyuqkama t'impuchinayki tiyan.
4. Paylapi, trigota qurichasqa chayanankama jank'anayki tiyan.
5. Maranpi, trigota kutanayki tiyan, chanta juk chuwapi chiri yakuwan piktunayki tiyan.
6. Q'umirkunawan trigowan yaku mankaman churanayki tiyan.
7. Papakunata mankaman churanayki tiyan, 30 chininiyuqkama t'impunankama tiyan.
[cut: step 8 repeats step 7 word for word and is omitted]

## english
Toasted-wheat soup (jank'aki lawa)

What we will make it with
- 3 onions
- 500 g wheat
- Celery
- 3 achojchas [a small Andean gourd]
- 3 carrots
- broad beans
- 3 cloves of garlic
- 1 bell pepper
- 3 tomatoes
- parsley
- salt to taste

How to prepare it
1. You have to wash the vegetables with water and cut them up [laray], then you have to peel the potatoes
2. In a pot, you have to put water with salt until it boils.
3. You have to put all the vegetables into the pot and boil them for 30 minutes.
4. In a wide pan, you have to toast the wheat until it is cooked golden.
5. On the grinding stone, you have to grind the wheat, then rinse it [piktuy; sense uncertain, perhaps "mix"] with cold water in a bowl.
6. You have to put the water with the vegetables and the wheat into the pot.
7. You have to put the potatoes into the pot, until it has boiled for 30 minutes.
[cut]
