## original
[cut: the narrator's introduction before the dialogue and the closing narrator line are omitted]
Bu Pawiro: Ada apa pak, kok kelihatan sedang mikirin sesuatu?
Pak Pawiro: Iya, Bu. Ini tentang hasil panenan terakhir kita. Sepertinya ndak cukup untuk membiayai sekolah anak-anak.
Bu Pawiro: Kita dapat berapa kwintal pak?
Pak Pawiro: Kita hanya dapat 5 kwintal gabah dan harga gabah sekarang sedang turun kan bu? Kemarin aku tanya ke Pak Takim, katanya Rp 2.000 per kilo.
Bu Pawiro: Murah ya pak.
Pak Pawiro: Iya, itu mengapa aku pusing bu. Darimana dapat uang untuk biaya sekolah anak-anak?
Bu Pawiro: Hmhmhhmhm, bagaimana kalau kita pinjam uang dari Pak Santosa, mungkin dia bisa membantu.
Pak Pawiro: Tapi kalau pinjam di Pak Santosa, bunganya besar bu, kita susah nanti.
Bu Pawiro: Terus bagaimana pak? atau mau nyoba pinjam di koperasinya Pak Teguh? Banyak tetangga bilang kalau koperasinya Pak Teguh bunganya kecil.
Pak Pawiro: Tapi kita belum jadi anggota koperasi, bu. Gimana ya?
Bu Pawiro: Kalau bapak setuju, besok aku tanya ke Pak Teguh, pak. Bagaimana meminjam uang di koperasi.
Pak Pawiro: Baik, bu. Makasih ya.

## english
Bu Pawiro: What's up, Pak? You look like you're brooding over something.
Pak Pawiro: Yes, Bu. It's about our last harvest. It looks like it won't be enough to pay for the children's school.
Bu Pawiro: How many quintals did we get, Pak?
Pak Pawiro: We only got 5 quintals of unhulled rice, and the rice price is going down right now, isn't it, Bu? Yesterday I asked Pak Takim, and he said it's Rp 2,000 a kilo.
Bu Pawiro: That's cheap, Pak.
Pak Pawiro: Yes, that's why I've got a headache, Bu. Where will we get the money for the children's school fees?
Bu Pawiro: Hmmm, what if we borrow money from Pak Santosa? Maybe he can help.
Pak Pawiro: But if we borrow from Pak Santosa, the interest is high, Bu. We'll be in trouble later.
Bu Pawiro: So what then, Pak? Or do you want to try borrowing from Pak Teguh's cooperative? Lots of neighbours say the interest at Pak Teguh's cooperative is low.
Pak Pawiro: But we're not members of the cooperative yet, Bu. What do we do?
Bu Pawiro: If you agree, tomorrow I'll ask Pak Teguh, Pak. How to borrow money from the cooperative.
Pak Pawiro: All right, Bu. Thanks.
