## english

A: Thanks for the ping, brother Alaa. In Morocco we write it and pronounce it "tajine" (Tagine), and the tajine is at the same time the name of a popular dish in Morocco and also the name of the pot in which this dish is prepared and cooked. Greetings to you.

B: Thanks for the ping. From a casual search in search engines, and not just one, the following becomes clear
First, I noticed that the dishes are very delicious, which makes me strongly warn you against doing the search on an empty stomach, as this will certainly stir up your stomach juices and the secretion of acid juices that give a sharp feeling of heartburn.
Second: tajen with a kasra under the jim: it is used in the Mashreq, from what I found, to refer to the Turkish tajen or the Alexandrian akkawi, and no clay pots are used in it
Third: tajine with an added ya: this pronunciation and usage we find in the Maghreb, and it is very common in Morocco, and there are dishes specific to cities, such as the authentic Marrakech tajine
