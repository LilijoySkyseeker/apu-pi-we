## original
回锅肉

原料
- 猪肉 500克
- 蒜苗 150克
- 猪油 40克
- 盐 1克
- 郫县豆瓣（也可用一般豆瓣代替）50克
- 酱油 25克
- 生姜 15克
- 葱 20克
- 花椒十余粒

做法
1. 把带皮的肥瘦相连的猪肉洗干净；
2. 锅内放开水置旺火上，下猪肉和葱、姜、花椒。猪肉煮熟不煮烂，在煮肉过程中撇去汤面浮沫，蒜苗洗净切2－3厘米长节，豆瓣剁细；
3. 将捞起的猪肉敞开水汽，在还有余热时切成约0.3厘米厚的连皮肉片；

## english
Twice-cooked pork

Ingredients
- pork 500 g
- garlic shoots 150 g
- lard 40 g
- salt 1 g
- Pixian chilli bean paste (ordinary bean paste can be used instead) 50 g
- soy sauce 25 g
- fresh ginger 15 g
- spring onion 20 g
- a dozen or so Sichuan peppercorns

Method
1. Wash clean a piece of pork with the skin on, fat and lean together;
2. Put boiling water in a pot over a high flame and add the pork with the spring onion, ginger and Sichuan pepper. Cook the pork through but not to falling apart, skimming the froth off the surface while it boils; wash the garlic shoots and cut them into pieces 2-3 cm long; chop the bean paste fine;
3. Let the steam come off the pork you have lifted out, and while it is still warm slice it, skin and all, into slices about 0.3 cm thick;
