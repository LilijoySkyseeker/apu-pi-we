## original
Sickness - People
A: adu yārunga?
B: adu yen tagappanār
A: avarukku yenna oḍambu?
B: nālu nāḷā romba kāchchal
A: āspatirikki aḷachchukkiṭṭu pōnīngaḷā?
B: āmānga, nēttu aḷachchukiṭṭu pōnēnunga
A: ḍākṭar yenna sonnāru?
B: kuḷir kāchchal nu sonnāru
A: yēdāvadu marundu koduttārā?
B: āmānga, koduttāru
A: sīkkiram, ellām sariya pōyiḍum, kavala paḍādīnga
B: kaḍavuḷ tān kāppāttaṇunga

Sickness - Animal
A: unga pasu māṭṭukku yenna vyādi?
B: 'komari nōi' nu solrānga
A: kālnaḍe āspatirikki kondu pōnīngaḷā?
B: illīnga. āspatiri romba dūram irukkudunga
A: nān unga māṭṭe pākkalāmā?
B: ungaḷukku māḍugaḷe patti teriyumā?
A: konjam konjam teriyum. kālnaḍe vaidiyam patti paḍichchirukkēn
B: yeppaḍiyāvadu udavi seiyunga
A: yennālē muḍinjadu seiyarēn
B: nīnga nallā iruppīnga, inda māḍu settupōnā, yengaḷukku poḷappu pōyidunga
A: kavala paḍādīnga, māṭṭukku ūsi pōṭṭa sariyāyidunga

## english
Sickness - People
A: Who is that?
B: That is my father
A: Is he not well?
B: He has been having high fever for the last four days.
A: Did you take him to the hospital?
B: Yes. Yesterday I took him to the hospital.
A: What did the doctor say?
B: He said it was 'cold fever'.
A: Did he give any medicine?
B: Yes. He did.
A: Everything will be alright soon, Don't worry.
B: Oh! God help us.

Sickness - Animal
A: What is your cow suffering from?
B: They say it is 'kow mari'. (a cattle disease)
A: Did you take it to the veterinary hospital?
B: No, the hospital is very far away.
A: Shall I take a look at the cow?
B: Do you know about cows?
A: I know a little. I have studied about veterinary science.
B: Somehow help us.
A: I (will) do whatever I can.
B: God bless you. If this cow dies, we will lose our livelihood.
A: Don't worry. It will be alright if the cow is inoculated.
