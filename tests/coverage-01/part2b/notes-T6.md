## Concepts with no word and no placeholder even at 2000 (the gaps)

- food words of the texts' own subjects: hilsa, phở, jiaozi/dumpling, wonton, guotie, shaomai, noodles, dough (all fell back to "pasu tan", "pasu meli pi …", "sowe bewon", "pasu netu")
- fry (pan-fry); mash; wrap (as distinct from fold)
- word (as in "the word abgoosht"; used suso "name") and saying/proverb (ge oyi)
- section/paragraph of a text (kin pi kemin; ge tusi meli)
- template (Wikipedia; suso, then ⟨tag⟩ at 2000); stub; gallery (pu pi foto la)
- habitat (pu nume)
- unnecessary / necessary (go mu wupi); insignificant (mu bawe); tasty below 2000 (upe li nawi)
- point of view below 1500 (pume)
- rename/move an article (kobi … tun)
- colloquial / everyday speech (mon kegu pi go); obsolete, no longer used (go mu le bone)
- synonymous, mean the same (yofu musu kige)
- including (fu; ⟨include⟩ exists at 2000 but a relative "man ⟨include⟩" attaches to the nearest noun)
- representative, typical (go manni muge … fu ben)
- tell apart, distinguish (lugo … se …)
- fall in love (tuko wigen); wife as distinct from spouse (ofin, ofin futa); lover below 1500 (no wigen)
- profession/trade other than ⟨job⟩/⟨trade⟩ (doku)
- clearly/obviously (wanme, the evidential, worked well)
- language names: Arabic, Persian, Turkish, Vietnamese — said as apu pi en X; Persian had no obvious owner name (used en fars)
- "really?" incredulous (ba min worked)
