## Concepts with neither a word nor a placeholder at any size (the gaps)

- yard, the unit (⟨yard⟩ in the lists is the garden; passed through as the symbol `yd`)
- texture, how something feels to the touch (sanne)
- discount, take off a price (nuton in; only ⟨decrease⟩ at 2000)
- really, truly as an intensifier (feko as a stance word)
- dinner, supper (nawi mon kegin); lunch only at 2000
- local dishes: amala (yam-flour paste), pounded yam (iyan); yam itself is only tengi "tuber"
- naming ceremony (no "naming" concept; ⟨ceremony⟩ exists from 1500)
- the land of a people (Yorubaland → fowo pi en yoruba)
- disease, illness as a noun distinct from "sick" (simi)
- veterinary (⟨hospital⟩/⟨medicine⟩ pi noso)
- livelihood, a living (⟨income⟩ from 1500 is close)
- somehow, in some way (gan lite bo)
- cold fever, chills (fever + gan weki)
- recently, lately (mon fawu uyo pi fugi)
- the more … the more (no correlative construction)
- sow, plant seeds (gitin yikan); no verb "plant"
- crop (pinfe)
- rice paddy vs dry field (sekan wulon / sekan kilan)
- ginseng (yinki bo)
- silkworm (enna man muge ⟨silk⟩ at best)
- rank first, be number one (ke … se we)
- textile, fabric (demi "cloth" covers it)
- blouse (⟨shirt⟩ from 1000)
- rural area as distinct from countryside/village (sekan; ⟨countryside⟩ from 1500)
