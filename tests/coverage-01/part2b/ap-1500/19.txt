tika bunle man gun ⟨mix⟩

musu man go wupi
- tika 3
- tunlo, ⟨spoon⟩ kudu 2 lu 3 (don me momu)
- ⟨butter⟩, ⟨spoon⟩ kudu 2
- bunwu, ⟨spoon⟩ meli ¼ (lu fu tenmi pi me)
- yikan tago, ⟨spoon⟩ meli ⅛ (lu fu tenmi pi me)

lite
1. tin alun tika la tun ladon pi ⟨measure⟩.
2. tin ginni tunlo (don me momu) yen bunwu.
3. gan du ⟨mix⟩ lu ⟨fork⟩, tin payon ge ⟨mix⟩ anko bu ibe ⟨bubble⟩ feli.
4. tin muge ⟨liquid⟩ ⟨butter⟩ mon ⟨pot⟩ mawun, gan tikun timon.
5. tin ⟨pour⟩ tika tun ⟨pot⟩ mawun.
6. tin ⟨mix⟩ ge ⟨mix⟩ nin bu siten, li alun tika, yen li efon tika bewon ke feli sanne ⟨pot⟩ mawun tikun.
   ⟨mix⟩ yobe muge ge alun meli, un ⟨mix⟩ ke gake muge ge alun ke kudu.
7. tin siten anko foke ya me feme wun tika la gun wonwe, gi tika la le siten wo me yidu bu la se ⟨pot⟩ mawun.
   tika la gun wonwe nin wino pi bu kusa.
   tin bamo siten foke ya ben.
