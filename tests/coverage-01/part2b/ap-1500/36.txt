fuyu man sigo tun ⟨pot⟩

⟨material⟩ la
fuyu pi ⟨pig⟩, gulamu 500
wonin pi lengu meli pinda, gulamu 150
gimen pi ⟨pig⟩, gulamu 40
bunwu, gulamu 1
⟨sauce⟩ pi ⟨bean⟩ man obu se en pixian (go fe lenu bone ⟨sauce⟩ ⟨normal⟩ pi ⟨bean⟩ mon kemu pi bu), gulamu 50
⟨sauce⟩ tago, gulamu 25
yinki tikun ⟨fresh⟩, gulamu 15
lengu gisu, gulamu 20
yikan tikun konsin 10

lite pi muge
1. pi fuyu man ibe unne yen gimen, pensi bu fu inan.
2. yaga osa binla mon ⟨pot⟩, yen yaga bu mon lumi konke, yen ginni fuyu yen lengu gisu, yinki tikun yen yikan tikun.
binla fuyu anko bu wonwe, un mu anko bu alun, yen nin binla, nuton ⟨bubble⟩ la mon ⟨surface⟩ pi osa, pensi wonin pi lengu meli pinda fu inan, yen fonsu bu tun musu la man netu sentimetulu se 2 tun 3, yen fonsu ⟨sauce⟩ pi ⟨bean⟩ fu meli.
3. pi fuyu man me sin yidu se osa, efon penso kusa se bu, yen nin bu le tikun, fonsu bu tun musu la man gun woten sentimetulu fu 0.3 yen ibe unne.
