en kemenhub ri oyi wun le ibe kemu li dinyo ⟨motorcycle⟩ nuwen unfu.

en j.a. barata, man gun ⟨leader⟩ pi ⟨center⟩ pi ⟨communication⟩ ⟨public⟩ pi en kemenhub ri, sin oyi mon ⟨Monday⟩ (6/7) mon en jakarta wun go gun ⟨urge⟩ li ⟨register⟩ fu yobe, yen li bone dinyo man ⟨government⟩ neke li ⟨motorcycle⟩ nuwen unfu.

en j.a. barata sin ginni wun go le lenu inwa ⟨program⟩ man ⟨ministry⟩ pi dinyo muge li ⟨motorcycle⟩ nuwen unfu, gi go lenu ⟨register⟩ anko fawu mena 2015, dunan mena 7, dili mena 8, lu anko kemu we gun kete.

"gan dinyo tan nuwen unfu, din walo muge eten isi pi ⟨motorcycle⟩ pi no sigo tun guyi, anko ⟨destination⟩ pi wan la.
fu ⟨example⟩, se en jakarta tun en semarang, don ewo bo momu sigo tun guyi mon en kendal, wan mu ⟨ride⟩ ⟨motorcycle⟩ pi nen, ⟨train⟩ dinyo bu, wan ⟨ride⟩ ⟨train⟩ yen liwon mon en semarang, yen na mon ⟨station⟩ pi ⟨train⟩ pi en semarang, wan yidu ⟨motorcycle⟩ pi nen li ⟨continue⟩ isi gan bu tun en kendal.
gi tan, isi man wan la keya muge gan ⟨motorcycle⟩ fu bi gofa, sin lewe eten, wan la mu muge ben," wan sin ⟨explain⟩.
