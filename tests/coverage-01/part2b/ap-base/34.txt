lite pi muge
pen pensi yikan kowan, yen yaga bu la nin awuwa 8, lu se kegin anko leso, mon osa man sin binla gan ladon meli meli 1 pi bunwu pinda, wo, nuton osa, yen binla bu la, yen kuwi osa pi binla.
mon du monpa, pen yaga yikan kowan man sin binla, tunlo tufun, ge inke pi yikan pinda, lengu meli pinda, osa pi tanun tufun, uyo pi osa pi yikan kowan, yen bunwu, yen fanmu bu.
pen alun sowe tun musu la man mu kudu yen mu meli, yen yaga bu la mon osa pi yikan kowan man sin binla.

lite pi neke
pen yaga sowe mon nansa mawun, yen yaga ge fanmu pi yikan kowan mon bige pi bu gan ge wupi eni la, pen muge tikun gimen pi tunlo, yen yaga bu mon bige gan uyo pi yikan kanko yen tanun tikun wiwi lu ge inke wiwi tufun, yen fe muge sesa bu gan yikan siten pi gala yen wonin kanko man gun fonsu.
