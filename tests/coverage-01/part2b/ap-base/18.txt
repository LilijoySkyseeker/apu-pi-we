lite li mu dunu simi pi tikun

li kuwi nen se simi pi tikun, gun bawe wun go muge musu denku li kemu man go gun mon.
nin ewo bo oyi "yo simi pi tikun", so pen nedin mu isi tun aban, yen mibun se tikun.

mibun se tikun, kuwi nen

mon bese
- finsa tikun gan du muge weki wunta, lu musu fu bu
- bone demi man mu efon wino, yen du muge situ
- manni loban tikun pi fudon
- fe miya wifu pi en wbgt

mon aban
- bone du kuwi se giwe, lu yunko musu pi lukan
- bone situ, manni yenbo
- mon dili man yaye gun upe, nedin mu isi tun aban nin dili

li tikun mu fanmu mon gawo
- yunko lonne man efon wunta yen yidu tiwe yen kilan fu yobe
- muge weki gawo gan yufa weki, nokun, demi weki yen eni

manni koye osa

mon bese lu mon aban, wi don pen mu feme wupi osa, so pen manni koye osa.
