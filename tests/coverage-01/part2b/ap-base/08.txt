yaga pi funti la

A: to oyi "mon batu tan, so pen eda waka fu gufu."
pen sin kegu tonya fu bu mon bige.
fon pen yaga funti la mon lunyu pi mido pi ewo la fu wifu pi waka la man bu la sin dunu mon waka ke duwa pi gigin, yen yaga funti la man mu sin gun mon waka ke duwa, un gun mon lunyu pi mido pi ewo la, mon fugi, fu muyi man bu la sin tuko, se ke pani tun ke duwa, se funbe tun nata.
so pen yaga lunyu la we fu lite kige, gan wifu pi minwe la yen wifu pi lifin la.

B: tayu

C: gi yaga la lenu kubo mon muyi we, gun ke denku wun pen piyi fu lite kige, fu mido la we man gun mon bige, yen le yaga fu ge muwo pi waka ke duwa.

D: tayu, to fe pume ben, gun ke gole wun pen yaga fu waka la mon waka ke duwa pi gigin

E: li gigin man gun fu en türkiye, man musu we kubo bi yobe mon, gun gagan wun pen yaga fu waka ke duwa man gigin sin muge ya fawu 2.
gi tan, gun ke denku wun pen yaga konsin fu waka ke duwa pi oton la.
