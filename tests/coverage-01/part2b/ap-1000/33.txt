lite pi muge

lite
muge inan wonin gisu pi niki sa, yen pensi bu la mon osa fu upe, gimu sa.
wo pensi, fonsu bu la fu meli, yen yaga bu la yen litulu 1 pi osa mon ⟨pot⟩ pi ⟨pressure⟩, li binla.
wo ninge 5, yidu ⟨pot⟩ se lumi, yen wo bu lewe weki, ginni gulamu 50 pi ge inke pi ⟨maize⟩, yen inke bu mon du inke fu meli meli.

wo, mon ⟨pot⟩ lawan, wo ⟨spoon⟩ kudu 1 pi yunen inan lewe tikun, siten lengu, yinki tikun, lengu meli pinda, wonin finlu, tanun tikun gisu yen ⟨tomato⟩ mon yunen, wo, ginni wonin gisu man gun inke mon du inke, yen fanmu bu, yen siten bu nin muyi eten, minitu 10, wo, ginni ⟨butter⟩ yen wonin kanko, yen bu gun wonwe.
