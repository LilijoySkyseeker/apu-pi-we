yunen uyo pi en kenya gun batu li no dinyo ewo gan ⟨motorcycle⟩

mon en kenya, yunen uyo yen ⟨price⟩ ke tisan pi yunen muge batu pi doku li ewo loyen la man dinyo ewo gan ⟨motorcycle⟩.

nin ⟨interview⟩ gan en voice of america mon liga, kin pi wan la sin oyi wun gi yunen uyo, wan la keya bika yunen gan ⟨price⟩ tisan, nin no bika mu lenu unfu ⟨price⟩ tisan man wan la mido li isi, yen gi tan, ⟨business⟩ tan, man neke doku li ewo loyen feli mon en kenya, gun gofa.

en james mwangi gun no dinyo ewo gan ⟨motorcycle⟩ mon oton en nairobi, yen sin oyi "nin go iko no bika ⟨price⟩ pi isi, go yen wan tuko migi gan apu, yen wo, wan oyi wun sun mu lenu unfu nimo tan".

en paul kuja man fe dinyo ewo gan ⟨motorcycle⟩ mon oton en nairobi, sin ginni "gi nimo pi isi sin lewe tisan, no bika niba, gi tan, din mu dunu no bika".
