en kemenhub ri oyi wun le ibe kemu li dinyo ⟨motorcycle⟩ nuwen unfu.

en j.a. barata, man gun ⟨leader⟩ pi ⟨center⟩ pi ⟨communication⟩ ⟨public⟩ pi en kemenhub ri, sin oyi mon ⟨Monday⟩ (6/7) mon en jakarta wun go gun yebin li yaga suso pi nen fu yobe, yen li bone dinyo man ⟨government⟩ neke li ⟨motorcycle⟩ nuwen unfu.

en j.a. barata sin ginni wun go le lenu inwa ⟨program⟩ man funti pi dinyo pi gigin muge li ⟨motorcycle⟩ nuwen unfu, gi go lenu yaga suso pi nen anko fawu mena 2015, dunan mena 7, dili mena 8, lu anko kemu we gun kete.

"gan dinyo tan nuwen unfu, din walo muge eten isi pi ⟨motorcycle⟩ pi no sigo tun guyi, anko kemu man wan la momu isi tun.
fu ⟨example⟩, se en jakarta tun en semarang, don ewo bo momu sigo tun guyi mon en kendal, wan mu ⟨ride⟩ ⟨motorcycle⟩ pi nen, ⟨train⟩ dinyo bu, wan ⟨ride⟩ ⟨train⟩ yen liwon mon en semarang, yen na mon ⟨station⟩ pi ⟨train⟩ pi en semarang, wan yidu ⟨motorcycle⟩ pi nen li le isi gan bu tun en kendal.
gi tan, isi man wan la keya muge gan ⟨motorcycle⟩ fu bi gofa, sin lewe eten, wan la mu muge ben," wan sin ⟨explain⟩.
