Ways to avoid heat illness

To protect yourself from heat illness, it's important to do the right things for the place you're in.
When someone announces "Warning: heat illness", let's hardly go outside, and hide from the heat.

Hide from the heat, protect yourself

Indoors
- Adjust the temperature with an air conditioner, or something like it
- Use cloth that doesn't let light through, and things that make shade
- Regularly check the room temperature
- Also look at the WBGT number

Outdoors
- Use something to protect you from the sun, or wear a hat
- Use shade, and rest regularly
- On days when the weather is good, hardly go outside during the day

So that heat doesn't build up in the body
- Wear clothes that let air through and take up sweat and dry quickly
- Cool your body with cold packs, ice, cold towels and other things

Drink water regularly

Indoors or outdoors, even if we don't feel thirsty, let's drink water regularly.
