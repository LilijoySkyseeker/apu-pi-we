Now, to pick up parcels at Cainiao Yizhan, you must install Taobao

At Cainiao Yizhan, people now pick up parcels with Taobao, which looks at a code, and that is a few times faster than picking up with a code from a text message, but people must download Taobao to pick up parcels, even if the parcel comes from Pinduoduo or Tmall or others that sell on the internet.
A Wikinews reporter tried it, and felt that Taobao's strong pushing is not pleasant, and some buyers think that Taobao is big money that wants to take the whole market.
