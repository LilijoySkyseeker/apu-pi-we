In Yoruba land, we don't usually say to people, "Come to my house to eat in the evening" or "I want to see you at noon, come and eat with me."

But if a friend goes to visit his own friend at home, he eats just the food he finds at that man's house, at noon or in the evening.

Also, if he goes in the morning, he eats just whatever he finds at their house.

At a festival time, such as Christmas, Yoruba people like to kill animals, and like to cook food such as rice, or ground tubers, or crushed tubers.

The person who comes, or the people he visits, eat just whatever they find.

That is also the same at the time of a wedding, or at the time when people give a child a name.
