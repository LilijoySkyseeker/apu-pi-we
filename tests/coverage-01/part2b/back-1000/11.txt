JR West company worker used ICOCA to ride trains again and again without paying, and was removed from work

According to news written by the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, JR West announced on the 5th of the month that a 61-year-old man who works at Mikunigaoka Station on the Hanwa Line, in Sakai City, was removed from work on January 4, because he misused an ICOCA card to ride JR trains again and again without paying, for example from Hiroshima to Ashiya in Hyogo Prefecture, and did not pay ¥22,840 in fares.

According to the news the Yomiuri Shimbun wrote, that worker works at JR West Transportation Service, a company that JR West owns.
It seems he didn't pay, for example, when he visited a member of his own family who was in hospital in Hiroshima.
