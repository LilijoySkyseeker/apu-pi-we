How to make it

Method
Clean two kinds of green leaves, and wash them well in water, twice.
After washing, cut them small, and put them with 1 litre of water in a pressure pot to boil.
After 5 sounds, take the pot off the fire, and once it has cooled, add 50 grams of ground maize, and grind it very fine in a grinder.

Then, in a deep pot, once 1 big spoon of clean oil has become hot, cook onion, hot root, small white onion, bitter leaves, green hot fruit and tomato in the oil; then add the green leaves that were ground in the grinder, mix it, and cook it for a short time, 10 minutes; then add butter and fragrant leaves, and it's ready.
