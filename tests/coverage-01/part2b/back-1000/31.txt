Thai people who live in America celebrate very happily at Songkran, which is the Thai new year.

After being unable to return to Thailand for many years, Thanyarat Milanda and Phawadee Rattanawet, who live in the city of Los Angeles, talked about their own feelings as Thai people in another country, when they came to celebrate the Thai new year at each year's Songkran at Wat Thai Los Angeles in California.

Gathering and talking, being good to each other, doing Buddhist religious activities, pouring water on the hands of old people to receive blessings from them, and pouring water on followers of the Buddha and on things made in the Buddha's likeness, in Thai style, in the community of Thai people in another country, helps many people a great deal to miss their own birthplace less.
