Grass-seed soup

What do we make it with?
3 onions
500 grams of grass seeds
Green plants
3 cucumbers
3 carrots
Big beans
3 small white onions
1 big hot fruit
3 tomatoes
Fragrant leaves
Salt to taste

Method of preparation
1. You must wash the green things in water and cut them up; then you must peel the potatoes.
2. In a pot, you must put water with salt until it boils.
3. You must put all the green things into the pot, and boil them for 30 minutes.
4. In a wide pot, you must toast the grass seeds until they are cooked and golden.
5. On a grinding stone, you must grind the grass seeds; then you must wash them in cold water in a bowl.
6. You must put the water with the green things and the grass seeds into the pot.
7. You must put the potatoes into the pot, until it has boiled for 30 minutes.
