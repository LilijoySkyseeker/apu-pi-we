Big road problems in Buenos Aires because of rain and lots of water

The city of Buenos Aires has road problems because of heavy rain and a lot of water on the main roads of the capital of Argentina.
Almost all of the roads that enter the city are under water, and the underground is running late and some services, such as trains and buses, aren't running.
Some of the city's districts are without electricity, to protect people from accidents.
This situation is also found in the cities near Buenos Aires.
