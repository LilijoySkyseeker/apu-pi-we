In Nigeria, people have seen the moon that starts the Ramadan fast.

From Washington DC, the king of the Muslims said that Muslim leaders and organisations had told them it is certain that the Ramadan moon has been seen, and they had agreed.

He called on all the Muslims of Nigeria to start the Ramadan fast tomorrow, Monday.

He also called on the Muslim community everywhere in Nigeria not to stop praying, so that the country can leave the difficult time it has entered.

Earlier, officials of Saudi Arabia had announced that the new moon of Ramadan had been seen.
A short time later, many Arab countries in the Gulf region, and Iraq and Syria, followed the announcement, saying that they too would certainly start fasting on Monday.

The leaders were also sending each other happy messages that the honoured month of Ramadan had begun.
