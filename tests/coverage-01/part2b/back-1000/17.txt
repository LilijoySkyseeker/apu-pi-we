How do you protect yourself from mosquito diseases?

To stop mosquito diseases and other diseases that people get from Aedes aegypti mosquitoes, the best way is to remove the places where mosquitoes breed, or make them harmless.
That means all containers or anything that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive cold and dryness, it's important that people check for and remove mosquito breeding places without stopping, all year round.

Ideas for protecting yourself

- Get rid of all unused containers that can collect water (food containers, bottles, car tyres, and others), inside and outside the home or workplace.
- Puncture containers that can't be used before throwing them away.
  If you can't, break them or crush them.
  Put them in a closed bag, so that the garbage collectors take them away safely.
