Kemenhub RI says there are still places to carry motorcycles free of charge.

J.A. Barata, who is head of the Public Communication Center of Kemenhub RI, said on Monday (6/7) in Jakarta that people are called on to register their names quickly, and to use the transport the government provides for motorcycles free of charge.

J.A. Barata added that people can still join the program that the national transport group made for carrying motorcycles free of charge, because people can register until July 8, 2015, or until all places are full.

"With this free transport, we are trying to shorten the motorcycle journey of people returning home, as far as the place they want to go.
For example, from Jakarta to Semarang: if someone wants to return home to Kendal, he doesn't ride his own motorcycle; the train carries it, he rides the train and arrives in Semarang, and only at the Semarang train station does he take his motorcycle to go on with it to Kendal.
So the journey they would have to make by motorcycle with great difficulty has become short; they don't make it," he explained.
