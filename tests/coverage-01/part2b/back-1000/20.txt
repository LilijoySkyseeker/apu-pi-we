Step by step

Before anything else, prepare the place where you'll keep the computer, following these ideas.
- It's best to use a wide, empty table.
- If you want, work standing up, so you can move more freely.
- Choose a place with lots of light.

Gather the things you need.
- A Phillips screwdriver.
- A brush with thin bristles (it can be a paintbrush).
- Thermal paste (you can buy it at a computer shop).
- A wiping cloth, or paper towels.
- A white school eraser.
- A blower.
- A flat screwdriver (if you want).
- A flashlight (if you want).
- Spray water for cleaning the connectors (if you want).
- Black powder, or sewing machine oil (if you want).

Step 1: open the computer
1. Remove all cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, network, phone, printer, and others).
2. Open the computer case (some people wrongly call it the CPU), by removing the small locking pieces on the back (some only have clips), and taking off the hard cover.
