In Addis Ababa, buyers complain that the price of small seeds is rising fast, and life is hard for them.

In the city of Addis Ababa, buyers who say the price of small seeds is rising fast complain that life is becoming hard for them.

They said that two months ago, one kilo of white small seeds was 80 birr, but now it sells for as much as 150 birr.

City residents told the Voice of America that, because of the fighting and unrest that has spread to the places in Amhara that grow small seeds, there isn't enough food grain in the market, and there are no small seeds either.

Mr. Mesfan Assefa, who is the second leader of the office of trade in the city of Addis Ababa, said that food grain is still coming into Addis Ababa from all over the country, including from Amhara.
He also announced that the city government has put 1,400,000,000 birr toward this, and is working to increase the crops coming in.

Mr. Gete Andualem, who is a professor of commerce at Addis Ababa University, also points out that the price of food grain is rising fast because there is no peace, but also because of other economic reasons.
