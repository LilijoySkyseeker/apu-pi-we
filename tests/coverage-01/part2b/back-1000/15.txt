Kenya's fuel shortage is a problem for those who carry passengers on motorcycles

In Kenya, the shortage of fuel and higher fuel prices are causing work problems for young people who carry passengers on motorcycles.

In an interview with Voice of America by phone, some of them said that because of the fuel shortage, they have to buy fuel at a high price, while customers can't pay the high price they ask for a ride, and so this business, which gives work to many young people in Kenya, is struggling.

James Mwangi carries passengers on a motorcycle in Nairobi, and he said, "When you tell a customer the price of the ride, you and they start fighting with words, and afterwards they say that they can't pay that much money."

Paul Kuja, who also carries passengers on a motorcycle in Nairobi, added, "Because the price of rides has gone up, customers walk, so we don't get customers."
