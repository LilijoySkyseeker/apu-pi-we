Broken bread is one of the foods that very many people know in Sham, especially in Dimashq, and its name is Tasqiyeh or Tas'iyeh.

It is usually an important food at Friday breakfast, when all the members of the family gather.

Broken bread of Sham

Ingredients
A cup of whole beans
A cup of yogurt
Ground white seeds
Juice of one sour fruit
One small white onion
Bread
3 spoons of butter
Salt
Ground red hot fruit, or a ground red sour powder
Tree nuts and fragrant leaves, to decorate

Method of preparation
We wash the beans, and leave them for 8 hours, or from evening until morning, in water that has been boiled with 1 small spoon of white salt; then we pour off the water, boil them, and keep the boiling water.
In a crusher, we put the boiled beans, the yogurt, the ground white seeds, the small white onion, the sour fruit juice, a little of the bean water, and salt, and mix it.
We break the bread into pieces that are neither big nor small, and put them in the water from the boiled beans.

How to serve
We put the bread on a big plate, and pour the bean mixture with the other ingredients over it; we heat the butter, and pour it over the top with a few fragrant seeds and red hot fruit or ground red sour powder, and also decorate it with cooked tree nuts and chopped fragrant leaves.
