Meat that goes back to the pot

Ingredients
Pork, 500 grams
Leaves of small white onion, 150 grams
Pig fat, 40 grams
Salt, 1 gram
Bean sauce from Pixian (you can also use any bean sauce in its place), 50 grams
Black sauce, 25 grams
Raw hot root, 15 grams
Green onion, 20 grams
At least 10 hot seeds

Method
1. Take the meat that has skin and fat, and wash it clean.
2. Put water on to boil in a pot, put it on a strong fire, and add the meat and the green onion, hot root and hot seeds.
Boil the meat until it is done, but not until it falls apart, and while it boils, remove the bubbles from the top of the water; wash the small-white-onion leaves clean, cut them into pieces 2 to 3 centimetres long, and chop the bean sauce finely.
3. Take the meat you have lifted out of the water, let the smoke leave it, and while it is still hot, cut it into pieces about 0.3 centimetres thick, each with skin on.
