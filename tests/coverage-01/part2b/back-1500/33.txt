How to make it

Method
Clean two kinds of green leaves, and wash them well in water, twice.
After washing, cut them small, and put them with 1 litre of water in a pressure cooker to boil.
After 5 whistles, take the pot off the fire, and once it has cooled, add 50 grams of ground maize, and grind it very fine in a grinder.

Then, in a deep pot, once 1 tablespoon of clean oil gets hot, cook onion, hot root, small white onion, bitter leaves, green hot pepper and tomato in the oil; then add the green leaves ground in the grinder, mix it, and cook it for a short time, 10 minutes; then add local butter and fragrant leaves, and it's ready.
