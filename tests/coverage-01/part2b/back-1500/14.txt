Now, to pick up parcels at Cainiao Yizhan, you have to install the Taobao app

At Cainiao Yizhan, people now pick up parcels with Taobao, which scans a code, and that is a few times faster than picking up with the code from a text message, but people have to get the Taobao app to pick up parcels, even if the parcel comes from Pinduoduo or Tmall or another platform that sells online.
A Wikinews journalist tried it, and felt that the Taobao app's hard push is not pleasant, and some customers think that Taobao is big money that wants to take over the whole market.
