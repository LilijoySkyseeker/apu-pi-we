Kenya's fuel shortage is a challenge for people who carry passengers by motorcycle

In Kenya, the fuel shortage and the higher price of fuel are creating job challenges for young people who carry passengers by motorcycle.

In a phone interview with Voice of America, some of them said that because of the fuel shortage, they have to buy fuel at a high price, while customers can't pay the high fares they ask for a ride, and so this business, which gives jobs to many young people in Kenya, is struggling.

James Mwangi carries passengers by motorcycle in the city of Nairobi, and he said, "When you tell a customer the price of the ride, you and he start an argument, and then he says that he can't pay that money."

Paul Kuja, who also carries passengers by motorcycle in the city of Nairobi, added, "Because the fare has become high, customers walk, so we don't get customers."
