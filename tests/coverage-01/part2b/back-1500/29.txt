In Nigeria, the moon that begins the Ramadan fast has been seen.

From Washington DC: the king of the Muslims said that Islamic leaders and organizations had told them it was certain the Ramadan moon had been seen, and that they had agreed.

He called on all Muslims in Nigeria to begin the Ramadan fast tomorrow, Monday.

He also called on the Muslim community everywhere in Nigeria not to stop praying, so that the country may leave the hard times it has entered.

Earlier, officials in Saudi Arabia announced that the new moon of Ramadan had been seen.
A short time later, many Arab countries in the Gulf region, as well as Iraq and Syria, followed the announcement, saying that they too would certainly begin the fast on Monday.

Leaders were also exchanging messages of joy that the honoured month of Ramadan had begun.
