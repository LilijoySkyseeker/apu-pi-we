Wheat soup

What do we make it with?
3 onions
500 grams of wheat
Greens
3 cucumbers
3 carrots
Large beans
3 small white onions
1 large hot pepper
3 tomatoes
Fragrant leaves
Salt to taste

Preparation
1. Wash the vegetables with water and cut them up; then peel the potatoes.
2. In a pot, put water with salt until it boils.
3. Put all the vegetables in the pot, and boil them for 30 minutes.
4. In a wide pan, toast the wheat until it is cooked and golden.
5. Grind the wheat on a grinding stone; then wash it with cold water in a bowl.
6. Put the water with the vegetables, and the wheat, into the pot.
7. Put the potatoes in the pot, until it has boiled for 30 minutes.
