Meat returned to the pot

Ingredients
Pork, 500 grams
Small white onion leaves, 150 grams
Pork fat, 40 grams
Salt, 1 gram
Bean sauce from Pixian (you can also use ordinary bean sauce in its place), 50 grams
Black sauce, 25 grams
Fresh hot root, 15 grams
Green onion, 20 grams
At least 10 hot seeds

Method
1. Wash the meat that has skin and fat clean.
2. Put boiling water in a pot, put it on a strong fire, and add the meat and the green onion, the hot root and the hot seeds.
Boil the meat until it is done, but not until it falls apart, and while it boils, skim the bubbles off the surface of the water; wash the small white onion leaves clean, and cut them into pieces 2 to 3 centimetres long, and chop the bean sauce fine.
3. Let the steam come off the meat you have taken out of the water, and while it is still hot, cut it into pieces about 0.3 centimetres thick, with skin on.
