Thai people living in America have a very happy time at Songkran, the Thai New Year.

After not being able to go back to Thailand for many years, Thanyarat Milanda and Phawadee Rattanawet, who live in the city of Los Angeles, talked about their own feelings as Thai people in another country, as they came to celebrate the Thai New Year and the yearly Songkran at Wat Thai Los Angeles in California.

Gathering and talking, making merit together, taking part in Buddhist religious activities, pouring water on the hands of old people to receive their blessing, and pouring water on Buddhist priests and on images of the Buddha, in the Thai style, in a community of Thai people in another country, helps many people miss their birthplace a lot less.
