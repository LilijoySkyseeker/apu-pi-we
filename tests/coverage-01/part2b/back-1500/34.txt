Broken bread is one of the most famous foods in Sham, especially in Damascus, and its name is Tasqiyeh or Tas'iyeh.

It is usually an important dish at Friday breakfast, when all the members of the family gather.

Sham broken bread

Amounts
A cup of whole beans
A cup of yogurt
Ground white seeds
The juice of one sour fruit
One small white onion
Bread
3 spoons of butter
Salt
Ground red hot pepper or ground red sour spice
Tree nuts and fragrant leaves, for decoration

Preparation
We wash the beans and put them for 8 hours, or from evening until morning, in water that has been boiled with 1 small spoon of white salt; then we pour off the water, boil the beans, and keep the boiling water.
In a mixer, we put the boiled beans, the yogurt, the ground white seeds, the small white onion, the sour fruit juice, a little of the bean water, and salt, and mix it.
We break the bread into medium pieces, and put them in the water from the boiled beans.

Serving
We spread the bread on a large plate and pour the bean mixture over it with the other ingredients; we heat the butter, and pour it over the top with a little fragrant seed and red hot pepper or the ground red sour spice, and we also decorate it with cooked tree nuts and chopped fragrant leaves.
