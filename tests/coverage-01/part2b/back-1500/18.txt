Ways to prevent heat illness

To prevent heat illness, it's important to do the right things for the place you are in.
When a "heat illness warning" is announced, let's go outside as little as possible, and shelter from the heat.

Shelter from the heat, protect yourself

Indoors
- Adjust the temperature with an air conditioner, or something like it
- Use cloth that blocks light, and things that make shade
- Regularly check the room temperature
- Also refer to the WBGT value

Outdoors
- Use something to protect you from the sun, or wear a hat
- Use the shade, and take regular breaks
- On days when the weather is fine, go outside as little as possible during the day

So that heat doesn't build up in the body
- Wear clothes that let air through and absorb sweat and dry quickly
- Cool the body with cold packs, ice, cold towels and so on

Drink water regularly

Indoors or outdoors, even if we don't feel thirsty, let's drink water regularly.
