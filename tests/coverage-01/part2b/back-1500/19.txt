Easy scrambled eggs

What you need
- 3 eggs
- milk, 2 or 3 tablespoons (if you like)
- butter, 2 tablespoons
- salt, ¼ teaspoon (or to taste)
- black seeds, ⅛ teaspoon (or to taste)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you like) and the salt.
3. With a mixer or a fork, beat the mixture until it has lots of bubbles.
4. Melt the butter in a flat pan, on medium heat.
5. Pour the eggs into the pan.
6. Stir the mixture while it cooks, to break up the eggs, and to let more of the raw egg touch the hot pan.
   Stirring fast makes small pieces, but stirring more slowly makes bigger pieces.
7. Cook until just before you feel the eggs are ready, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them out of the pan.
   The eggs are ready when their shine goes away.
   Stop cooking just before that.
