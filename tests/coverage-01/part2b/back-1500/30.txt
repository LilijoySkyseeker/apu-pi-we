In Addis Ababa, customers complain that the price of small grain is rising fast, and life is hard for them.

In the city of Addis Ababa, customers who say that the price of small grain is rising fast complain that life is becoming hard for them.

They said that two months ago, one kilo of white small grain was 80 birr, but now it sells for as much as 150 birr.

City residents told Voice of America that, because of the fighting and unrest that have spread to the places in Amhara that grow small grain, there isn't enough grain in the market, and there is no small grain either.

Mr./Ms. Mesfan Assefa, the deputy head of the trade office in the city of Addis Ababa, said that grain is still coming into Addis Ababa from all over the country, including from Amhara.
They also announced that the city government has put 1 400 000 000 birr toward this, and is still working to increase the supply of produce.

Mr./Ms. Gete Andualem, a professor of the ways of selling at Addis Ababa University, also showed that the price of grain is rising fast because there is no peace, but also because of other economic reasons.
