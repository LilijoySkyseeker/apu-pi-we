How do you prevent mosquito diseases?

To stop the spread of mosquito diseases and other diseases that people get from the Aedes aegypti mosquito, the best way is to remove the places where mosquitoes breed, or make them harmless.
That means all containers or anything that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive cold and dry conditions, it is important to keep preventing and checking for mosquito breeding places continuously, all year round.

Ideas for prevention

- Get rid of containers that aren't in use, anything that can collect water (metal containers, bottles, car tyres, and so on), inside and outside the home or workplace.
- Make holes in containers that can't be used, before throwing them away.
  If you can't, break them or crush them.
  Put them in closed bags, so that the garbage collectors can take them away safely.
