Step by step

Before anything else, prepare the place where you keep the computer, following these ideas.
- I'd suggest using a wide table that is empty.
- If you like, work standing up, so you can move more freely.
- Choose a place that has a lot of light.

Gather the materials you need.
- A Phillips screwdriver.
- A thin-bristled brush (it can be a paintbrush).
- Thermal paste (you can buy it at a computer shop).
- A wiping cloth, or paper towels.
- A white school eraser.
- A blower.
- A flat screwdriver (if you like).
- A hand lamp (if you like).
- A spray liquid for cleaning contacts (if you like).
- Black powder, or sewing machine oil (if you like).

Step 1, open the computer
1. Unplug all the cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, network, phone, printer, and so on).
2. Open the computer case (some people wrongly call it the CPU), by removing the small fasteners at the back (some have only clips), and by taking off the metal cover.
