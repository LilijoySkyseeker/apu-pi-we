There is a road emergency in Buenos Aires because of rain and flooding

The city of Buenos Aires is in road trouble, because of heavy rain and flooding on important roads of the Argentine capital.
Almost all the access routes into the city are under water, and the underground trains are running late and some aren't running, as with the trains and buses.
Some districts of the city are without electricity, to protect people from accidents.
The same situation is also in the cities near Buenos Aires.
