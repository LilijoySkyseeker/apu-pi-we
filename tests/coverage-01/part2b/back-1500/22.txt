In Yoruba land, we don't usually say to people, "Come to my house to eat in the evening" or "I want to see you at noon, come eat with me".

But if a friend goes to visit his own friend at home, he eats just the food he finds at that man's house, at noon or in the evening.

Also, if he goes in the morning, he eats just what he finds at their house.

At festival times, like Christmas, Yoruba people like to kill animals, and like to cook food such as rice, or ground tubers, or crushed tubers.

This guest or the hosts eat just what they find.

That is also the same at weddings, or at the time when a child is given a name.
