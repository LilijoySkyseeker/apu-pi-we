Kemenhub RI says there is still room to carry motorcycles free of charge.

J.A. Barata, head of the Kemenhub RI public communication center, said on Monday (6/7) in Jakarta that people are urged to register quickly and to use the free motorcycle transport the government provides.

J.A. Barata added that people can still join the free motorcycle program the transport ministry has set up, since people can register until 8 July 2015, or until all the places are full.

"With this free transport, we are trying to shorten the motorcycle journey of people going home, all the way to their destination.
For example, from Jakarta to Semarang: if someone wants to go home to Kendal, they don't ride their own motorcycle; the train carries it, they ride the train and arrive in Semarang, and only at Semarang train station do they take their own motorcycle to continue on it to Kendal.
So the journey they would have had to make by motorcycle with great difficulty has become shorter; they don't do that," Barata explained.
