A JR West company worker used ICOCA to ride trains over and over without paying, and was removed from his job as punishment

According to news reports written by the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, JR West announced on the 5th of the month that a 61-year-old man who works at Mikunigaoka station on the Hanwa line, in the city of Sakai, was removed from his job as punishment on January 4, because he wrongly used an ICOCA card to ride ordinary JR trains over and over without paying, from Hiroshima to Ashiya in Hyogo prefecture, and did not pay ¥22,840 in fares.

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun report, that worker works at the company JR West Transportation Service, which JR West owns.
It appears he didn't pay while he was visiting a member of his own family who is in a hospital in Hiroshima.
