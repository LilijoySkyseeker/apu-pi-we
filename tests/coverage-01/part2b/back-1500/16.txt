Losing a bank card

Have you lost your bank card?
You must block it quickly.
If someone has paid with it, you can file a complaint with the law enforcement (police or gendarmerie).
If it isn't your fault, your bank returns the money that someone stole with it.
If getting the money back is difficult, you can complain to the banks' mediator, or to a court.
We show you the information you need to know.

1. Block the card

You must block it very quickly, which means that nobody can use your card.

To do that, contact your bank, or call the phone line that is only for blocking bank cards.
