Fuel shortage in Kenya is a problem for people who carry passengers on small motor vehicles

In Kenya, little fuel and higher fuel prices are causing work problems for young people who carry passengers on small motor vehicles.

Talking with Voice of America by phone, some of them said that because fuel is scarce, they have to buy fuel with a lot of money, while customers can't pay the large amount they ask for the trip, and so this work, which gives jobs to many young people in Kenya, is hard.

James Mwangi carries passengers on a small motor vehicle in the city of Nairobi, and said, "When we tell a customer the fare, we start arguing with them, and afterwards they say they can't pay that money."

Paul Kuja, who also carries passengers on a small motor vehicle in the city of Nairobi, added, "Because the fare has become high, customers walk, so we don't get customers."
