Now, to pick up parcels at Cainiao Yizhan, people must install Taobao

At Cainiao Yizhan, people now pick up parcels with Taobao, which reads a number, and that is a few times faster than picking up with the number from a text message, but people must get Taobao to pick up parcels, even if the parcel comes from Pinduoduo or Tmall or another online seller.
A Wikinews reporter tried it, and feels that Taobao's strong push is bad, and some buyers think that Taobao is big money that wants to take over the whole market.
