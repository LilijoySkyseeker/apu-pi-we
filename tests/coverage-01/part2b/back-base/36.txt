Meat that returns to the pot

What you need
Meat, 500 grams
Leaves of small white onions, 150 grams
Fat, 40 grams
Salt, 1 gram
Ground big seeds that come from Pixian (you can also use any ground big seeds in their place), 50 grams
Black salty water, 25 grams
Raw hot root, 15 grams
Green onion, 20 grams
Hot seeds, at least 10

How to make it
1. As for the meat that has skin and fat, wash it clean.
2. Put boiling water in a pot, put it on a strong fire, and add the meat and green onion, hot root and hot seeds.
Boil the meat until it is done, but not until it falls apart; while it boils, remove the white stuff on top of the water, wash the small white onion leaves clean, cut them into pieces 2 to 3 centimetres long, and cut the ground big seeds fine.
3. As for the meat you took out of the water, let the smoke leave it, and while it is still hot, cut it into pieces that are about 0.3 centimetres thick and have skin.
