Step by step

First of all, prepare the place where you will keep the computer, following these ideas.
- I suggest using a wide, flat, empty wooden surface.
- If you want, work standing, so you can move more freely.
- Choose a place that has a lot of light.

Gather the things you need.
- A Phillips screwdriver.
- A thin brush (a wall brush will do).
- Thick heat paste (you can buy it at computer shops).
- A cleaning cloth, or cleaning paper.
- A white school eraser.
- Something that makes a blast of air.
- A flat screwdriver (if you want).
- A hand light (if you want).
- Spray liquid for cleaning the connectors (if you want).
- Black powder, or sewing-machine oil (if you want).

Step 1, open the computer
1. Remove all the cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, internet, phone, printer, and others).
2. Open the computer's case (some people wrongly call it the CPU), by removing the small locking pieces at the back (some only have holders), and by taking off the hard side panel.
