People in Finland pay by phone to ride underground trains and trams

Now, three thousand people living in Helsinki pay by phone to ride underground trains and trams.
According to a report from RIA Novosti, this new method started in December 2001, and travellers quickly came to like it.

According to Kirsi Niemelä, who speaks for public transport in Helsinki, this new method works well not only because paying afterwards is better than paying with cash, but also because it is happening while the euro is starting in Finland.
It means that people who buy tickets by phone don't have to look for small change, because the ticket's 1.4 euros is added to the phone bill by itself.

According to the ticket inspectors, this new method is still not easy.
They say that many travellers pay, that is, send the ticket text message, after getting on the train or tram, but according to the inspectors, you should do this before getting on.
