Ways not to get heat illness

To protect yourself from heat illness, it is important to do the right things for the place you are in.
When someone says "warning: heat illness", let's hardly go outside, and hide from the heat.

Hide from the heat, protect yourself

Inside
- Set the temperature with an air cooler, or something like it
- Use cloth that doesn't let light through, and things that make shade
- Regularly check the room temperature
- Also look at the WBGT number

Outside
- Use something that protects you from the sun, or wear something on your head
- Use shade, and rest regularly
- On days when the weather is fine, hardly go outside during the day

So that heat doesn't build up in the body
- Wear clothes that let air through and absorb sweat and dry quickly
- Cool the body with cold packs, ice, cold cloths and so on

Drink water regularly

Inside or outside, even if we don't feel we need water, let's drink water regularly.
