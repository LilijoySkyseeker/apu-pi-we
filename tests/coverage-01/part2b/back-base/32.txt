Kemenhub RI says there are still places for carrying power carts without paying.

J.A. Barata, who is the head of the office for talking to the public of Kemenhub RI, said on Monday (6/7) in Jakarta that people are called on to register their names quickly, and to use the transport that the country's group provides for power carts without paying.

J.A. Barata added that people can still get into the free transport for power carts that the country's transport group runs, because people can register their names until 8 July 2015, or until all the places are full.

"With this free transport, we are trying to make the power-cart journey of people returning home shorter, up to the place they want to go.
For example, from Jakarta to Semarang: if someone wants to return home to Kendal, they don't use their own power cart; the train carries it, they travel by train and arrive in Semarang, and only at Semarang's train station do they take their own power cart to continue with it to Kendal.
So the journey that they would have had to make by power cart with great difficulty has become short; they don't do that," he said.
