How to make it

Method
Clean two kinds of green leaves, and wash them well in water, twice.
After washing, cut them small, and put them with 1 litre of water in a closed pot, to boil.
After 5 sounds, take the pot off the fire, and after it has cooled, add 50 grams of ground yellow seeds, and grind it in a grinder very finely.

Then, in a deep pot, after 1 big small cup of clean oil has become hot, cook onion, hot root, small white onions, bitter leaves, green hot fruit and red fruit in the oil; then add the green leaves ground in the grinder, mix it, and cook it for a short time, 10 minutes; then add milk fat and fragrant leaves, and it is ready.
