Losing a bank card

Have you lost your bank card?
You must block it quickly.
If someone has paid with it, you can complain to the police (the police or the gendarmerie).
If the problem isn't your fault, your bank gives back the money that someone stole with it.
If getting the money back is difficult, you can complain to the banks' mediator, or to a court.
We show you what you need to know.

1. Block the card

You must block it very quickly, which means nobody can use your card.

To do that, call your bank, or call the phone line that is only for blocking bank cards.
