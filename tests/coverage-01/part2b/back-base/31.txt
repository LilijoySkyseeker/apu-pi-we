Thai people who live in America have great fun at Songkran, which is the Thai new year.

After being unable to return to Thailand for many years, Thanyarat Milanda and Phawadee Rattanawet, who live in the city of Los Angeles, talked about their own feelings as Thai people in another country, when they came to celebrate the Thai new year and each year's Songkran at Wat Thai Los Angeles in California.

Gathering and talking, doing good with one another, doing things of the Buddha's way, putting water on the hands of old people to receive good from them, and putting water on the Buddha's followers and on things made in the likeness of the Buddha, in the Thai way, within the community of Thai people in another country, helps many people a great deal to miss their own homes less.
