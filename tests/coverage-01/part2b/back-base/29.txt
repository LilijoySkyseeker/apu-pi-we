In Nigeria, people have seen the moon that starts the Ramadan fast.

From Washington DC, a leader of the Muslims said that the leaders and Muslim groups had told them that people had certainly seen the moon of Ramadan, and they had agreed.

He called on all the Muslims of Nigeria to start the Ramadan fast tomorrow, on Monday.

He also called on Muslims everywhere in Nigeria not to stop talking with God, so that the country leaves the difficult time it has entered.

Earlier, people working for Saudi Arabia had told people that the new moon of Ramadan had been seen.
After a short time, many Arab countries in the Gulf region, and Iraq and Syria, followed the announcement, saying that they too would certainly start the fast on Monday.

The leaders were also sending each other happy messages that the honoured month of Ramadan had begun.
