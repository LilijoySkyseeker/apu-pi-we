A JR West group employee used ICOCA to ride trains again and again without paying, and has been dismissed

According to news reports written by the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, JR West announced on the 5th of the month that a 61-year-old man who works at Mikunigaoka station on the Hanwa line, in Sakai city, was dismissed on January 4, because he had wrongly used an ICOCA card to ride JR trains again and again without paying, from Hiroshima to Ashiya in Hyogo, and did not pay ¥22,840 in fares.

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun's report, that employee works at JR West Transportation Service, a company that JR West owns.
It seems he didn't pay around the times he went to see a member of his own family who is in hospital in Hiroshima.
