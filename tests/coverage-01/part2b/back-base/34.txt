Broken bread is one of the foods that very many people know in Sham, more so in Damascus, and its name is Tasqiyeh or Tas'iyeh.

Also, it is usually an important food at the Friday morning meal, when the whole family gathers.

Broken bread of Sham

What you need
A cup of whole round seeds
A cup of sour milk
Ground white seeds
The juice of 1 sour fruit
1 small white onion
Bread
3 small cups of milk fat
Salt
Ground red hot fruit, or something ground, red and sour
Tree seeds and fragrant leaves, to make it look beautiful

How to make it
We wash the round seeds, and leave them for 8 hours, or from evening until morning, in water that has been boiled with 1 very small cup of white salt; then we remove the water, boil them, and keep the boiling water.
In a crusher, we put the boiled round seeds, sour milk, ground white seeds, small white onion, sour-fruit juice, a little of the round-seed water, and salt, and mix it.
We break the bread into pieces that are neither big nor small, and put them in the water of the boiled round seeds.

How to serve it
We put the bread in a flat box, and put the round-seed mixture on top of it with the other ingredients; we heat the milk fat, and put it on top with a few fragrant seeds and red hot fruit or the ground red sour thing, and also make it look beautiful with cooked tree seeds and cut fragrant leaves.
