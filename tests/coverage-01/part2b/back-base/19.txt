Easy stirred eggs

What you need
- Eggs, 3
- Milk, 2 or 3 tablespoons (if you want)
- Butter, 2 tablespoons
- Salt, ¼ teaspoon (or to your taste)
- Black pepper, ⅛ teaspoon (or to your taste)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you want) and the salt.
3. With a whisk or a fork, beat the mixture until it has a lot of air in it.
4. Melt the butter in a flat pan, over half heat.
5. Pour the eggs into the flat pan.
6. Stir the mixture while it cooks, to break up the eggs, and to let more raw egg touch the hot pan.
   Stirring fast makes small pieces, but stirring more slowly makes bigger pieces.
7. Cook until a moment before you feel the eggs are ready, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them off the pan.
   The eggs are ready when their shine goes away.
   Stop cooking a moment before that.
