In Yoruba land, we don't usually say to people, "Come to my house to eat in the evening" or "I want to see you at noon, come eat with me".

But if a friend goes to see their own friend at home, they eat just the food they find at that man's house, at noon or in the evening.

Also, if they go in the morning, it's whatever they find in their hosts' house that they eat.

At the time of some important day, such as Christmas, Yoruba people like to kill animals, and like to cook food such as rice, or ground tubers, or crushed tubers.

This person who comes, or the people they see, eat just what they find.

That is also the same at the time of a wedding, or at the time when people give a child a name.
