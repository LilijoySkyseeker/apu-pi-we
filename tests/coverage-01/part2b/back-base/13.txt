There are big road problems in Buenos Aires because of rain and a lot of water

The city of Buenos Aires has road problems because of heavy rain and a lot of water on the important roads of Argentina's important city.
Almost all the roads that enter the city are under water, and the underground trains are running late and some aren't running, as with trains and buses.
Some parts of the city are without power, to protect people from problems.
This problem is also in the cities near Buenos Aires.
