In Addis Ababa, buyers complain that the price of small seeds is rising fast, and life is hard for them.

In the city of Addis Ababa, buyers who say that the price of small seeds is rising fast complain that life is becoming hard for them.

They said that two months ago, one kilo of white small seeds was 80 birr, but now people sell it for up to 150 birr.

City residents told Voice of America that, because of fighting and unrest that has spread to the places in Amhara that grow small seeds, there aren't enough food seeds in the market, and there are also no small seeds.

The honourable Mesfan Assefa, who is the second leader of the selling-and-buying group in the city of Addis Ababa, said that food seeds are coming into Addis Ababa from all parts of the country, including from Amhara.
He also told people that the group that leads the city has put 1 400 000 000 birr toward this, and is working to increase the produce coming in.

The honourable Gete Andualem, who is a teacher of the methods of selling at the place of learning in Addis Ababa, also showed that the price of food seeds is rising fast because there is no peace, but also because of other money reasons.
