Food of grass-seed water

What do we make it with?
3 onions
500 grams of grass seeds
Green plants
3 small green fruits
3 yellow roots
Big seeds
3 small white onions
1 big hot fruit
3 red fruits
Fragrant leaves
Salt as you like

How to make it
1. You must wash the green things with water, and cut them; then you must remove the skin of the tubers.
2. In a pot, you must put water with salt until it boils.
3. You must put all the green things into the pot, and you must boil them for 30 minutes.
4. In a wide pot, you must cook the grass seeds dry, until they are cooked and yellow.
5. On a grinding stone, you must grind the grass seeds; then you must wash them with cold water in a big cup.
6. You must put water with the green things and the grass seeds into the pot.
7. You must put the tubers into the pot, until it has boiled for 30 minutes.
