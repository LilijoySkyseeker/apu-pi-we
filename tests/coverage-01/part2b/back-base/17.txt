How do people protect themselves from mosquito illnesses?

To stop mosquito illnesses and the other illnesses people get from the Aedes aegypti mosquito, the best way is to remove the places where mosquitoes breed, or make them harmless.
That means all containers or all things that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive cold and dry conditions, it is important to check for and remove mosquito breeding places without stopping, all year round.

Ideas for protecting yourself

- Remove unused containers, all those that can collect water (food containers, drink containers, car tyres, and others), inside and outside the house or workplace.
- Puncture containers that can't be used, before throwing them away.
  If you can't, break them or crush them.
  Put them in a closed bag, so that the garbage collectors can take them away safely.
