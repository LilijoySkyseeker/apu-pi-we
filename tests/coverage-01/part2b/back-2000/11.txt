A worker at the company JR West used ICOCA to ride trains again and again without paying, and has been dismissed from the job as punishment

According to news reports written by the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, JR West announced on the 5th of the month that a man who is 61 years old and works at Mikunigaoka Station on the Hanwa Line, in the city of Sakai, was dismissed from the job as punishment on January 4, because he used an ICOCA card improperly to ride ordinary JR trains again and again without paying, on trips such as from Hiroshima to Ashiya in Hyogo Province, and avoided paying ¥22,840 in fares.

According to the news written by the Yomiuri Shimbun, that worker works at the company JR West Transportation Service, which JR West owns.
It seems he avoided paying around the times when he visited a member of his own family who is in hospital in Hiroshima.
