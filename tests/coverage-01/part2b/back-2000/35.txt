Wheat soup

What do we make it with?
3 onions
500 grams of wheat
Green plants
3 cucumbers
3 carrots
Big beans
3 small white onions
1 big hot fruit
3 tomatoes
Herbs
Salt to taste

Method of preparation
1. You must wash the vegetables with water, and cut them; then you must peel the potatoes.
2. In a pot, you must put water with salt until it boils.
3. You must put all the vegetables into the pot; you must boil them for 30 minutes.
4. In a wide pot, you must toast the wheat until it is cooked and golden.
5. On a grinding stone, you must grind the wheat; then you must wash it with cold water in a bowl.
6. You must put the water with the vegetables, and the wheat, into the pot.
7. You must put the potatoes into the pot, until it has boiled for 30 minutes.
