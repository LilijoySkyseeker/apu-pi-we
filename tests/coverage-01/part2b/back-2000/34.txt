Broken bread is one of the very famous foods in Sham, especially in Dimashq, and its name is Tasqiyeh or Tas'iyeh.

It is usually an important food at Friday breakfast, when all the members of the family gather.

Sham broken bread

Amounts
A cup of whole beans
A cup of yogurt
Ground white seeds
The juice of one sour fruit
One small white onion
Bread
3 spoons of butter
Salt
Ground red hot fruit, or something ground that is red and sour
Tree nuts and herbs, for decoration

Method of preparation
We wash the beans, and leave them for 8 hours, or from evening until morning, in water that has been boiled with 1 small spoon of white salt; then we remove the water, and boil them, and keep the cooking water.
In a mixer, we put the boiled beans, the yogurt, the ground white seeds, the small white onion, the sour fruit juice, a little of the bean water, and salt, and mix it.
We break the bread into medium pieces, and put them in the water from the boiled beans.

Method of serving
We spread the bread on a big plate, and pour the bean mixture over it with the other things needed; we heat the butter, and pour it over the top with a few fragrant seeds and red hot fruit or something ground that is red and sour, and we also decorate it with cooked tree nuts and chopped herbs.
