Step by step

Before anything else, prepare the place where you'll service the computer, following these ideas.
- It's best to use a wide, empty table.
- If you prefer, work standing up, so you can move more freely.
- Choose a place with plenty of light.

Gather the materials you need.
- A Phillips screwdriver.
- A brush with thin bristles (it can be a paintbrush).
- Thick heat oil (you can buy it at a computer shop).
- A wiping cloth, or paper towels.
- A white school eraser.
- Something that makes wind.
- A flat screwdriver (if you like).
- A hand lamp (if you like).
- A blowing liquid for cleaning contacts (if you like).
- Black powder, or oil for clothes machines (if you like).

Step 1, open the computer
1. Remove all the cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, network, phone, printer and others).
2. Open the computer's box (some people wrongly call it the CPU) by removing the small locking pieces on the back (some only have a holder), and taking off the metal cover.
