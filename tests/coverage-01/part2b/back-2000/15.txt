Kenya's fuel shortage is a challenge for those who carry people by motorcycle

In Kenya, the shortage of fuel and the higher price of fuel are creating job challenges for young people who carry people by motorcycle.

In a phone interview with Voice of America, some of them said that because of the fuel shortage, they have to buy fuel at a high price, while customers can't pay the high price they ask for a trip, and so this business, which gives jobs to many young people in Kenya, is struggling.

James Mwangi carries people by motorcycle in the city of Nairobi, and said, "When we tell a customer the price of the trip, we and they start an argument, and afterwards they say they can't pay that money."

Paul Kuja, who also carries people by motorcycle in the city of Nairobi, added, "Because the fare has gone up, customers walk, so we don't get customers."
