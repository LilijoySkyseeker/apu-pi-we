A person's illness
A: Who is that?
B: That's my father.
A: How is his body?
B: For 4 days his fever has been very high.
A: Did you go with him to the hospital?
B: I went with him yesterday.
A: What did the doctor say?
B: He said "fever with cold".
A: Did he give any medicine?
B: He did.
A: Everything will soon be well.
Please don't worry.
B: May God keep us.

An animal's illness
A: What illness does your cow have?
B: People say it is Komari.
A: Did you go with it to the animal hospital?
B: We didn't go.
The hospital is very far.
A: May I look at your cow?
B: Do you know about cows?
A: I know some things.
I studied animal medicine.
B: Please help us in some way.
A: I'll do everything I can.
B: May you live well.
If this cow dies, we lose our income.
A: Please don't worry.
If the cow is vaccinated, it will get well.
