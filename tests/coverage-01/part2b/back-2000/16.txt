Lost money card

Have you lost your money card?
You must lock it quickly.
If someone has paid with it, you can file a complaint with those who enforce the law (the police or the gendarmerie).
If it isn't your fault, your bank gives back the money that someone stole with it.
If getting the money back is difficult, you can complain to the banks' mediator, or to a court.
We show you the information you need to know.

1. Lock the card

You must lock it very quickly, which means that no one can use your card.

To do that, contact your bank, or call the phone line that is only for locking money cards.
