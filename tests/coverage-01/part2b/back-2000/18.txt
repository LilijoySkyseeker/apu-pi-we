Ways to prevent heat illness

To prevent heat illness, it's important to make things suitable for the place you're in.
When someone announces a "heat illness warning", let's go outside as little as possible, and avoid the heat.

Avoid the heat, protect yourself

Indoors
- Set the temperature with an air cooler, or something like it
- Use curtains that don't let light in, and things that make shade
- Check the room temperature regularly
- Also refer to the WBGT number

Outdoors
- Use something that protects you from the sun, or wear a hat
- Use shade, and rest regularly
- On days when the weather is good, go outside as little as possible during the day

So that heat doesn't build up in the body
- Wear clothes that let air through, absorb sweat and dry quickly
- Cool the body with cold bags, ice, cold towels and so on

Drink water regularly

Indoors or outdoors, even if we don't feel thirsty, let's drink water regularly.
