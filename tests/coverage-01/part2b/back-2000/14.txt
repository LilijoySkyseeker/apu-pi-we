Now, to collect packages at Cainiao Yizhan, people must install the Taobao app

At Cainiao Yizhan, people now collect packages with Taobao, which reads a code, and that is a few times faster than collecting with the code from a message, but people must get the Taobao app to collect packages, even if the package comes from Pinduoduo or Tmall or another platform that sells on the internet.
A Wikinews journalist tried it, and felt that the Taobao app's strong pushing is not pleasant, and some customers think that Taobao is big money that wants to take the whole market.
