Meat that returns to the pot

Materials
Pork, 500 grams
Small white onion leaves, 150 grams
Pig fat, 40 grams
Salt, 1 gram
Bean sauce from Pixian (you can also substitute ordinary bean sauce), 50 grams
Black sauce, 25 grams
Fresh hot root, 15 grams
Green onion, 20 grams
Hot seeds, at least 10

Method
1. Wash the pork that has skin and fat clean.
2. Put water on to boil in the pot, put it over a strong fire, and add the pork and the green onion, hot root and hot seeds.
Boil the pork until it is done, but not until it falls apart, and while it boils, remove the bubbles on the surface of the water; wash the small white onion leaves clean, and cut them into pieces 2 to 3 centimetres long, and chop the bean sauce fine.
3. Let the steam leave the pork you have taken out of the water, and while it is still warm, cut it into pieces about 0.3 centimetres thick that have skin.
