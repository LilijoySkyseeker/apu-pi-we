Kemenhub RI says there are still places to carry motorcycles free of charge.

J.A. Barata, who is the head of the public communication centre of Kemenhub RI, said on Monday (6/7) in Jakarta that people are urged to register quickly, and to use the transport that the government provides for motorcycles free of charge.

J.A. Barata added that people can still join the program the Ministry of Transport set up for motorcycles free of charge, because people can register until 8 July 2015, or until all the places are full.

"With this free transport, we are trying to shorten the motorcycle journey of people going home, as far as their destination.
For example, from Jakarta to Semarang, if someone wants to go home to Kendal, they don't ride their own motorcycle; the train carries it, they ride the train and arrive in Semarang, and only at the Semarang train station do they take their own motorcycle to continue on it to Kendal.
So the journey they would have had to make by motorcycle with great difficulty has become short; they don't make it," he explained.
