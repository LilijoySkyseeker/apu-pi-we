In the land of the Yoruba, we don't usually say to people, "Come to my house to eat in the evening" or "I want to see you at noon, come and eat lunch with me".

But if a friend goes to visit his friend at home, he eats whatever food he finds at that man's house, at noon or in the evening.

And if he goes in the morning, he eats whatever he finds at their house.

At some festival times, such as Christmas, Yoruba people like to kill animals, and like to cook food such as rice, or tuber flour, or crushed tubers.

These guests or the hosts eat whatever they find.

That is also the same at weddings, or at the time when people give a child a name.
