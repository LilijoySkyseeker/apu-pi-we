In Addis Ababa, customers complain that the price of small grain is rising fast, and life is hard for them.

In the city of Addis Ababa, customers who say the price of small grain is rising fast complain that life is getting hard for them.

They said that two months ago, one kilo of white small grain was 80 birr, but now people sell it for as much as 150 birr.

City residents told the Voice of America that, because of the fighting and instability that have spread to the places in Amhara that grow small grain, there isn't enough grain in the market, and there is also no small grain.

Mr./Ms. Mesfan Assefa, who is the deputy of the trade office in the city of Addis Ababa, said that grain is still coming into Addis Ababa from all over the country, including from Amhara.
He also announced that the city government has put 1 400 000 000 birr toward this, and is working to increase the supply of the harvest.

Mr./Ms. Gete Andualem, who is a professor of marketing at Addis Ababa University, also points out that the price of grain is rising fast because there is no peace, but also because of other economic reasons.
