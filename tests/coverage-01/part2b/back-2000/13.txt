There is a road emergency in Buenos Aires because of rain and flooding

The city of Buenos Aires is on road alert, because of heavy rain and flooding on important roads of the capital of Argentina.
Almost all of the ways into the city are under water, and the underground trains are running late and some have been cancelled, as with trains and buses.
Some of the city's neighborhoods are without electricity, to avoid accidents.
The same situation is also found in the cities that surround Buenos Aires.
