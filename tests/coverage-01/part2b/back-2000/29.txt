In Nigeria, people have seen the moon that begins the Ramadan fast.

From Washington DC: the king of the Muslims said that the Muslim leaders and organizations had told him that the Ramadan moon had certainly been seen, and he agreed.

He called on all the Muslims of Nigeria to begin the Ramadan fast tomorrow, Monday.

And he called on the Muslim community everywhere in Nigeria not to stop praying, so that the country may leave the difficult time it has entered.

Before that, officials in Saudi Arabia had announced that the new moon of Ramadan had been seen.
A short time later, many Arab countries in the Gulf region, as well as Iraq and Syria, followed the announcement, saying that they too would certainly begin the fast on Monday.

Leaders were also exchanging happy messages that the holy month of Ramadan had begun.
