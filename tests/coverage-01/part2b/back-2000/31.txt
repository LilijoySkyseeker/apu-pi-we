Thai people who live in America celebrate very happily at Songkran, which is the Thai New Year.

Having been unable to return to Thailand for many years, Thanyarat Milanda and Phawadee Rattanawet, who live in the city of Los Angeles, talked about their own feelings as Thai people in another country, while they came to celebrate the Thai New Year and the yearly Songkran at Wat Thai Los Angeles in California.

Gathering and talking, making merit together, doing Buddhist religious activities, pouring water on the hands of old people to receive blessings from them, and pouring water on Buddhist priests and on statues of the Buddha, in the Thai style, in a community of Thai people in another country, helps many people a great deal to miss their own birthplace less.
