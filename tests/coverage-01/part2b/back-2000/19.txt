Easy scrambled eggs

What you need
- 3 eggs
- Milk, 2 or 3 tablespoons (if you like)
- Butter, 2 tablespoons
- Salt, ¼ teaspoon (or to taste)
- Black seed, ⅛ teaspoon (or to taste)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you like) and the salt.
3. With a mixing tool or a fork, beat the mixture until it has lots of bubbles.
4. Melt the butter in a flat pan over medium heat.
5. Pour the eggs into the flat pan.
6. Stir the mixture while it cooks, to break up the eggs, and to let more of the raw egg touch the hot pan.
   Stirring fast makes small pieces, but stirring more slowly makes bigger pieces.
7. Cook until a moment before you feel the eggs are ready, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them out of the pan.
   The eggs are ready when their shine disappears.
   Stop cooking a moment before that.
