How do people prevent mosquito diseases?

To stop the spread of mosquito diseases and other diseases that people get from the Aedes aegypti mosquito, the most effective way is to remove the places where mosquitoes breed, or make them harmless.
That means every container, or anything, that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive through cold and dry times, it's important to keep preventing and checking mosquito breeding places, all year round.

Ideas for prevention

- Remove all unused containers that can collect water (metal containers, bottles, car wheels and others), inside and outside the house or workplace.
- Make holes in containers that can't be used, before throwing them away.
  If you can't, break them or crush them.
  Put them in a closed bag, so the garbage collectors can take them safely.
