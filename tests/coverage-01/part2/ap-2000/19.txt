lite bunle pi tika man gun ⟨mix⟩

⟨ingredient⟩ la
- tika 3
- ⟨spoon⟩ kudu se 2 tun 3 pi tunlo (don me momu)
- ⟨spoon⟩ kudu 2 pi ⟨butter⟩
- ⟨spoon⟩ meli ¼ pi bunwu (lu fu me tana)
- ⟨spoon⟩ meli ⅛ pi yikan tago (lu fu me tana)

lite
1. tin alun tika la tun ladon pi ⟨measure⟩.
2. tin ginni tunlo (don me momu) yen bunwu.
3. gan du ⟨mix⟩ lu yetu pi nawi, tin ⟨mix⟩ bu fu yobe, anko bu ibe wunta feli.
4. tin muge ⟨liquid⟩ ⟨butter⟩ mon ⟨bowl⟩ mawun, gan lumi ⟨middle⟩
5. tin ⟨pour⟩ tika la tun ⟨bowl⟩ mawun.
6. tin ⟨mix⟩ bu, li alun tika nin bu le siten, yen li efon ⟨liquid⟩ ke feli sanne ⟨bowl⟩ mawun tikun.
⟨mix⟩ yobe muge musu meli la, un ⟨mix⟩ gake muge musu ke kudu la.
7. tin siten anko tika la nedin wonwe, gi tika la ⟨continue⟩ siten wo me yidu bu la se ⟨bowl⟩ mawun.
tika la wonwe nin ⟨shine⟩ pi bu la ⟨disappear⟩.
mon foke ya ben muwo, tin bamo siten.
