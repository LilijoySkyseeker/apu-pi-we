agi mise pi ⟨bank⟩

min me sin agi mise pi ⟨bank⟩ pi me?
me keya tapi mise pi me fu yobe.
don ewo bo sin unfu gan mise pi me, me lenu tayin tun polisu (polisu lu en gendarmerie).
don me mu ibe ⟨fault⟩, ⟨bank⟩ pi me neke me, nimo man ewo bo sin ⟨spend⟩ gan ⟨fraud⟩.
don me ibe batu li dunu nimo pi me moti, me lenu sege batu tun ewo man ⟨settle⟩ batu pi ⟨bank⟩ la, lu tun ⟨court⟩.
din wuye me ⟨information⟩ man me wupi lugo.

1. tapi mise

me keya tapi mise fu yobe, yofu ⟨block⟩ bone pi mise pi me.

li ben, tin ⟨contact⟩ ⟨bank⟩ pi me, lu yebin ⟨server⟩ pi ⟨bank⟩ la man gun na li tapi mise.

go neke me wifu pi ⟨register⟩.

gi tan, me ibe ⟨record⟩ pi mido pi me, man ibe ⟨date⟩.
