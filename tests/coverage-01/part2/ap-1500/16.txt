agi mise pi ⟨bank⟩

min me sin agi mise pi ⟨bank⟩ pi me ?
me keya tapi mise pi me nuwen sefu.
don ewo bo sin unfu gan mise pi me, me lenu tayin tun no kuwi wabun (polisu lu en gendarmerie).
don ben mu gun ⟨fault⟩ pi me, ⟨bank⟩ pi me neke sigo me, nimo man ewo bo sin bone fu ⟨fraud⟩.
don me ibe batu nin me walo dunu nimo sigo, me lenu iko batu tun no ⟨settle⟩ batu pi ⟨bank⟩ kiyo lu tun ⟨court⟩.
din wuye me ⟨information⟩ man me wupi lugo.

1. tapi mise

me keya tapi mise fu yobe, tan yofu wun me ⟨block⟩ bone pi mise pi me.

li tan, tin ⟨contact⟩ ⟨bank⟩ pi me, lu yebin liga man ⟨bank⟩ la we bone na li tapi mise la.

go neke me wifu pi ⟨register⟩.

gi tan, me ibe ⟨record⟩ pi mido pi me, gan ⟨date⟩.
