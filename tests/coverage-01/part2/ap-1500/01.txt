⟨doctor⟩: min me gun upe, mole?
mole: to gun upe, ⟨doctor⟩.
⟨doctor⟩: me ibe batu yu?
mole: tiwu pi to gun simi.
⟨doctor⟩: wan gun simi fu yu?
mole: wan ibe wekun pi suyon, yen gabu.
⟨doctor⟩: min wan feme tikun lu weki?
mole: wan feme bi tikun, yen fe susin.
⟨doctor⟩: simi sin tuko mon muyi yu?
mole: bu sin tuko ya dili sa.
⟨doctor⟩: min wan desin fu osa?
mole: wan fe desin fu osa.
⟨doctor⟩: wan nume fawu wifu yu?
mole: fawu ka yen dunan ufo.
⟨doctor⟩: gi tan, de to neke wan, ⟨medicine⟩ pi koye yen ⟨needle⟩.
⟨doctor⟩: pi ⟨medicine⟩ tan pi koye, wan koye ⟨spoon⟩ meli fi, gimu ka mon dili mesun.
wan koye bu nin nelon fi, yen to nagu wan moti wo nelon fi.
mole: to unfu nimo wifu yu?
⟨doctor⟩: me unfu wo tiwu feme upe.
mole: lota.
⟨doctor⟩: upe isi.
