Kenya's fuel shortage is a challenge for motorcycle taxis

In Kenya, the shortage of fuel and the rise in its price create a job challenge for young people who work with motorcycle taxis, whose popular name is "boda boda".

In interviews with Voice of America, some of them said by phone that because of the fuel shortage they must buy fuel at a high price, while customers can't pay the large amount they charge, and that makes it a challenge to manage this business, which gives jobs to many young people in Kenya.

James Mwangi, who drives a motorcycle taxi in the city of Nairobi, said: "When you tell a customer the price of the ride, you start an argument, and afterwards they say they can't pay that money."

Paul Kuja, who also works with a motorcycle taxi in the city of Nairobi, added: "Because the price of rides has risen, customers walk, and so we lose customers."
