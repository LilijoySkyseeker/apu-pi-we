Doctor: Are you well, Mother?
Mother: I am well, Doctor.
Doctor: What problem do you have?
Mother: My child is sick.
Doctor: How is the child sick?
Mother: They have a pain in the belly, and they vomit.
Doctor: Do they feel hot or cold?
Mother: They feel very hot, and they also shake.
Doctor: When did the sickness start?
Mother: It started two days ago.
Doctor: Is their stool watery?
Mother: Yes, their stool is also watery.
Doctor: How old are they?
Mother: Three years and six months.
Doctor: So I will give them medicine to drink, and a needle.
Doctor: As for this medicine to drink, they take one small spoonful three times each day.
They take it for one week, and I will see them again a week from now.
Mother: How much money do I pay?
Doctor: You pay after the child feels well.
Mother: Thank you.
Doctor: Go well.
