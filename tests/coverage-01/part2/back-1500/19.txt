Easy scrambled eggs

Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 2 to 3 tablespoons of milk (if you like)
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- ¼ teaspoon of salt (or to taste)
- ⅛ teaspoon of black seeds (or to taste)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you like) and the salt.
3. With a whisk or a fork, beat quickly until it is full of air.
4. Melt the butter in a flat pan over medium heat
5. Pour the eggs into the flat pan.
6. Turn them to break up the eggs while they are still cooking, and to let more of the liquid touch the hot pan.
If you turn quickly, the eggs become small pieces, but if you turn more slowly, the eggs become bigger pieces.
7. Cook until a short moment before you feel they are ready, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them out of the pan.
The eggs are ready when their shine disappears.
Stop cooking a short moment before that happens.
