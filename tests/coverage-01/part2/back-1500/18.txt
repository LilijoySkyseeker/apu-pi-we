Preventive measures, so as not to get heat illness

To prevent heat illness, it is important to use methods that suit the place.
When "danger of heat illness" is announced, let's not go outside if we can avoid it, and let's avoid heat.

Avoid heat, protect yourself

Indoors
- Set the temperature with a room cooler or something else
- Use dark cloth on the windows, and wooden screens
- Check the room temperature often
- Also look at the WBGT number

Outdoors
- Use a sunshade or wear a hat
- Use shade, and rest often
- On sunny days, don't go outside during the day, if you can avoid it

So the body doesn't build up heat
- Wear clothes that let air through, absorb sweat and dry quickly
- Cool the body with cold packs, ice, a cold cloth or something else

Drink water often

Indoors or outdoors, even if we don't want to drink, let's drink water often
