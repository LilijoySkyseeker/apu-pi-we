Step by step

Before anything else, prepare the place where you will fix up the computer, and follow these tips:
- I suggest using a wide table with nothing on top of it;
- if you think it's better, work standing up, so you can move more freely;
- choose a place with a lot of light.

Gather the materials you need:
- a Phillips screwdriver.
- a cleaning brush with fine bristles (it can be a paintbrush).
- heat paste (you can buy it at a computer shop).
- a cleaning cloth or cleaning paper.
- a white school eraser.
- a blower.
- a flat screwdriver (if you like).
- a light (if you like).
- a liquid that cleans connectors (if you like).
- carbon powder or sewing-machine oil (if you like).

Step 1 - open the computer
1. Remove all the cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, network, phone, printer, and others).
2. Open the computer box (some people wrongly call it the "CPU"), remove the screws at the back (some only have squeeze clips), and remove the metal cover.
