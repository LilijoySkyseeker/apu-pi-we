A worker at a company of the JR West group is removed from work as punishment because he rode without paying many times with ICOCA

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, JR West announced on the 5th of the month that it had removed a male worker (aged 61) at Mikunigaoka Station (Sakai city) on the Hanwa Line from work as punishment on January 4 (UTC+9), because he had wrongly used an ICOCA card to ride JR's normal lines without paying many times, even from Hiroshima to Ashiya (Ashiya city, in Hyogo Prefecture), and avoided paying ¥22 840.

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun, this worker works for JR West Transportation Service, which is a company that JR West owns.
It seems he avoided paying when he visited a member of his own family at a hospital in Hiroshima, and on other occasions.
