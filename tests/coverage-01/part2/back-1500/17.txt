How do you prevent dengue?

For dengue and the illnesses that are transmitted by the Aedes aegypti mosquito, the most effective way is to remove or make safe the places where mosquitoes breed, to cut their cycle of transmission.
This means all containers or all things that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive low temperatures and dry periods, it is important to prevent and check mosquito breeding places continually, all year round.

Prevention ideas

- Remove all containers that are not in use and can collect water (metal containers, bottles, car wheels, and others), inside and outside the house or workplace.
- Make holes in containers that can't be used, before throwing them away.
If you can't, break them or crush them.
Put them in a closed bag, so that the garbage collector takes them away safely.
