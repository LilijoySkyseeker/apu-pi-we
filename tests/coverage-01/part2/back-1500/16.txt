Losing a bank card

Have you lost your bank card?
You must close your card without waiting.
If someone has paid with your card, you can complain to the law enforcers (the police or the gendarmerie).
If it isn't your fault, your bank gives you back the money that someone used fraudulently.
If you have a problem when you try to get the money back, you can take the problem to the bank's dispute settler or to a court.
We show you the information you need to know.

1. Close the card

You must close the card quickly, which means you block the use of your card.

To do this, contact your bank, or call the phone number that all banks use only for closing cards.

You are given a registration number.

So you have a record of your request, with the date.
