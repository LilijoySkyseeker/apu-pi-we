Don't put warm food in the fridge?

A: I had some leftover food, which I was about to keep in the fridge, but my mother said I shouldn't do that, because it was still warm.
I should put it on the table until it reaches room temperature, before I put it in the fridge.
She said the same thing when we bought something warm from a shop.
What is the problem?
Does a faster change in temperature make food bad in some way?
If I put something warm in the fridge (only food I didn't eat all of, not something big), does the inside of the fridge become warm for a short moment, and do the other foods in it rot faster?
Or is this whole thing just foolishness?
Thank you.

B: Philippa Hudson, who lectures on food safety at Bournemouth University, says: "Check that the food has become cold before you put it in the fridge."
