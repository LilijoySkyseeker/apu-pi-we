To take parcels at Cainiao stations, people must now install Taobao software

Cainiao stations have changed to a new way in which people read a code with Taobao software to take a parcel, and it is many times faster than the old message-code way, but people must get the Taobao software to take a parcel, even if the parcel comes from Pinduoduo, Tmall, or some other platform that sells on the internet.
A Wikinews journalist tried it, and felt that people don't like being pushed into Taobao software, and some customers think that Taobao is big money that is still taking the whole market.
