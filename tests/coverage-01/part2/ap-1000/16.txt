agi pi mise pi ⟨bank⟩

min me sin agi mise pi ⟨bank⟩ pi me ?
me keya udin mise pi me eda.
don ewo bo sin unfu gan mise pi me, me lenu tayin tun polisu (polisu lu polisu pi ⟨army⟩).
don ben mu gun ⟨fault⟩ pi me, ⟨bank⟩ pi me muge sigo nimo man ewo bo sin bunsi.
don nimo mu sigo fu bunle, me lenu isi tun no muge bonwi pi ⟨bank⟩ la lu tun ⟨court⟩.
din wuye me ⟨information⟩ man me wupi lugo.
