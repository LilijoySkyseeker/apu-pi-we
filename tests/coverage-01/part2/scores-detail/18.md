Notes:
- All sizes say "heat illness" for heatstroke. The Japanese source term (熱中症) is in fact "heat illness", so this loss is partly an artefact of the English gloss.
- Bamboo blinds become "shades" / "wooden shades" / "wooden screens" in base, 1000, 1500.
- 1000 "notice" for alert, "cloth that doesn't let light through" for blackout curtains, "something that protects you from the sun" for parasol, "something on your head" for hat.
- 1500 "dark cloth on the windows" for blackout curtains.
- base "cooling items", 2000 "cooling devices" for ice packs.
- 2000 drops "as little / much as possible" twice (the first time as "avoid going outside").
