Step by step

First of all, prepare the place where you will maintain the computer, following these ideas.
- Prefer a wide table with nothing on top of it.
- If you think it's better, work standing up, so you can move more freely.
- Choose a place with a lot of light.

Gather the materials you need.
- A Phillips screwdriver.
- A brush with fine bristles for wiping (a paintbrush is good too).
- Thermal cream (you can buy it in computer shops).
- A cleaning cloth or wiping paper.
- A white school eraser.
- An air blower.
- A flat screwdriver (if you like).
- A light (if you like).
- A blower that cleans contacts (if you like).
- Carbon powder or sewing machine oil (if you like).

Step 1, open the computer
1. Remove all the cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, network, phone, printer, and others).
2. Open the computer case (which some people wrongly call the CPU) by removing the pins at the back (some only have clips), and removing the metal cover.
