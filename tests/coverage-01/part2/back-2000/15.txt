Fuel shortage in Kenya, a challenge for boda boda

In Kenya, the fuel shortage and the rise in its price are creating a challenge for the work of young people who carry passengers on motorcycles, whose popular name is "boda boda".

In a phone interview with Voice of America, some of them said that because of the fuel shortage, they have to buy fuel at a high price, while customers can't pay the high price they ask for a ride, and so the boda boda business, which gives work to many young people in Kenya, is facing a challenge.

James Mwangi, who drives a motorcycle in the city of Nairobi, said, "When you tell a customer the price of the ride, you start an argument, and then he says he can't pay that money"

Paul Kuja, who also carries passengers by motorcycle in the city of Nairobi, added, "Because the price of rides has gone up, customers walk, and so we lose customers".
