JR West group company worker who rode without paying many times using ICOCA is dismissed as punishment

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, on Friday JR West announced that on January 4 (UTC+9) it had dismissed as punishment a male worker (aged 61) at Mikunigaoka station on the Hanwa line (Sakai city), because he had misused the ICOCA ticket card system to ride many times without paying on ordinary JR lines, for example from Hiroshima to Ashiya (Ashiya city, Hyogo prefecture), and avoided paying 22,840 yen.

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun, this worker worked for JR West Transportation Service, which is a subsidiary of JR West.
He apparently avoided paying around the times when he visited a relative of his who is in hospital in Hiroshima.
