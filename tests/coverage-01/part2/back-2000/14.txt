Cainiao stations now require installing the Taobao app to pick up packages

Cainiao stations have changed, and people now scan a code with Taobao to pick up a package, which is many times faster than the old method of codes sent by message, but people must get the Taobao app to pick up packages, even if the package comes from Pinduoduo, from Tmall, or from other sales platforms.
A Wikinews journalist tried it, and felt that the strong push of the Taobao app is not pleasant, and some consumers think that Taobao is big money that keeps taking the whole market for itself.
