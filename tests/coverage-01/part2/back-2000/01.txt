Doctor: Are you well, mother?
Mother: Well, doctor.
Doctor: What problem do you have?
Mother: My child is sick.
Doctor: How are they sick?
Mother: They have a pain in the belly, and they keep vomiting.
Doctor: Do they feel hot or cold?
Mother: They feel very hot, and they are also shaking.
Doctor: When did it start?
Mother: It started two days ago.
Doctor: Is their stool watery?
Mother: Yes, their stool is watery too.
Doctor: How old are they?
Mother: Three years and six months.
Doctor: Good, I will give them medicine to drink and an injection.
Doctor: As for this medicine, they drink one teaspoon, three times each day.
Doctor: They drink it for one week, and I will see them again in a week.
Mother: How much money do I pay?
Doctor: You pay after the child feels well.
Mother: Thank you.
Doctor: Go well.
