How do you prevent dengue?

If you want to break the transmission cycle of dengue and the cycles of the other diseases that the Aedes aegypti mosquito carries, the most effective way is to remove mosquito breeding places, or to make them unusable.
That means all containers or all things that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive low temperatures and dry conditions, it is important to prevent and check mosquito breeding places without stopping, throughout the whole year.

Ideas for prevention

- Get rid of all old containers that can collect water (metal cans, bottles, car tyres, and other things), at home or at work, inside and outside.
- Make holes in containers that can't be used, before throwing them away.
If you can't, break or crush them.
Put them in a closed bag, so that the garbage collectors take them away safely.
