An easy method for scrambled eggs

Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 2 to 3 tablespoons of milk (if you like)
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- ¼ teaspoon of salt (or to taste)
- ⅛ teaspoon of black seeds (or to taste)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you like) and the salt.
3. With a mixing tool or an eating utensil, mix it quickly, until it has a lot of air in it.
4. Melt the butter in a flat pan over medium heat
5. Pour the eggs into the flat pan.
6. Stir it, to break up the eggs while they are still cooking, and to let more of the liquid touch the hot flat pan.
Stirring quickly makes small pieces, but stirring slowly makes bigger pieces.
7. Cook until the eggs are almost done, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them out of the flat pan.
The eggs are done when their shine disappears.
Just before that happens, stop cooking.
