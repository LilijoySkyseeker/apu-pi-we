Lost bank card

Have you lost your bank card?
You must block your card quickly.
If someone has paid with your card, you can file a complaint with the police (police or gendarmerie).
If you are not at fault, your bank will give you the money that someone spent through fraud.
If you have trouble getting your money back, you can take the problem to the person who settles disputes with banks, or to court.
We show you the information you need to know.

1. Block the card

You must block the card quickly, which means blocking the use of your card.

To do that, contact your bank, or call the banks' server that is only for blocking cards.

You will be given a registration number.

That way, you have a record of your request, with a date.
