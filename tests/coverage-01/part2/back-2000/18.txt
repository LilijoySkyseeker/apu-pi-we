Preventing heat illness

To prevent heat illness, it is important to act in a way that suits the place you are in.
When a "heat illness danger" alert is announced, let's avoid going outside, and avoid the heat.

Avoid the heat, protect yourself

Indoors
・Adjust the temperature with an air conditioner or other things
・Use bamboo curtains, and curtains that block light
・Check the room temperature often
・Also look at the WBGT number

Outdoors
・Use a sunshade, or wear a hat
・Use the shade, and rest often
・On sunny days, avoid going outside during the day

So that the body doesn't build up heat
・Wear clothes that are open to the air, absorb sweat, and dry quickly
・Cool the body with cooling devices, ice, cold cloths, and other things

Drink water often

Indoors or outdoors, even if we don't feel thirsty, let's drink water often
