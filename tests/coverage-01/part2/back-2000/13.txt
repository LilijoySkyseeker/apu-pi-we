Road emergency in Buenos Aires because of rain and flooding

The city of Buenos Aires is in a road emergency because of heavy rain and flooding on the main roads of Argentina's capital.
Almost all the roads entering the city are covered with water, and the metro is running late, and some services, such as train lines and bus lines, are cancelled.
Some neighbourhoods of the city are without electricity, to avoid accidents.
The situation has also spread to cities near Buenos Aires.
