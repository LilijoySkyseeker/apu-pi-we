Losing a bank card

Have you lost your bank card?
You must block your card now.
If someone has paid with your card, you can make a complaint to the police (police or gendarmerie).
If the problem is not your fault, your bank gives you back the money that someone stole with the card.
If you have difficulty getting that money back, you can take the problem to the banks' mediator, or to a court.
We show you what you need to know.

1. Block the card

You must block it very quickly, so that no one can use your card.

To do this, talk to your bank, or call the phone line that the banks have only for blocking cards.

You will be given a record number.

That way, you have a written record that shows the day on which you asked to block the card.
