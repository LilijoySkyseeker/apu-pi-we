Step by step

First of all, prepare the place where you are going to look after the computer, following these ideas:
It is best to use a wide, flat surface with nothing on top of it.
If you think it is better, stand while you work, so you can move more freely.
Choose a place with plenty of light.

To one side, put the things you need:
A Phillips screwdriver.
A thin brush (it can be a writing brush).
Soft heat-conducting material (you can buy it in computer shops).
A cloth to clean with, or paper to dry with.
A white school eraser.
A blower.
A flat screwdriver (if you want).
A light (if you want).
Water that blows out, to clean the connectors (if you want).
Black powder or oil for connecting cloth (if you want). [?]

Step 1, open the computer
1. Remove all the power cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, internet, phone, printer and so on).
2. Open the computer case (which some people wrongly call the "CPU") by removing the fasteners on the back (some have only other kinds of fasteners), and take off the hard cover.
