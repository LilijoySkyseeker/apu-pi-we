How do you protect yourself from dengue?

The best way to stop dengue and the other diseases that the Aedes aegypti mosquito usually carries is to get rid of the places where mosquitoes breed, or make those places unusable for them.
These are all containers, or anything that can hold water.

Because mosquito eggs can stay alive through cold and dry spells, it is important to check the places where mosquitoes breed and get rid of them, without stopping, all year round.

Good ways to protect yourself

Get rid of all containers that can hold water but are not used (small containers, glass containers, car tyres and so on), at home or at work, indoors and outdoors.
Pierce containers that can't be used before you throw them away.
If you can't, break them or crush them.
Put them in a closed bag, so that the garbage collectors can take them away safely.
