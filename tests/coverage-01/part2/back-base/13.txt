Serious problems on the roads of Buenos Aires because of rain and flooding

In the city of Buenos Aires, the roads are not safe, because of heavy rain and flooding on the main roads of Argentina's capital.
Almost all of the roads that come into the city are under water, and the underground trains are late or not running, as are the trains and buses.
Some parts of the city are without electricity, to protect people from accidents.
The problem has also spread to the cities near Buenos Aires (the Conurbano Bonaerense).
