Fuel shortage in Kenya is a problem for boda bodas

In Kenya, fuel is scarce and its price keeps rising, and this causes work problems for the young people who carry passengers on motorbikes, which people commonly call "boda boda".

When Voice of America asked them by phone, some of them said that because fuel is scarce they have to pay a lot of money for it, while customers can't pay the high fares they ask, and this causes problems for the boda boda business, which gives work to many young people in Kenya.

James Mwangi, who leads the boda bodas in Nairobi, said: "When you tell a customer the fare, you both start talking angrily, and then he says he can't pay that amount."

"Because fares have gone up, customers walk, so we are losing customers," added Paul Kuja, who also carries passengers in Nairobi.
