Ways to protect yourself from heat illness

To protect yourself from heat illness, it is important to use the right method for each place.
When a "heat illness warning" is announced, let's stay indoors as far as we can, and get away from the heat.

Get away from the heat, protect yourself

Indoors
Adjust the room temperature with an air conditioner or something else
Use curtains that block light, and shades
Check the room temperature often
Also look at the WBGT number

Outdoors
Use something for shade, or wear a hat
Use the shade, and rest often
On days when the weather is fine, go outside in the daytime as rarely as possible

To avoid holding heat in the body
Wear clothes that let air in, soak up sweat and dry quickly
Cool the body with cooling items, ice or a cold cloth, and so on

Drink water often

Whether indoors or outdoors, even if we don't feel we need water, let's drink water often
