A JR West group worker is dismissed for using ICOCA to travel many times without paying

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, on January 5 JR West said that a man (age 61) who worked at Mikunigaoka station (Sakai city) on the Hanwa line had been dismissed as of January 4 (UTC+9), because he had used ICOCA (a travel card system) improperly and travelled many times on JR train lines without paying, for example from Hiroshima to Ashiya (Ashiya city, Hyogo), and did not pay ¥22,840 in fares.

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun, this person worked at JR West Transportation Service, which is a JR West group company.
It seems he didn't pay, for example when he travelled to see his family at a hospital in Hiroshima.
