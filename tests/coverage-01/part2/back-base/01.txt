Doctor: Hello, ma'am?
Mother: I'm well, doctor.
Doctor: What's the problem?
Mother: My child is sick.
Doctor: How is she sick?
Mother: She has pain in her belly, and she keeps vomiting.
Doctor: Does she feel hot or cold?
Mother: She feels very hot, and she is also shaking.
Doctor: When did it start?
Mother: It started two days ago.
Doctor: Is she passing watery stools?
Mother: Yes, watery stools too.
Doctor: How old is she?
Mother: Three years and six months.
Doctor: Good.
I will give her an injection of medicine, and medicine to drink.
Doctor: As for this medicine, she takes one small spoonful, three times each day.
She takes it for one week, and in a week I will see her again.
Mother: How much do I pay?
Doctor: You pay after the child is feeling well.
Mother: Thank you.
Doctor: Goodbye.
