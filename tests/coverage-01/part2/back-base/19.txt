Easy scrambled eggs

What you need
3 eggs
Milk, 2 to 3 tablespoons (if you like)
Butter, 2 tablespoons
Salt, ¼ teaspoon (or to taste)
Black pepper, ⅛ teaspoon (or to taste)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you like) and the salt.
3. Beat the mixture in the cup with a whisk or a fork until it has a lot of air in it.
4. Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat.
5. Pour the eggs into the pan.
6. Stir them to break up the eggs as they cook, and to let more of the wet part touch the hot pan.
If you stir them quickly, the eggs turn into small pieces, but if you stir them more slowly, the eggs turn into bigger pieces.
7. Cook them until a moment before you feel they are ready, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them out of the pan.
The eggs are ready when their shine goes away.
Stop cooking a moment before that happens.
