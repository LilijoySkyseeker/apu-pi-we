ge yaga pi funti la

A: so pen eda waka fu tan, yen mu kubo bu moti.
pen sin kegu tonya mon bige.
fon pen yaga funti la mon kemin pi mido fu wifu pi waka man wan la sin dunu mon waka minwe pi fugi, yen pi funti la man mu sin gun mon waka pi fugi un gun mon kemin pi mido, pen yaga bu la mon fugi minwe, fu muyi man wan la sin tuko, se pani tun duwa, se funbe tun nata.
so kemin we pi mido gun yaga fu lite kige, wi wifu pi lifin la.

B: to tayu.

C: fu kemin we pi mido man gun mon bige, gi ge yaga la lenu kubo mon gimu we, gun ke denku wun pen le yaga funti la fu lite kige, fu ge muwo pi waka pi fugi.

D: to tayu, yen to fe pume fu ben, gun ke gole wun pen yaga fu wifu pi waka mon waka minwe pi fugi

E: mon gigin man we kubo bi yobe mon, fu en türkiye, gun gagan wun pen yaga fu waka minwe pi fugi man sin muwo ya fawu 2.
gi tan, gun ke denku wun pen yaga konsin fu waka duwa pi oton la.
