Fuel shortage in Kenya is a challenge for boda bodas

In Kenya, the fuel shortage and fuel prices that keep rising are creating work challenges for young people who carry people on motorbikes, which people call "boda boda".

In a phone interview with Voice of America, some of them said that because of the fuel shortage, they have to buy fuel at high prices, while customers can't pay the high prices they ask, and this creates a challenge for the boda boda business, which gives work to many young people in Kenya.

James Mwangi, who drives a boda boda in the city of Nairobi, says: "When you tell a customer the price of the trip, you start fighting with words, and afterwards, they say they can't pay that price."

Paul Kuja, who also carries people in the city of Nairobi, also says: "Because the price of trips has gone up, customers walk, so we lose customers."
