How do you protect yourself from mosquito diseases?

The method that works best to stop mosquito diseases, and other diseases that the Aedes aegypti mosquito carries, is to remove or dry out the places where mosquitoes breed.
This means all containers or anything that can collect water.

Because mosquito eggs can survive low temperatures and dry times, it is important to know that you must guard against mosquito breeding places and check them without stopping, all year round.

Ways to protect yourself

- Remove all containers that are not in use and can collect water (small iron containers, bottles, car tyres, and other things), inside and outside the house or workplace.
- Puncture containers that can't be used before throwing them away.
If that isn't possible, break or crush them.
Put them in a closed bag, so that garbage collectors can take them away safely.
