Easy scrambled eggs

What you need
- 3 eggs
- 2–3 small cups of milk (if you want)
- 2 small cups of butter
- ¼ very small cup of salt (or as you like)
- ⅛ very small cup of black seeds (or as you like)

Method
1. Break the eggs into a measuring cup.
2. Add the milk (if you want) and the salt.
3. With a stirring tool or a fork, beat it quickly until it is full of air.
4. Melt the butter in a flat pan, over medium heat.
5. Put the eggs in the flat pan.
6. Stir it to break up the eggs while it cooks, and to let more of the liquid touch the pan.
If you stir quickly, you make small pieces, but if you stir more slowly, you make bigger pieces.
7. Cook until a moment before you feel they are ready, because the eggs keep cooking after you take them out of the pan.
The eggs are ready when they no longer shine.
Stop cooking a moment before this happens.
