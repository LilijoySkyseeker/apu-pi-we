Loss of a bank card

Have you lost your bank card?
You must block your card now.
If someone has paid with your card, you can make a complaint to the police (the police or the military police).
If that isn't your fault, your bank gives back the money that someone stole.
If the money doesn't come back easily, you can go to the banks' mediator or to court.
We show you the information you need to know.

1. Block the card

You must block the card very quickly; this means no one can use your card.

To do this, contact your bank, or call the phone number that all banks use for blocking cards.

You will be given a record number.

So you have a record of your request, with the date.
