Step by step

First of all, prepare the place where you're going to fix the computer, following these ideas:
- it's best to use a wide table with nothing on it;
- if you think it's better, work standing up, so you can move more freely;
- choose a place with a lot of light.

Gather the things you need:
- a Phillips-type screwdriver.
- a brush with fine hairs (it can be an art brush).
- thermal grease (which you can buy at a computer shop).
- a cleaning cloth or cleaning paper.
- a white school eraser.
- a blower.
- a flat screwdriver (if you want).
- a light (if you want).
- cleaning fluid for connections (if you want).
- black powder or sewing machine oil (if you want).

Step 1 - Open the computer
1. Remove all the cables from the computer (mouse, keyboard, screen, power, network, phone, printer, and other things).
2. Open the computer's box (some people call it the "CPU", wrongly), by taking the small iron tools off its back (some only have a holding piece), and take off the iron cover.
