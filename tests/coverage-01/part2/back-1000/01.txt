Doctor: Are you well, mother?
Mother: I am well, doctor.
Doctor: What problem do you have?
Mother: My child is sick.
Doctor: Sick in what way?
Mother: They have a pain in the belly, and they keep vomiting.
Doctor: Do they feel hot or cold?
Mother: They feel very hot, and they are also shivering.
Doctor: When did it start?
Mother: It started two days ago.
Doctor: Do they have watery stools?
Mother: Yes, they have watery stools too.
Doctor: How old are they?
Mother: They are three years and six months old.
Doctor: Good, I will give them a medicine to drink and a medicine that I inject.
Doctor: As for this medicine to drink, they drink one small cup three times each day.
They drink it for one week, and I will see them again in a week.
Mother: How much money do I pay?
Doctor: You pay after the child feels well.
Mother: Thank you.
Doctor: Goodbye.
