Now, to collect packages at Cainiao Yizhan, you have to install Taobao

Cainiao Yizhan now uses Taobao to see the code and hand over packages, and this is much faster than the old way (a code by message), but you have to download Taobao to collect packages, even if the package comes from Pinduoduo or Tmall or some other internet shop.
After a Wikinews writer tried it himself, he felt unhappy because you have to use Taobao, and some of the buyers think that Taobao is big money that is taking the whole market.
