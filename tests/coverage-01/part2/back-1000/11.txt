JR West company employee loses his job because he travelled without paying many times using ICOCA

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun and the Asahi Shimbun, on January 5, JR West announced that it had dismissed, as a punishment, a male employee of Mikunigaoka Station (Sakai city) on the Hanwa Line (aged 61), on January 4 (UTC+9), because he had used ICOCA (イコカ, a ticket card system) wrongly to travel many times on JR lines without paying, including from Hiroshima to Ashiya (Ashiya city, Hyogo Prefecture), and had not paid ¥22,840 for the travel.

According to the Yomiuri Shimbun, this employee works at JR West Transportation Service, which is a company that JR West owns.
It seems he didn't pay around the times when he was visiting family who were in a hospital in Hiroshima.
