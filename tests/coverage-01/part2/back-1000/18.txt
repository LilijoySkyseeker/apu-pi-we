Ways to protect yourself from heat illness

To protect yourself from heat illness, it's important to do the right things for wherever you are.
When a 『heat illness notice』 is announced, let's not go outside unless we need to, and protect ourselves from the heat.

Stay far from the heat, protect yourself

Indoors
・Set the temperature with an air conditioner or something like it
・Use cloth that doesn't let light through, and wooden shades
・Check the room temperature often
・Also look at the WBGT number

Outdoors
・Use something that protects you from the sun, or wear something on your head
・Use shade, and rest often
・On days when the weather is good, don't go outside during the day unless you need to

So that heat doesn't build up in the body
・Wear clothes that let air through, soak up sweat and dry quickly
・Cool the body with ice bags, ice, cold cloths and things like that

Drink water often

Indoors or outdoors, even if we don't want water, let's drink water often
