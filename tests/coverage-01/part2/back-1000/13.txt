Road crisis in Buenos Aires because of rain and floods

The city of Buenos Aires is facing danger on its roads, because of heavy rain and flooding on the main roads of the capital of Argentina.
Almost all the roads that enter the city are covered with water, and the metro runs late or not at all, as do the train lines and the bus lines.
Some districts of the city have no electricity, to protect people from accidents.
The same situation also exists in the cities that are near Buenos Aires.
