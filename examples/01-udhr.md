# Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The United Nations adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948. It is the most translated document in the world, which makes it a good first long text for any language. This translation was made from the English and checked against the French, which is equally official.

Each paragraph is followed by the original English. Point at or tap an Apupiwe word to see what it means.

Some ideas in the declaration don't have a single word in Apupiwe yet, so they are described. Religion is **lite pi nosen** "the way of God", a court is **pu pi wabun** "a place of law", and the United Nations is **funti pi gigin la** "the group of countries".

The English text is from the United Nations, as published by the UDHR in Unicode project.

## kemin pi sogan pi ewo we {.apu}

## tuko pi kemin {.apu}

ewo mesun pi yonyo pi ewo we ibe bawe pi ewo bo wan gun ewo. ewo we pon ibe sogan kige. dan su lenu yidu bun la se wan. mon fowo minwe, yinki pi enpa gun wun ewo we tayu win. ben pon gun yinki pi kidun, yen gun yinki pi bonwi.
{.apu}

> Whereas recognition of the inherent dignity and of the equal and inalienable rights of all members of the human family is the foundation of freedom, justice and peace in the world,

ewo la su dundi sogan pi ewo. wan la pon bebu bun la. bo win, wan la sin muge musu ten yomo. bo ewo we lugo musu upe yen musu yomo, musu la win muge bonko wan la. ewo la ganin wun fowo minwe lewe upe. mon fowo win, dan oyi enpa, yen dan ibe gen pume feko fun enpa. mon bun, dan pon su nuta yen ibe tonya. ganin win gun ke tisan se we, yen ewo pa sin iko ben tun ewo we.
{.apu}

> Whereas disregard and contempt for human rights have resulted in barbarous acts which have outraged the conscience of mankind, and the advent of a world in which human beings shall enjoy freedom of speech and belief and freedom from fear and want has been proclaimed as the highest aspiration of the common people,

lula pi wabun keya kuwi sogan pi ewo fan ewo la su keya migi lula yomo pi no wungu. migi win gun lite fugi.
{.apu}

> Whereas it is essential, if man is not to be compelled to have recourse, as a last resort, to rebellion against tyranny and oppression, that human rights should be protected by the rule of law,

ewo we keya yema fan gigin la lewe sonu pi nen nen.
{.apu}

> Whereas it is essential to promote the development of friendly relations between nations,

mon kemin pi funti pi gigin la, ewo la pi funti win sin oyi moti wun son gewan sogan bawe pi ewo, yen son gewan bawe pi ewo, yen son gewan wun lowu yen futa ibe sogan kige. wan la pon sin waka yema fan ewo we guge. wan la pon sin waka yema fan ewo we nume ke upe. wan la pon sin waka yema fan ewo we ibe enpa ke feli.
{.apu}

> Whereas the peoples of the United Nations have in the Charter reaffirmed their faith in fundamental human rights, in the dignity and worth of the human person and in the equal rights of men and women and have determined to promote social progress and better standards of life in larger freedom,

gigin la pi funti pi gigin la sin oyi "me din doku nan funti pi gigin la fan ewo we dundi sogan pi ewo yen enpa bawe. me din pon doku nan bun fan ewo we kuwi bun la."
{.apu}

> Whereas Member States have pledged themselves to achieve, in co‐operation with the United Nations, the promotion of universal respect for and observance of human rights and fundamental freedoms,

lafi kige pi sogan la win gun ke bawe se we fan gigin la lenu muge minwe gen oyi ben.
{.apu}

> Whereas a common understanding of these rights and freedoms is of the greatest importance for the full realization of this pledge,

bo win, fanmu pi funti pi gigin la iko kemin pi sogan pi ewo we tun ewo we. ewo la we yen gigin we ibe kemin win fun gen momu kige. ta ewo mesun yen funti mesun pi ewo we imu kemin win lin muyi we. ta wan la walo nan tofo fan dan ke dundi sogan la win. nan doku gi guge mon gigin pi nen, ta wan la pon walo fan ewo we tayu sogan la win. ta gigin la pon doku nan nen nen fan win. ta ewo we kuwi sogan la win fun feko mon gigin la pi funti pi gigin la. ta ewo we pon kuwi bun la mon kemu gi gigin la win wungu.
{.apu}

> Now, therefore, The General Assembly Proclaims this Universal Declaration of Human Rights as a common standard of achievement for all peoples and all nations, to the end that every individual and every organ of society, keeping this Declaration constantly in mind, shall strive by teaching and education to promote respect for these rights and freedoms and by progressive measures, national and international, to secure their universal and effective recognition and observance, both among the peoples of Member States themselves and among the peoples of territories under their jurisdiction.

## wabun mena 1 {.apu}

ewo we tuko nume nan enpa, yen wan la ibe bawe kige yen sogan kige. wan la lenu pume, yen lenu lugo musu upe yen musu yomo. wan la keya muge nen nen fun faba la.
{.apu}

> All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. They are endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.

## wabun mena 2 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan we pi kemin win. musu fen kubo win. niki pi ewo lu niki pi unne lu lowu lu futa lu apu lu lite pi nosen lu gigin lu funti gi wan obu se, su kubo win. musu pi wan lu tuko nume pi wan lu musu eni lu gen pume pi wungu lu gen pume eni, pon su kubo win.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled to all the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration, without distinction of any kind, such as race, colour, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, property, birth or other status.

pi gigin pi wan lu kemu pi wan, don bun gun enpa, lu gigin eni wungu bun, lu bun su wungu nen, lu bun ibe lula ke uyo fun lite eni, lula pi bun pon su kubo win.
{.apu}

> Furthermore, no distinction shall be made on the basis of the political, jurisdictional or international status of the country or territory to which a person belongs, whether it be independent, trust, non‐self‐governing or under any other limitation of sovereignty.

## wabun mena 3 {.apu}

nume yen enpa yen sonli gun sogan pi dan.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to life, liberty and the security of person.

## wabun mena 4 {.apu}

dan su gun efon yonnu ewo fan doku su enpa. dan pon su gun efon ibe ewo, lu bika ewo, lu koda ewo. win gun feko fan niki mesun.
{.apu}

> No one shall be held in slavery or servitude; slavery and the slave trade shall be prohibited in all their forms.

## wabun mena 5 {.apu}

dan su gun efon muge ten wekun dan nan momu. dan pon su gun efon muge dan ten yomo, lu muge dan su fun ewo, lu muge dan fun musu. win gun feko yon fan wekun pi wabun.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment.

## wabun mena 6 {.apu}

mon kemu we, dan ibe sogan fan gun ewo fan wabun.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to recognition everywhere as a person before the law.

## wabun mena 7 {.apu}

ewo we gun kige fan wabun, yen wan la ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan la fun kige. ewo we pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan la fun kige se ewo gi su muge wan la fun kige fun kemin win su efon. wan la pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan la se ewo gi walo fan ewo eni muge ben.
{.apu}

> All are equal before the law and are entitled without any discrimination to equal protection of the law. All are entitled to equal protection against any discrimination in violation of this Declaration and against any incitement to such discrimination.

## wabun mena 8 {.apu}

don ewo pa alun sogan bawe gi wabun eni lu wabun bawe pi gigin neke yi, yi ibe sogan fan pu pi wabun pi gigin min muge upe ben.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to an effective remedy by the competent national tribunals for acts violating the fundamental rights granted him by the constitution or by law.

## wabun mena 9 {.apu}

don wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu dan, lu yonnu dan, lu muge kusa dan se gigin pi wan.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest, detention or exile.

## wabun mena 10 {.apu}

don pu pi wabun la waka fun sogan pi wan yen gen keya pi wan, lu waka wun bi wan sin alun wabun, wan ibe sogan fan pu pi wabun la doma wan fun kidun. pu pi wabun la keya doma wan lin ewo we lenu nagu. pu pi wabun la win keya gun kige fan we, yen keya gun enpa. win gun kige fan ewo we.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled in full equality to a fair and public hearing by an independent and impartial tribunal, in the determination of his rights and obligations and of any criminal charge against him.

## wabun mena 11 {.apu}

1\. don ewo pa oyi wun yi sin alun wabun, wabun la keya muge yi fun ewo gi su alun bun. win gun feko anko pu pi wabun la wuye wun yi sin alun bun. pu pi wabun la keya wuye ben fun wabun mon doma gi ewo we lenu nagu. mon doma win, bun la keya neke yi musu we gi yi wupi fan kuwi nen.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone charged with a penal offence has the right to be presumed innocent until proved guilty according to law in a public trial at which he has had all the guarantees necessary for his defence.

2\. don dan sin muge musu pa lu su muge musu pa, yen wabun pi gigin efon ben mon muyi ben, yen wabun lifin pi gigin la pon efon ben mon muyi ben, pu pi wabun la keya su oyi wun wan sin alun wabun. pu pi wabun la pon keya su neke wan wekun ke gogo se wekun gi wabun efon mon muyi ben.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be held guilty of any penal offence on account of any act or omission which did not constitute a penal offence, under national or international law, at the time when it was committed. Nor shall a heavier penalty be imposed than the one that was applicable at the time the penal offence was committed.

## wabun mena 12 {.apu}

pi ewo mesun, don wabun denku su efon, ewo eni su gun efon inwa nume kunsu pi wan, lu inwa yonyo pi wan, lu inwa guyi pi wan, lu inwa gen sege pi wan. ewo eni pon su gun efon alun dundi pi wan, lu alun suso upe pi wan. dan ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan se ben.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to arbitrary interference with his privacy, family, home or correspondence, nor to attacks upon his honour and reputation. Everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference or attacks.

## wabun mena 13 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan kobi enpa mon bese pi gigin gi wan piyi mon. wan pon ibe sogan fan nume enpa mon bese pi bun.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to freedom of movement and residence within the borders of each State.

2\. dan ibe sogan fan kusa gigin mesun yen yon gigin pi nen. wan pon ibe sogan fan sigo tun gigin pi nen.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right to leave any country, including his own, and to return to his country.

## wabun mena 14 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan ditun pu sonli mon gigin eni, don ewo la fesun wan fan muge wekun wan. wan pon ibe sogan fan nume mon pu sonli win.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to seek and to enjoy in other countries asylum from persecution.

2\. don ewo pa oyi wun yi sin alun wabun, yen ukun feko pi ben gun wun yi sin alun wabun, yen alun win su gun fun wungu, yi su lenu bone sogan win. don ewo pa oyi wun yi sin muge musu pa, yen ukun feko pi ben gun wun musu win ninsu gen momu pi funti pi gigin la, lu ninsu gason bawe pi bun, yi pon su lenu bone sogan win.
{.apu}

> 2\. This right may not be invoked in the case of prosecutions genuinely arising from non‐political crimes or from acts contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.

## wabun mena 15 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan gun ewo pi gigin pa.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to a nationality.

2\. don wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu sogan gi wan ibe fan gun ewo pi gigin pi nen, lu yidu sogan gi wan ibe fan lewe ewo pi gigin eni.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his nationality nor denied the right to change his nationality.

## wabun mena 16 {.apu}

1\. don lowu lu futa sin guge, wan ibe sogan fan bedan. wan pon ibe sogan fan tuko yonyo. wan la ibe sogan la win nuwen bamo gi gun fun niki pi ewo lu gigin lu lite pi nosen. lowu yen futa ibe sogan kige fan bedan. wan la pon ibe sogan kige lin bedan. wan la pon ibe sogan kige mon fugi pi bedan.
{.apu}

> 1\. Men and women of full age, without any limitation due to race, nationality or religion, have the right to marry and to found a family. They are entitled to equal rights as to marriage, during marriage and at its dissolution.

2\. ewo sa lenu bedan ga don wan la nen tayu enpa yen minwe.
{.apu}

> 2\. Marriage shall be entered into only with the free and full consent of the intending spouses.

3\. yonyo gun funti meli gi muwo bo dan gun ewo. bun gun yinki pi ewo we, yen bun ibe sogan fan ewo we yen gigin kuwi bun.
{.apu}

> 3\. The family is the natural and fundamental group unit of society and is entitled to protection by society and the State.

## wabun mena 17 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan ibe musu nuwen ewo eni. wan pon ibe sogan fan ibe musu nan ewo eni.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to own property alone as well as in association with others.

2\. don wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu musu pi wan se wan.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his property.

## wabun mena 18 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan pume enpa. pi upe yen yomo, wan pon ibe sogan fan pume enpa. wan pon ibe sogan fan ibe lite pi nosen lu gen pume feko fun enpa. sogan win pon gun sogan fan kubo lite pi nosen lu gen pume feko. wan pon ibe sogan fan wuye lite pi nosen lu gen pume feko fun enpa. wan lenu muge ben nuwen ewo eni. wan pon lenu muge ben nan ewo eni. wan lenu muge ben mon kemu kunsu, lu muge ben mon kemu su kunsu. tofo yen muge yen dundi nosen yen kuwi gun lite gi dan wuye bun la nan.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion; this right includes freedom to change his religion or belief, and freedom, either alone or in community with others and in public or private, to manifest his religion or belief in teaching, practice, worship and observance.

## wabun mena 19 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan ibe gen pume fun enpa, yen dan ibe sogan fan oyi enpa. sogan win pon gun sogan fan ewo eni su muge wan fun yomo bo wan pume musu pa. pi gason yen gen iko, sogan win pon gun wun wan lenu ditun bun la, yen lenu dunu bun la, yen lenu peton bun la. wan lenu muge win nan du iko we. lifin pi gigin la su bamo ben.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to freedom of opinion and expression; this right includes freedom to hold opinions without interference and to seek, receive and impart information and ideas through any media and regardless of frontiers.

## wabun mena 20 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan fanmu enpa nan bonwi. wan pon ibe sogan fan selo funti nan bonwi. wan pon ibe sogan fan inwa funti fun enpa.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to freedom of peaceful assembly and association.

2\. dan su keya inwa funti. ewo eni su gun efon oyi wun wan keya inwa funti pa.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one may be compelled to belong to an association.

## wabun mena 21 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan wungu gigin pi nen nan ewo eni. wan nen lenu muge win, lu no wungu gi dan waka enpa, lenu muge win fan wan.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to take part in the government of his country, directly or through freely chosen representatives.

2\. dan ibe sogan kige fan inwa doku gi yema ewo we pi gigin pi wan.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right of equal access to public service in his country.

3\. lula pi no wungu la keya obu se gen momu pi ewo la pi gigin. ewo la keya lenu wuye gen momu win nan waka feko pi no wungu la. waka win keya muwo moti moti. lin waka win, ewo we ibe sogan kige fan waka. dan keya lenu waka kunsu, lu keya lenu waka fun lite eni gi gun enpa fun ben.
{.apu}

> 3\. The will of the people shall be the basis of the authority of government; this will shall be expressed in periodic and genuine elections which shall be by universal and equal suffrage and shall be held by secret vote or by equivalent free voting procedures.

## wabun mena 22 {.apu}

dan gun ni pi ewo we, yen dan ibe sogan fan dunu yema se ewo we. wan pon ibe sogan gi gun fun nimo. wan pon ibe sogan gi gun fun ewo we. wan pon ibe sogan gi gun fun lite pi nume pi funti. wan wupi bun la fan kuwi bawe pi nen. wan pon wupi bun la fan guge enpa. gigin pi wan doku fan win, yen gigin la doku nan nen nen fan win. gigin mesun doku fan win fun yile pi nen yen musu pi nen yen nimo pi nen.
{.apu}

> Everyone, as a member of society, has the right to social security and is entitled to realization, through national effort and international co‐operation and in accordance with the organization and resources of each State, of the economic, social and cultural rights indispensable for his dignity and the free development of his personality.

## wabun mena 23 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan doku. wan pon ibe sogan fan waka doku pi nen fun enpa. wan pon ibe sogan fan doku gi gun kidun yen upe. wan pon ibe sogan fan gigin kuwi wan se su ibe doku.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to work, to free choice of employment, to just and favourable conditions of work and to protection against unemployment.

2\. don ewo la doku kige, wan la ibe sogan fan nimo kige. win gun kige fan we.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone, without any discrimination, has the right to equal pay for equal work.

3\. ewo gi doku, ibe sogan fan nimo gi gun kidun yen upe. wan yen yonyo pi wan keya lenu nume nan bawe pi ewo nan nimo win. don wan wupi bun, ewo we yen gigin pon keya yema wan fun lite eni.
{.apu}

> 3\. Everyone who works has the right to just and favourable remuneration ensuring for himself and his family an existence worthy of human dignity, and supplemented, if necessary, by other means of social protection.

4\. dan ibe sogan fan selo funti pi no doku la nan ewo eni. wan pon ibe sogan fan inwa funti pi no doku la. wan lenu muge win fan kuwi upe pi nen.
{.apu}

> 4\. Everyone has the right to form and to join trade unions for the protection of his interests.

## wabun mena 24 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan yenbo. wan pon ibe sogan fan ibe muyi enpa. win pon gun wun wan su keya doku awuwa ten feli. win pon gun wun wan ibe dili enpa nan nimo moti moti.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to rest and leisure, including reasonable limitation of working hours and periodic holidays with pay.

## wabun mena 25 {.apu}

1\. dan yen yonyo pi wan ibe sogan fan ibe tonya fan nume upe. win pon gun wun wan la ibe tonya fan gawo upe. win pon gun wun wan la ibe pasu yen lonne yen guyi. win pon gun wun ewo we yen gigin yema wan la lin wan la simi. win pon gun wun ewo we yen gigin yema wan la lin wan la wupi bun. don wan su ibe doku, lu gun simi lu su lenu doku, lu gun pani lu ofin pi wan sin kono lu wan su ibe tonya fun lite eni gi wan su lenu kubo, wan pon ibe sogan fan gun yema se ewo we yen gigin.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to a standard of living adequate for the health and well‐being of himself and of his family, including food, clothing, housing and medical care and necessary social services, and the right to security in the event of unemployment, sickness, disability, widowhood, old age or other lack of livelihood in circumstances beyond his control.

2\. mole yen tiwu ibe sogan fan gun kuwi fun ke feli, yen gun yema fun ke feli. pi tiwu we, yon don mole pi wan la yen wede pi wan la su gun ofin, wan la ibe sogan fan ewo we kuwi wan la fun kige.
{.apu}

> 2\. Motherhood and childhood are entitled to special care and assistance. All children, whether born in or out of wedlock, shall enjoy the same social protection.

## wabun mena 26 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan yesa. dan su keya unfu fan konsin tofo pi tuko. tiwu we keya dunu tofo pi tuko. ewo we keya lenu dunu tofo pi doku. ewo we pon keya lenu inwa tofo tisan fun lenu pi nen fun kige.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to education. Education shall be free, at least in the elementary and fundamental stages. Elementary education shall be compulsory. Technical and professional education shall be made generally available and higher education shall be equally accessible to all on the basis of merit.

2\. tofo keya yema fan ewo guge minwe. bun pon keya yema fan dan ke dundi sogan pi ewo yen enpa bawe. bun keya yema fan gigin we lafi nen nen, yen gun sonu, yen efon wun eni pume eni. win pon gun feko fan funti we pi niki pi ewo. win pon gun feko fan funti we pi lite pi nosen lu funti we pi gen pume feko. bun pon keya yema doku pi funti pi gigin la fan kuwi bonwi.
{.apu}

> 2\. Education shall be directed to the full development of the human personality and to the strengthening of respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms. It shall promote understanding, tolerance and friendship among all nations, racial or religious groups, and shall further the activities of the United Nations for the maintenance of peace.

3\. mole yen wede ibe sogan fan waka niki pi tofo pi tiwu pi nen. waka pi wan la gun ke bawe se waka pi ewo eni.
{.apu}

> 3\. Parents have a prior right to choose the kind of education that shall be given to their children.

## wabun mena 27 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan inwa lite pi nume pi funti gi wan nume mon kiyo fun enpa. wan pon ibe sogan fan tana gen muge fan sesa. wan pon ibe sogan fan inwa guge pi lugo. wan pon ibe sogan fan dunu upe pi guge win.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right freely to participate in the cultural life of the community, to enjoy the arts and to share in scientific advancement and its benefits.

2\. don dan sin muge musu pi lugo lu gen tusi lu gen muge fan sesa, wan ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi suso pi wan mon bun. wan pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi nimo gi wan dunu se bun.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right to the protection of the moral and material interests resulting from any scientific, literary or artistic production of which he is the author.

## wabun mena 28 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan yile gi sogan we pi kemin win lenu lewe feko minwe mon. yile win gun yile pi ewo we, yen gun yile lifin pi gigin la.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled to a social and international order in which the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration can be fully realized.

## wabun mena 29 {.apu}

1\. dan lenu guge enpa yen minwe ga mon funti. bo win, dan ibe gen keya fan funti gi wan nume mon.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has duties to the community in which alone the free and full development of his personality is possible.

2\. lin dan bone sogan pi nen, ga wabun la gun efon muge ke uyo sogan pi wan. wabun la gun efon muge ben ga fan ukun sa. ukun ni gun wun dan tayu sogan pi ewo eni, yen dundi bun la. mon ewo we gi waka no wungu pi nen, ukun eni gun wun ewo we kuwi lite upe fun kidun, yen kuwi bonwi, yen kuwi upe pi ewo we.
{.apu}

> 2\. In the exercise of his rights and freedoms, everyone shall be subject only to such limitations as are determined by law solely for the purpose of securing due recognition and respect for the rights and freedoms of others and of meeting the just requirements of morality, public order and the general welfare in a democratic society.

3\. don dan bone sogan la win, yen ben ninsu gen momu pi funti pi gigin la, lu ninsu gason bawe pi bun, dan su su gun efon muge ben.
{.apu}

> 3\. These rights and freedoms may in no case be exercised contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.

## wabun mena 30 {.apu}

fen pi kemin win yofu wun gigin pa lu funti pa lu ewo pa ibe sogan fan muge musu pa fan alun ni pi sogan pi kemin win.
{.apu}

> Nothing in this Declaration may be interpreted as implying for any State, group or person any right to engage in any activity or to perform any act aimed at the destruction of any of the rights and freedoms set forth herein.
