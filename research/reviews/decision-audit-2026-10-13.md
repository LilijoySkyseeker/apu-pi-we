## Agreed rules applied inconsistently

- **Frequency decides (764, CLAUDE.md).** Applied to soul, ghost, miracle and devil, then to sacred and bless, but not to the Wordbank layer (M2), not to the tradition list (M4: sin 522 in 17 languages), and with the wrong label for bless (M3). The speech-act review (776) set usage counts aside on purpose for rare, high-stakes acts. That's defensible for grammar words, but CLAUDE.md states the rule without that scope. Say which layer the frequency rule governs.
- **The ladders.** Ladder 2 rung 1 was skipped for currencies (M8). The rung-3 safeguard was cited against itself (M10). Rung 1's reading in 744 ("the calendar month and the Moon are two concepts by their standards") is a strong one. Applied evenly, it would also question **muyi** "time (a span or a point)", since ISO 8601 defines time instant and time interval separately. Decide whether rung 1 splits wherever a standard defines one sense, or only where the standard's sense is in daily use.
- **The culture principle (762).** Applied to institutions, roles and beings by judgement. The add-back it wrote ("shared widely enough to pass the ladders joins the core") wasn't run on the data at hand (M4). Wordbank's childhood items (stroller, crayon, tights, pancake, snowman, firetruck) are culture-bound in the same way and weren't put through it (M2). The planned check of other domains (roadmap.md:67) should include the Wordbank layer.
- **Check sources (758, CLAUDE.md).** The metric "17–19 of 20" has no recorded panel (M6). The religion re-check's ranks rely on uncommitted data (M3). Email's pass rests on unsourced columns (M7).
