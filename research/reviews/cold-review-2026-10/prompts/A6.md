You are a fact-checker. A constructed language was designed with the help of AI research agents, and its design notes rest on many empirical claims: citations, percentages, "most languages do X", study results. Some may be wrong, misremembered or invented. Your job is to find out which.

Inputs, in `/home/agent/work/apupiwe-review/packets/evidence/`: `goal.md` (the language's goal), `rationale.md` (the design notes: each decision with its reason), `vocabulary-method.md`, `drift.md`, `grammar-rebuild-spec.md`, `number-base.md`, and `research/` (the research notes the decisions draw on). Don't open other files on this machine. Use web search and fetch freely to check sources.

Method:
1. List the empirical claims the design decisions rest on, starting with those in `rationale.md` that justify a decision (prioritise claims that a decision would change if they were false). Aim for the 40 most load-bearing; include every named citation (author, year) in rationale.md.
2. Verify each against the source or reliable data (the cited paper, WALS, PHOIBLE, CLICS, Glottolog, standard handbooks). Classify: **verified** (source exists and says this), **partly** (source exists, claim overstated or numbers off; say how), **wrong** (source says otherwise), **unsupported** (no source found; claim may still be true), **likely fabricated** (citation doesn't exist or doesn't contain anything like this).
3. For every claim that isn't verified, name the design decision that rests on it and say how much it matters (would the decision change?).
4. Note internal tests the notes cite as evidence (their own experiments) separately: you can't verify those externally, but flag ones whose method as described couldn't support the conclusion.

Write to `/home/agent/work/apupiwe-review/reports/A6-factcheck.md`: totals per class at the top, then a table (claim, where it appears, source, verdict, what the decision rests on, impact). Final report: the totals and every claim rated wrong or likely fabricated, plus the five most consequential partly/unsupported ones.
