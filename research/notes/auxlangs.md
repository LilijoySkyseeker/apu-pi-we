## 0. Apupiwe in brief (the yardstick for this report)

Facts from the repo as of 2026-10-13, so the comparison below has something fixed to compare against.

| Feature | Apupiwe | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | A thought experiment in the best human language, for children who grow up with it (P3), and a real language its author learns and shares. No adoption campaign. | `docs/goal.md`, `CLAUDE.md` |
| Designer | One author deciding, with an AI (Claude) researching, drafting and recommending; 194 commits in six days (2026-10-08 to 10-13). | `git log` |
| Sounds | 5 vowels, 13 consonants, syllable (C)V(n), first-syllable stress, no tone. | language.md §2 |
| Writing | Latin, 18 letters, one per sound, lowercase, three grammatical punctuation marks. | §3 |
| Grammar type | Isolating; no inflection, agreement, case or gender; SVO; heads first; one-syllable grammar particles; optional tense, aspect particles; no word classes (role by position). | §2.5, §4 |
| Vocabulary source | **A priori**: root forms are arbitrary, generated by an optimiser that minimises predicted mishearing (`tools/words.py`); concepts chosen by cross-family evidence (CLICS4, Wordbank) and modern frequency. Loans only where the world shares a form (21 loans). | rationale §3, §5; L4 |
| Root count | 496 roots + 21 loans + 61 grammar words + 58 listed compounds today; a base of about **1,500 roots** decided 2026-10-13. | `data/*.tsv`; `tests/coverage-01` |
| Word formation | Four derivation markers (**no ge du pu**), phrases with **pi**/**man**, listed compounds; bare noun + noun only for gender and listed compounds. | §5.4 |
| Precision | Principle: a precise way to say anything exists, even when not used (B3); optional numeric confidence (**kon** + sixths), optional evidentials, speech-act particles, **kiyo** phrase closer. | §1.7, §6, §7 |
| Evidence | Blind AI translation and back-translation tests; one human reading test by the author; an unvalidated mishearing model; no human listening test, no learner trial. | rationale §11; roadmap |
| Governance | The author decides; goal B9 says it "doesn't try to control how it evolves"; a reference variety, not prohibitions (review repair 1b). CC0. | design-log |
| Tooling | Spec as data (`spec/reference.toml`), generated reference, a GF formal grammar that parses every reference example with its intended reading, word-form optimiser, juncture and offensive-word checks. | `grammar/`, `tools/` |
