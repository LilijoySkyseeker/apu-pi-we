# Apu Pi We

**apu pi we**, "everyone's language", is a thought experiment in the best human language: built from what the world's languages share, for people who would grow up speaking it. It is also a real language you can learn. It is still being designed, so words and rules may change.

- **[Learn it](learn.md):** fourteen short lessons for English speakers.
- **[Reference](language.md):** every rule, with examples.
- **[Dictionary](dictionary.md):** every word, searchable in both directions.
- **[Why it is this way](rationale.md)** and **[what changed when](design-log.md)**.
